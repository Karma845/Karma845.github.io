(LN) Reborn as an Extra in a Magical Girl Anime: I’ll Save Those Girls, But Only Because I Want to See Them Cry

Status:

Ongoing

Synopsis:

I was obsessed with “clouding” —

In my past life, that is. For some reason, I’ve now been reincarnated as a mob character named Hayami Sota in a magical girl anime.

This world — where magical girls get trapped in enemy schemes and pushed to the brink of total annihilation — is, to me, an absolute paradise. One day, while secretly watching a battle unfold, some kind of demon god suddenly spoke to me.

“Wait — if I make a contract and become the most powerful being around, I can keep stopping the girls from getting completely wiped out… and keep enjoying their suffering indefinitely!?”

And so I took on the identity of a mysterious entity known as “Omega,” silently watching over the magical girls and rescuing them time and time again. But things are starting to get a little… strange.

“Omega… will you make me one of your followers?”

Hold on — is she going yandere on me?! No no no, I need her to be suffering and miserable, not falling for me!

Genre:

Action, Comedy, Fantasy, Harem, Mystery, Romance, School Life, Supernatural.
