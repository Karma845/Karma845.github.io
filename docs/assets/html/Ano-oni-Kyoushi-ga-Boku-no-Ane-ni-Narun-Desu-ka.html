(LN) Wait, Is That Tyrant Teacher Going to Be My Sister?

Status:

Ongoing

Synopsis:

“Wait, Dad, you’re remarrying?”

And on top of that, his new partner has a daughter who is completely eager to dote on her new younger brother?

What the heck, that sounds amazing!

…Or so I thought, floating on cloud nine when I first heard the news. But the moment we actually met face to face, I fell into total despair.

Because the true identity of my new older sister was Shina-sensei—the physical education teacher famous for being ridiculously strict!

To make matters worse, Dad is moving out to start a new life with just my stepmother.

Co-habitating with a demon teacher is way too much to handle…

At least, that’s what I thought. But at home, Shina-sensei is completely different from how she is at school.

She totally lets her guard down, gets teary-eyed from being scared of bugs—and above all, she’s super sweet to me!

And so begins a cohabitation romantic comedy with a demon teacher who loves her little brother way too much!

Genre:

Comedy, Ecchi, Romance, School Life, Slice of Life
