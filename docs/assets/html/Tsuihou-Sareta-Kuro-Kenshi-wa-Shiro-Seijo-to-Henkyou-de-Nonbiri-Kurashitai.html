(WN) The Banished Black Swordsman Wants a Peaceful Life with the White Saint in the Frontier ~Huh? You Want Him to Come Back with the Saint? It’s Already Too Late~

Status:

Ongoing

Synopsis:

The strongest swordsman, who had despaired over a life in which nothing was ever rewarded, was now endlessly sought after by the legendary Saint-sama.

Black swordsman Lloyd was suddenly and unilaterally expelled from the Hero’s party. Lost and at a standstill, the one he encountered on a night road was a girl who had been branded a “False Saint” by the church.

“I’m not lying…!”

To protect the girl who pleaded through her tears, Lloyd took up his sword once again. In doing so, he was nearly swallowed by the uncontrollable “cursed power” within him, but the “prayer” of the White Saint, Lusha, saved him.

“I’ve always been taught to use my power for the world. But it’s different now. I want to use my power for you… That’s what I feel.”

The White Saint, who was declared a heretic in defiance of the church’s intentions. The strongest black swordsman, who had never been able to connect with anyone.

When the two of them met, what they began was a laid-back slow life in the frontier! Even while being tossed around by the slightly airheaded Saint-sama, Lloyd gradually built a bond with her and came to know the warmth of connecting with others.

A tale of slow life in another world where comeuppance, healing, and romance intersect—now begins!!

Genre:

Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Romance, Shounen, Slice of Life.
