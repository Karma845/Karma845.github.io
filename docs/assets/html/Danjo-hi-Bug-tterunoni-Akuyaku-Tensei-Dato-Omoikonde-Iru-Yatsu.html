Status:

Synopsis:

One day, a man woke up thinking, “I’ve reincarnated as a villain with a ton of doom flags!”

However, reality turned out to be very different—it was all one big misunderstanding.

Why? Because he found himself in a world where the gender ratio was so skewed it was practically broken, and societal roles between men and women had completely flipped.

Now, what would happen if this man acted like a “villain” (even though he was actually a good guy) in a world like this?
