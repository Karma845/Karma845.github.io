Chapter 7: Fran of 'Creation'

"Yeah, skipping class after lunch really is the best~"

I skipped 5th period right after lunch break and aimlessly wandered around the school.

Maybe I'll find a nice warm spot to take a nap... wait... hm? Is that...

"...Ah, I lost..."

A girl in a hoodie sitting on a bench playing a game on her smartphone muttered, not sounding particularly frustrated.

Dark brown hair and deep purple eyes peeking out from the gap of her hoodie... There's no mistaking it.

"You know class has already started, right?"

"Hm...?"

When I called out to her, the girl slowly raised her gaze from her smartphone screen.

I knew it, it's Fran.

This girl, Mio Nanase, is one of the magical girls of this world, known as Fran.

To be frank, Fran is the biggest problem child among the magical girls, while also serving as the pampered youngest-sister figure of the group.

Playing games in a place like this is exactly the kind of self-indulgent thing Fran would do.

"You... are skipping too..."

"Well, yeah. Classes are a pain."

"Same here... We really... hit it off, don't we..."

And with that, Fran resumed her game.

"What are you playing?"

"...This."

The smartphone screen Fran showed me displayed an FPS game that had been really popular lately.

"Oh, I play that game too."

"Really?"

"Really. See?"

When I showed her my own smartphone screen, Fran's eyes sparkled.

This kind of thing about Fran is just so cute~.

"You're good... You're the same rank as me...!"

"I've put a decent amount of time into it, yeah."

"...Let's... play together...!"

"Sure."

And so, through some completely natural turn of events, I ended up playing games with Fran.

As expected of Fran... I knew from the anime that she was a hardcore gamer, but I didn't realize she was *this* good.

It would be no exaggeration to say her skills were already at a professional level.

Then, a few minutes later.

"We won...!"

Fran raised both hands in the air, rejoicing happily.

I was playing seriously too, but Fran was still a bit better than me, so I mostly ended up playing a support role for her.

As expected of the magical girl Fran...!

"Good work, nice plays."

"Yeah... Um... I haven't... asked your name... What's... your name...?"

Fran asked with an adorable upward glance.

Ugh, so cu...te!!

This is way too unfair... The destructive power of a combo featuring max-charm Fran and an upward glance...!

Having a magical girl remember my name is basically equivalent to my reason for existing as a mob returning to nothingness, but it can't be helped.

"I'm Sōta Hayami."

"Sōta... Sota... Sota...!"

Fran said happily.

"Sota?"

Hm? Did my name just get abbreviated?

"Yep. Sota... It's... easier to say and more efficient..."

I see, that really is typical of the lazy Fran.

This is exactly why Fran is called the biggest problem child among the magical girls.

Fran finds things bothersome and sometimes skips meetings and the like. But it's exactly because of this personality that the other magical girls end up doting on her and pampering her.

"You can... call me... Mio..."

"Roger that. Mio."

"Good..."

When I called her by her name, Fran smiled in satisfaction.

Yep, Fran really is cute! End of discussion!

"Well then... now that... we've decided on nicknames... let's... resume... Sota."

Saying that, Fran took off her shoes and lay down on the bench.

"Why are you lying down?"

"I can... focus on playing... better this way..."

Ah, so it's the optimal posture... It does look pretty comfortable to lie down, sure.

Be that as it may...

"But why are you resting your head on my lap?"

"It's a substitute pillow. Usually... Yūna... does it for me, but..."

It's true that Noah and Fran were really close. I can easily picture Fran getting a lap pillow from Noah.

Noah and Fran... that combination... Fuffufu, truly magnificent... Which is exactly why a mob like me doesn't want to serve as a substitute for such a beautiful pairing... but Fran is cute. And so on and so forth.

"Well then... let's do this, Sota..."

Fran muttered in a good mood.

◇

"Sota, that was fun..."

"Glad to hear it."

In the end, we kept playing games until school was over.

Since it was already evening, the sky was gradually growing darker.

"Well, I'm heading home. What are you going to do, Mio?"

"Mhm... I'll... go home too..."

Fran sat up lazily and for some reason stared at me.

"Alright, Sota. Let's go home."

"Hm? Yeah."

"Piggyback."

As if it were the most natural thing in the world, Fran spread both of her arms, entering a standby pose for a piggyback ride.

"I... no longer have... any stamina left... to walk home..."

"Is that so."

"...Sota... can you really... just leave a frail girl like me... behind...?"

"I guess I have no choice... I just have to escort you home?"

"Yeah. I'll leave... the rest to you..."

"Alright, alright~"

Fran clung tightly to my back, but perhaps because she was so petite, she was incredibly light.

A piggyback ride like this should be a breeze.

And so, I walked the route home from school carrying Fran on my back.

I never thought I'd end up having to carry a magical girl home on my back... This is an utterly inexcusable act for a mob...!

Maybe it would have been better to have Luna come over? ...No, that would probably just make things more complicated.

"Mhm... sleepy..."

"Mio~, if you fall asleep I won't know where your house is, so stay awake~"

"Mhm... I know..."

Perhaps feeling sleepy, Fran leaned against my back while letting out a big yawn... *yaaaawn*. She was nodding off and looked like she could fall asleep at any second.

Seeing Fran look so sleepy is making me sleepy too... I'll take a nap once I drop her off.

Then, occasionally waking up the sleeping Fran to ask for directions, I somehow managed to reach the apartment building where she lived.

Finally here... it's been a long journey...

"Huh...? It's my house... we're here..."

Waking up, Fran looked around and confirmed that she had arrived at her house.

"We're here."

"Good work~"

"Alright, let's get you down then."

"Ehh..."

"I can't exactly carry you inside, you know."

"Muu... I guess it can't be helped..."

Reluctantly, Fran got off my back.

"Thanks... for walking me home."

Fran thanked me while rubbing her sleepy eyes.

"Yep, you're welcome."

Just receiving a 'thanks' from Fran is more than enough of a reward...! Fran really is cute!

"Ah... also..."

As if remembering something, Fran held out a small, adorable fist toward me.

"What's this?"

"A fist... bump... A greeting between friends... I wanted to try it..."

"I see. In that case."

I held out my fist just like Fran.

Our fists gently bumped together.

"Fufufu... This is nice... I think I might... like it..."

Fran seemed to like this fist bump and smiled happily.

"Glad to hear it."

"...Sota... let's play... together again... okay?"

"Of course."

"Yay...! It's a promise, then..."

Fran rejoiced like a child.

"Nice to meet you... bestie... ♪"
