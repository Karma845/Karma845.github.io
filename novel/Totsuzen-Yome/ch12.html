Chapter 12: Fleeting Everyday Life and Creeping Darkness

"Hey, did you hear? They said a demon appeared in the city again."

The next day, when I arrived at school, Seito spoke to me.

"Hm? Ah, you mean the one from yesterday."

I reflected on yesterday's events once again.

Yesterday's demon attack was quite good. Especially the scene where Fran was taken hostage and the rest of the magical girls could do nothing but feel their own powerlessness—it was a true masterpiece.

And I couldn't help but look forward to the *kumorase* (despair) scene waiting to happen after this.

"It's seriously bad, right? Isn't this world going to end soon? Apparently, there's also some guy named Omega running around."

"Ah, it seems so. They were talking about it on the news."

Currently, Omega's reputation among the citizens is terrible. He's being called an evil god, a grim reaper, or a devil who destroyed the town and created a massive crater in the city, and is quite hated.

But honestly, from the bottom of my heart, I couldn't care less about that. Even if I'm showered with abuse from the whole world or held legally responsible, I will not change.

I will simply continue to admire the magical girls' *kumorase* with all my might.

◇

"Sigh, I'm exhausted..."

Fourth period finally ended, and we entered the lunch break.

I had fallen asleep at some point during class and remembered almost nothing of the content, but miraculously, the magical girl next to me, Lady Fiona, let me copy her notes, so I made it to lunch break without any issues today as well.

Now then... guess I'll eat on the roof again today.

Thinking that, I made my way to the roof.

Though Luna probably won't be coming today either...

Luna hasn't been coming to the roof lately.

Well, she's probably busy with her student council and magical girl duties anyway.

As I was walking and thinking about this, someone lightly tapped me on the shoulder from behind.

"Hayami-kun."

Being called out to, I turned around and a slender white finger poked my cheek.

"Fufu, it was a success."

With graceful movements, she giggled like a child whose prank had succeeded.

"...Um... This is..."

"Fufu, I'm sorry. I tried calling out to you a few times, but you didn't seem to notice, so I decided to play a little prank ♪"

Standing before me was the magical girl of 'Affection', Noah—Yūna Kurusu.

...This is the first time we've met at school.

She serves as the head of the library committee at school. Thanks to her blessed looks and a personality that is kind to everyone, the number of boys going to the library has apparently skyrocketed.

However, in reality, she supposedly has a rather sadistic side as well. Boys who confess to her are met with merciless words, completely shattering their hopes and crushing them to pieces.

For her to specifically call out to a particular boy is an incredibly rare occurrence.

"It has been a while, Hayami-kun."

"It has been a while, Kurusu-san. Do you need something from me?"

"No, I simply saw you and decided to call out to you. Are you heading to lunch now, Hayami-kun?"

"Yeah, well... I was planning to eat all by myself today."

"My, is that so... In that case..."

Hm? Wait, this flow...

"I am also heading to lunch right now. If you don't mind, would you like to join me?"

......................I screwed up...

Regardless, I need to decline this. Getting any more involved with magical girls is a crisis for my mob life!

"No, I'm—"

"Is it absolutely no good?"

Being pleaded to with those teary eyes, my resolve shook violently.

"...Understood."

"Fufu, I thought you would say that, Hayami-kun. Shall we go, then?"

Well, Luna probably isn't coming today anyway... I guess it's fine...

And so, we came to the cafeteria together.

The cafeteria was bustling with people eating with their friends, couples eating together, and so on.

We secured a table for two and received our respective meals.

"The prices here are quite reasonable, aren't they."

"Yes, they are. At the school's request, wanting the students to grow up eating good food, they are priced considerably lower than normal."

By the way, I ordered omurice, and Noah ordered cream stew.

"You're eating quite a small amount today, aren't you."

I fully expected her to eat an amount similar to the donuts from the other day, so when I asked, Noah looked a bit flustered and made a 'shh' gesture to keep quiet.

"A-At school, I want to keep the setting that I'm a light eater..."

"I see, so that's how it is."

I guess eating that amount at school would be embarrassing for her. She has a surprisingly maiden-like side to her too.

"U-Um, Hayami-kun, the fact that I'm a big eater is..."

"Of course, I'll keep it a secret."

"That would be a great help."

Even someone like me wouldn't do something a person hates.

As for the *kumorase*... well, the demons are the ones doing that. I'm just watching it.

"Shall we eat, then?"

We put our hands together, said "Itadakimasu," and I scooped some omurice with my spoon and brought it to my mouth.

As expected of the Ryūsei Academy cafeteria, which hires top-class chefs. The eggs are runny, and the chicken rice has an exquisite seasoning. It's incredibly delicious.

『Mhm, delicious! Sōta! I've taken a liking to this!』

It's such a delicious omurice that I can even understand why Voldia likes it.

After eating enthusiastically for a while, I noticed Noah staring intently in this direction.

No, not at me.

She was staring at the omurice.

"..."

"Umm... Kurusu-san?"

"Ah!? I'm sorry! I was just thinking about how deliciously you were eating it..."

Saying 'please don't mind me' with flushed cheeks, she embarrassedly looked away.

Don't tell me... did she want to eat some?

"If you'd like, do you want a bite?"

When I offered that, Noah's face lit up with a bright smile.

Looks like I hit the mark.

"I-Is that really okay?"

"Of course."

"Th-Then... I'll take you up on that offer..."

Saying that, Noah opened her mouth and waited for something.

"U-Umm, Kurusu-san? This is..."

"When sharing food, isn't this how you feed each other?"

Being told that with such pure, innocent eyes, I couldn't bring myself to say it was wrong.

I scooped some omurice with the spoon and timidly offered it to Noah.

"Mhm..."

Tucking her hair behind her ear and wearing a bewitching expression, Noah took the offered spoon into her mouth.

"Mhm... It's delicious."

"Th-That's good... to hear..."

I'm feeling some incredible stares from our surroundings... Nuooooooo! What is this atmosphere!

"Then, from me as well..."

This time, Noah offered a spoon to me.

"Here, it's delicious, you know?"

"...Th-Then... Thanks for the food."

Taking the offered spoon into my mouth, the sweet taste of cream stew spread inside.

However, I was so confused that I couldn't even taste it.

Why am I, a supposed mob... doing an 'ahh' feed with a magical girl...

"How is it?"

"It was... very... delicious..."

"I'm glad. Then, another bite..."

"No, I'm good now!"

I declined with all my might.

◇

After school, the magical girls gathered at their headquarters as usual, sitting around the round table.

"Those two still aren't here yet, huh."

The leader, Yui, muttered as if tired of waiting for the two who had yet to arrive.

Ayano, in particular, hadn't been to school for the past few days.

"Reina mentioned she had Student Council work today as well. But... I'm worried about Ayano..."

"A cold... maybe..."

"That's the thing, we don't know... I tried visiting her house, but no one answered..."

Yui had tried contacting her and visiting her house multiple times, but there was no response from Ayano, and the days of not knowing how she was doing continued.

Since Ayano had sustained the worst injuries in the previous battle, the entire team was worried.

"After the meeting... let's all... drop in on her..."

"Ehh... Wouldn't it be a bother if we all went together?"

"That sounds good. Let's go!"

"Even you, Yūna!?"

At that moment, the chime announcing the arrival of the elevator echoed in the room.

Simultaneously, the elevator doors opened, and Ayano appeared from inside.

"Ayano...! We finally get to see you!"

"Oh... so you were alive..."

"Hey, Mio. You shouldn't say things like that. But I'm truly glad you're safe..."

Everyone rushed over and offered their own words... but they quickly realized that something about Ayano was slightly different from usual.

Her eyes held an empty void.

"Sorry, everyone. For not seeing you for a while."

After a moment, she began to speak without any energy.

The three of them felt a sense of doubt about her state, but decided not to mind it for the moment.

"There's something I need you all to hear..."

Then, in the next instant, Ayano said something unbelievable.

"Reina—the magical girl Luna... is a traitor."

At Ayano's shocking statement, the expressions of the other three froze in astonishment.

"Eh...?"

Words of confusion leaked from Yui's mouth.

"...Ayano, please retract that statement immediately. We absolutely cannot let what you just said slide as a joke."

However, the first to react to this was the magical girl of 'Affection', Noah—Yūna Kurusu.

Unlike her usual gentle self, her voice was terrifyingly cold.

"You're scary, Yūna. And... I don't plan on retracting my statement."

In response to Yūna's warning, Ayano returned a provocative smile.

"Ayano... what's wrong with you...?"

"...Did you... hit your head... or something...?"

"Not really. In fact, I'm in a better mood than usual."

Ayano replied brusquely to Yui and Mio's worried voices and sat down in an empty seat.

"Back then, Reina was the only one who defended Omega... Reina and Omega were working together from the very beginning. And the two of them are trying to lead the world to ruin."

Ayano stated her claims.

But they were far too absurd.

"That's just a forced conclusion! Reina would absolutely never do something like that!"

"Yui is right. I... I believe in the kind Reina I've always seen."

"Ayano's claims... are impossible... If you speak ill of Reina... any more than this... even I... will get angry..."

In the face of the three's counterarguments, Ayano let out an exasperated sigh... *haah*.

"You three really don't understand, do you? We are magical girls, right? Magical girls are the ones who protect the order of the world. We have to eliminate anything that even has the slightest possibility of bringing about the worst possible future."

"But even so... you can't hurt your friends for that...! 'Quarrels between magical girls shouldn't be carried over to the next day'... Ayano, you, the one who made that rule, should understand that better than anyone!"

Ayano... the magical girl Christina, had always cherished the bonds between magical girls.

Precisely because they had seen her be so strong, caring for her comrades more deeply than anyone else among the magical girls, the three of them noticed something ominous lurking deep within the eyes of this unusual Ayano.

"That's why, Ayano... Let us hear your true feelings—"

"...That's enough."

In the next instant, a horrifying miasma of magic power spilled out from Ayano.

It was clearly not Ayano's own magic power.

"Ayano... we'll save you right now...!"

The three of them readied themselves.

"It's useless. After all... you're all going to end up just like me..."

The moment Ayano muttered that meaningfully, whatever was deep within Ayano's eyes propagated to the eyes of the other three.

"Wh... at... is, this..."

"It feels like... a fog is covering... my mind..."

"...My thoughts are... being rewritten... it seems..."

The thoughts of the three were gradually being overwritten.

Overwritten by the thought that the magical girl Luna was a traitor.

"Looks like it's working properly. What a relief."

Ayano looked down at the suffering three with a satisfied expression.

"No...! Reina is...! Reina, is..."

"...Cowardly... I don't... want to think... such things... but..."

"You two...! Keep... your... consciousness... str..."

The consciousness of the three was being swallowed by something.

"Good night, you three."

With those final words, the consciousness of the three was completely swallowed.

"—Everything as Lord Selec commands."

Ayano muttered mechanically with hollow eyes.
