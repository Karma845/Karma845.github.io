Volume 1 Chapter 11: Hostage

Chapter 11: Hostage

Hearing the demon appearance alarm, we rushed out of the store and sprinted through the shopping mall, following the demon's presence.

Sōta... we ended up leaving him behind, but... I wonder if he's okay... I hope he managed to escape properly...

On the way, my mind kept drifting back to Sōta, whom we had left at the coffee shop.

What if Sōta couldn't escape in time? What if a demon appeared inside the shopping mall? Those possibilities crossed my mind over and over.

...No, knowing Sōta, I'm sure he's fine, right?

Yes, I'm sure he's fine. After all, if there's one thing he seems good at, it's running away.

"Jeez, and we were actually enjoying our day off, too!"

"It can't be helped. Demons don't care about our personal circumstances."

Before heading outside the shopping mall, we stopped by a clothing store, slipped into the fitting rooms, and each chanted our transformation spells.

"【Magical Girl of Silver: Magical Blizzard】"

"【Magical Girl of Roaring Thunder: Magical Thunder】"

"【Magical Girl of Halo: Magical Shine】"

"【Magical Girl of Affection: Magical Relief】"

"【Magical Girl of Creation: Magical Create】"

In that instant, freezing air converged onto me, taking the shape of a white dress.

I transformed into Luna of the 'Silver'.

"Everyone, let's go!"

We burst out of the fitting rooms and headed for the exit.

Emerging outside, we immediately took to the sky and hurried toward the location of the presence.

"They're taking too long. When is the signal to start Kuza's operation going to come..."

After flying for a bit, we spotted a demon in the middle of the city, looking like he was deep in thought about something.

"That's far enough, demon!"

"Hm? You are...!"

"The magical girls are here!"

"Tch, so the magical girls have arrived. What is that idiot Kuza playing around at..."

Muttering that, he lazily readied the greatsword he was holding.

I could feel an immense amount of miasma radiating from that sword.

"Everyone, be careful. This demon is incredibly strong! And that sword..."

"Very perceptive. I am a high-level demon, Quadra of the 'Dark Magic Sword' and 'Regeneration'. And this sword is my demonic sword, Farias."

Saying that, Quadra proudly pointed the tip of his sword at us.

Two titles? What is that demonic sword?

We've encountered demons with titles before, but I've never heard of one having two, nor have I ever heard of a sword being called a 'demonic sword'.

Could this be... one of the 'Seven Demon Emperors'?

I've heard about them from Kiryūin-san. Even among demons, the most powerful ones are called the 'Seven Demon Emperors'.

Even so, we can't back down!

No matter how strong the opponent may be, a magical girl bravely challenges them anyway!

"Now then, shall we begin. Magical girls... try not to break too easily."

Quadra swung his sword with tremendous force.

The trajectory of the blade, which should have only cut the air, manifested as a slashing attack and flew straight toward us.

"Everyone, Magical Shield!"

We deployed Magical Shields in front of us.

The moment the slash collided with our Magical Shields, a massive shockwave assaulted the surrounding area.

"Guh! What incredible power!"

His attacks are even stronger than the demon who manipulated explosions!

This is bad, our Magical Shields won't hold at this rate...

Numerous cracks had already appeared on the Magical Shields, and as time passed, the number of cracks was rapidly increasing. It was only a matter of time before they collapsed entirely.

Even though it's a Magical Shield maintained by all five of us...!

"Everyone, we can't fully block this! I'll count to 5, then drop the Magical Shields and scatter all at once!"

At our leader Fiona's words, everyone nodded.

"5... 4... 3... 2... 1... Now!"

On Fiona's cue, we dropped the Magical Shields and dodged the slash.

"Hoh, what a nimble bunch. Then how about this!"

Quadra readied his sword again, this time slashing the air repeatedly, sending countless slashing attacks flying randomly at us.

Every single one carried the same power as the previous slash. A direct hit would likely mean instant death.

Even if it isn't, a severe injury would be unavoidable...

Dodging the incoming slashes, I gradually closed the distance between myself and Quadra.

"I've reached you!"

"Splendid. I shall entertain you directly."

Readying my exclusive weapon, the ice sword Freeze, I slashed at Quadra.

The moment our swords clashed, a shockwave was generated, violently shaking the atmosphere around us.

"Hahaha! How fun!"

"This isn't fun at all..."

"Of course it isn't! The one having fun is always the one doing the tormenting!"

"Kuh!"

Overpowered by Quadra's overwhelming strength, I was blown backward.

(...But you know, letting your guard down like that is the reason for your defeat!)

Blood sprayed from the mouth of Quadra, who had been watching me get blown away with a satisfied look.

It seems... it was a success.

"Gah! Kuh... to think you managed to slip inside my guard without me noticing..."

"Carelessness... is your greatest... enemy...!"

"Seriously, guys like you are so easy to deal with."

While I had been exchanging blows with him, Fran and Christina had slipped behind the enemy and pierced Quadra's body with their swords.

"So this means..."

"Yes, it is our victory."

The two who were on standby in the rear were also convinced of our victory upon seeing that.

"Good work, you were strong."

"...Fufufu... Hahahaha! What are you all spouting? Did you forget? My titles... I am Quadra of 'Regeneration'. Wounds that would critically injure a normal demon are nothing more than scratches to me!"

In that instant, the wounds the two of them had inflicted closed up immediately.

"N-No way!"

"You're too naive!"

Quadra slammed his fist into Christina, sending her crashing violently into a building.

"Christina...!"

"You're next, you know?"

"...!"

Fran used Creation to generate various weapons and threw them at Quadra.

However, none of them seemed to have any effect. He closed the distance, drove a fist into her stomach, and she lost consciousness just like that.

"Fran!"

"Whoops, don't move. If you move, this one's life is forfeit."

Holding the unconscious Fran, Quadra pressed the blade of his sword against her throat and floated a sadistic, mocking smile at us.

Don't tell me... he's taking Fran hostage...!

"Everyone disarm and gather in one place."

"Like we would ever do that!"

"I really do not mind, you know? I could easily lop off this one's head right now."

"Kuh!"

Making eye contact to confirm our intentions, we threw down our weapons and gathered in one spot.

"Good, good, good girls."

"We did exactly as you instructed! Release Fran!"

"Ah, of course. After I kill you all, I will send this one to the exact same place."

Quadra swung his sword with one hand.

Another slashing attack came flying toward us.

I see... so this was just to kill us all at once...!

"Everyone, defend—"

"Whether you defend or dodge, this woman's life is forfeit, you hear me?"

"...Ghk!"

It's no good... If this slash hits us directly, nothing awaits us but certain death.

What do we do...! Defend? Dodge? But if we do, Fran will—

Even as I was thinking this, the slash continued to close the distance between us.

"......"

"......"

Tears of frustration overflowed from the two girls' eyes at our inability to do anything.

Help us...

Just as tears began to fall from my own eyes, a voice echoed from somewhere.

『What awful taste.』

In that instant, the slash that had been heading toward us disintegrated.

"Gwaaaaaaah!"

Immediately after, an agonizing death cry from Quadra that made me want to cover my ears echoed through the area.

"Y-You're...!"

"You are..."

"My name is Omega. Demon, your final destination has already been decided as death."

He spoke with a voice that seemed to resonate from the abyss.

Omega...!

There stood Omega, carefully holding Fran in his arms.

◇

"Omega, I knew you would come."

"......"

Ignoring the words of the demon in front of me, I healed Fran, whom I had retrieved from the demon.

Her expression, which initially looked pained, gradually softened, and her eyes fluttered open slightly.

"Leave the rest to me and rest."

When I told her that, perhaps due to exhaustion or maybe a sense of relief, she fell asleep in my arms, letting out soft, sleeping breaths.

"...Luna."

"E-Eh, yes."

Handing Fran over to Luna, I turned back to face the demon.

Now then, let's do this.

"What's this, Omega? Playing babysitter for the magical girls? You're a lot more underwhelming than I heard."

"Hmph, don't make me laugh, you scum."

"Kufufu, it seems your repulsive mouth is the real deal. But that works perfectly. I was just getting tired of tormenting them completely one-sidedly anyway."

"Are you trying to pick a fight with me?"

"Naturally! I will defeat you and become one of the 'Seven Demon Emperors'!"

"...How worthless."

"...I'll make you regret those words!"

In that instant, the demon kicked off the air, closing the distance all at once, and took a stance to deliver a diagonal slash with his massive demonic sword.

I casually observed his movements, then placed my hand on the flat side of the demonic sword that was just about to be swung down, applying a small amount of force.

"What!"

The demon lost his balance and hurriedly tried to put distance between us.

Swords are weak to lateral force, that's just common sense.

I drove my fist right into the retreating demon's face.

"Gah!"

Having hit him with all my might, half of the demon's face was gouged out, leaving a grotesque appearance like something straight out of a horror movie.

Even so, he didn't die instantly; his head had already begun regenerating.

"Guuh... It is useless! Whether you blow my head off or gouge out my heart, I will not die!"

"Is that so? Then let us continue."

I kicked off the air, closing the distance to him in an instant, and unleashed a barrage of attacks.

Holes were opened all over the demon's body, but the wounds slowly began to close up anyway.

I see, so his regenerative abilities are high. No wonder the magical girls were struggling.

"Gaah! Kuh, I told you it is useless—"

"I am not finished yet."

"!?"

Moving ahead of the guy as he was blown away, I delivered a devastating kick to his face, slamming him into the side of a building.

"You make for quite a satisfying punching bag."

"Ghk! Who the hell are you calling—!"

Having regenerated his body, the demon readied his sword and charged at me again.

"I shall show you... a true sword."

I altered the shape of my own miasma.

Eventually, the miasma transformed into the shape of a single, pitch-black sword.

"Ready your weapon."

"I wondered what you were going to show me... but there is no way something like that can surpass my demonic sword...!"

The demon readied his sword.

"You will become the rust on my demonic sword...!"

"A sword like that will never reach me."

Our swords clashed.

Immediately after, the demon's demonic sword snapped cleanly in two.

"M-My... my demonic sword is!!"

The demon wailed in despair, looking at his pathetically broken demonic sword.

"Just as I thought. A completely dull blade..."

At the exact same time, the demon was sliced into tiny pieces.

◇

"Wait... please... Omega..."

Having been smashed into a building by Quadra, I reached out my hand toward Omega, who was about to fly off somewhere.

Watching what just happened, I was convinced.

That guy is dangerous.

I cannot possibly imagine someone who unleashes such overwhelming death being an ally.

Move... I have to... protect everyone...!

I wished for it strongly, but contrary to my desires, my body wouldn't twitch, and pain shot through my entire frame.

Why... am I so weak...

Just as tears of frustration welled up, I felt a presence land right in front of me.

"Good grief, I really wish he would give me a break. Killing a high-level demon so easily like that."

When I looked up, a man was floating there.

The man wearing glasses and a soft smile exuded an atmosphere that unconsciously made people want to trust him.

However, an ominous horn was growing from his forehead.

A demon... No way... don't tell me it's reinforcements...!

Moreover, this guy is incomparably stronger than the demon from before...!

"A pleasure to meet you. I am one of the 'Seven Demon Emperors', and I am called Selec."

The demon who appeared introduced himself as Selec of the 'Seven Demon Emperors' and bowed politely.

The 'Seven Demon Emperors'!? This guy is...!

"Please do not glare at me so fiercely. It is not as if I came here with the intention of killing you, after all."

The man spoke in a gentle, soothing tone, pushed up his glasses, and continued.

"We are also having quite a bit of trouble dealing with the existence of that man... Omega... What do you say? Would you like to cooperate with me?"

"Like I would ever... cooperate with the likes of you...!!"

Teaming up with Omega would be many times safer than teaming up with these guys.

"Fufufu, I suppose so. I expected you would say that. Well, it doesn't really matter to me either way."

Saying that, Selec approached me and placed his hand over my eyes.

"I think I would like you to dance for me a little."

"What... are... you..."

"I wonder what kind of expressions... what kind of emotions they will show when they are murdered by you, someone they trusted, now under my control?"

"No... way... you can't... be...!"

I felt a terrible premonition and desperately tried to move my body.

But my body wouldn't move.

At the same time, a fog clouded my thoughts, and they began to be overwritten.

"I think I'll have all of you meet the exact same end as the previous generation of magical girls...!"

Through the gaps in his fingers, I could see Selec's face contort sadistically.

"S... top—"

"Fufufu, then I shall look forward to it. Christina of the 'Roaring Thunder'."

"N-Nooooooooooooooooooooooooooo!!"

My scream vanished uselessly into the sky.
