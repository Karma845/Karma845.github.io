Chapter 14: Despair and His Expectations

"Oh, you came, Reina."

"Ayano..."

When I went to the roof of the Artemis Group building, which was the designated meeting place, Ayano was waiting there with a spiritless expression.

Ayano hadn't been coming to school lately. It had been quite a while since we last met.

"It's been a while, Reina. Have you been well?"

"Yes, I've been well... I suppose I can't really say that. What about you? You were resting for a while, but are your injuries okay now?"

"You don't need to worry about that. My injuries have already healed to some extent."

"I see... So, what is the reason you called me here?"

I was afraid of talking to Ayano any longer and urged her to get to the point.

Ayano was acting strange today. Normally, I could feel kindness in every word she spoke, but today, I felt as if her words contained a hint of malice.

"Fufu, I suppose so. That makes sense... Reina, you've been ignored by everyone lately, haven't you?"

"Ghk!"

As if seeing right through my heart, she accurately pointed out my worries.

"It's pitiful, isn't it? Being ignored by everyone... Is it possible that I'm the first comrade you've talked to in a while?"

"...And what does that matter to you?"

"Fufufu, what if I told you that I was the one who told everyone to cut off contact with you?"

Ayano floated a smile laced with malice.

"Eh...?"

I was at a loss for words.

No way... why...

"You look like you're wondering 'why', Reina. But the cause lies with you, you know?"

I desperately tried to find a reason, but nothing came to mind.

"You've been working with Omega from the very beginning, and you're planning to betray us in the future, aren't you?"

"Something like that... is absolutely impossible...!"

"There's a perfectly good reason why you're being suspected, you know? After all, you were always the one defending Omega, right?"

"He saved us! Defending our benefactor is only natural as someone who was saved by him!"

"Well, I figured you would say that. But to me, whether you're betraying us or not doesn't matter."

"...What does that mean?"

Ayano smiled fearlessly and slowly walked toward me.

"Reina. You know, I've always hated you."

"Eh..."

Those words pierced deeply into my heart.

"Your constant condescending remarks, those eyes that look down on people. I hate everything about you."

S... top...

"I imagine everyone else feels the same way. That's why they so easily obeyed my words and ignored you together."

"No... way..."

Everyone... hates me...

At that moment, I felt my heart, my spirit, audibly crumbling away.

For what purpose... am I... a magical girl...

I was rapidly losing sight of my reason for existing as a magical girl.

"I'll say it again, Reina... could you disappear from our sight?"

With those words, my spirit completely collapsed.

For what purpose... am I...

Told to 'disappear' by a precious comrade, I lost sight of my very reason for existing.

Before I knew it, I had unconsciously stepped back, approaching the edge of the roof.

When I looked back, the sparkling nightscape spread out before me. It was the scenery that generations of magical girls had protected.

In the next instant, a strong impact struck my back.

"Goodbye, Reina. You are not suited to be a magical girl."

Finally, I saw Ayano smile sadistically, and I fell toward the shining nightscape.

I see... Ayano hated me... that much...

Realizing how much I was hated by my comrade broke my heart even further.

In my current mental state, I cannot transform into a magical girl.

Certain death was approaching.

"Eh..."

But immediately after that, light returned to Ayano's eyes.

Then, looking at me falling, the sadistic smile Ayano had been wearing rapidly clouded over.

"No way... no... it's a lie...! Why did I...! Reina!"

For some reason, she reached her hand out toward me.

Why... are you reaching out to me with a face like that...? Just like a child about to lose a precious friend...

Just as I was about to surrender myself to everything and close my eyes, his words crossed my mind.

『Do not die.』

Omega's voice... Is this what they call a life flashing before your eyes... To think I'd remember him at the very end...

Just as tears overflowed from my eyes, a voice echoed from somewhere.

"This is surely not the end for you."

That voice wasn't an illusion; this time, I heard it clearly as a real voice.

Immediately after, my falling body was caught by something, and I floated softly in the air.

"Ah..."

"How light. You are as light as an angel's feather."

Although hidden by his mask, I could tell he was smiling gently.

"Ome... ga...!"

I impulsively clung to Omega.

Omega gently patted my back to calm me down.

"—To think you would save the falling girl... Magnificently done."

The demon, who had appeared at some point, praised him admiringly.

"...You are... Selec of the 'Seven Demon Emperors'..."

"To think you know of me... I am honored. I never expected to be recognized by Omega."

This man is... one of the 'Seven Demon Emperors'...!

Ominous horns and dragon-like wings growing from his back. I could feel power from that man that couldn't even be compared to the demons we had fought so far.

"Setting that aside... Magical Girl Christina, that was a wonderful performance. You have a talent for acting."

Selec sent dry applause *clap clap clap* as if mocking Ayano.

"Ah... Aaa..."

"Well, although you didn't fulfill the mission to crush Luna of the 'Silver', you worked quite well."

Don't tell me Ayano was being controlled by this guy...

Right after, Selec held his hand up to the sky, and the sky began to dye a dark reddish-black.

"Now then, shall we begin... A very, very fun show..."

◇

Night, the city center.

Standing at the top of a tower hundreds of meters tall, I stared intently at the roof of a certain building.

There, two girls, Luna and Christina, were talking, and Luna's face was dyed in despair.

"......"

Without voicing any particular thoughts, I simply stared at the scene.

When you watch a moderately good movie, you might enjoy it while chatting happily with friends, but when you watch an overwhelming masterpiece, you stay silent and appreciate it with all your might, right? It was exactly like that.

And the scene finally reached its climax.

Christina pushed Luna off the building.

In that instant, Christina regained her sanity.

"No way... no... it's a lie...! Why did I...! Reina!"

Her face was dyed in despair and regret, and even though she knew she couldn't reach, she desperately reached out her hand anyway.

Ah... this is good...! This is so good!!

The silence of the night, a pleasant night breeze... and the *kumorase* of two magical girls... Tonight, I'm feeling absolutely fantastic.

Normally, I would want to go home right now and slowly bask in the afterglow of this feeling... but well, that will have to wait until after I finish a job.

It'd be a problem if they died... I need to enjoy their *kumorase* much, much more... right?

『...Is your time for appreciation over?』

"Yeah, thanks for staying quiet."

『I have already grown accustomed to you... More importantly, I sense a demon's magic power from that girl... Furthermore, that reading belongs to one of the 'Seven Demon Emperors'.』

"Yeah, it's the great Professor Selec of the 'Seven Demon Emperors'. That guy is a great demon who contributed massively to the development of magical girl *kumorase*...!"

That's right, speaking of Selec, he's a *kumorase* master who produced many despairs in this magical girl anime.

He was hated by the magical girls... but I don't dislike him.

『I see, this sounds like it will be enjoyable.』

"It'll probably be a breeze, partner."

『Indeed. Let us make him realize that he doesn't even reach our feet.』

I created a mask out of miasma and put it on.

"...Shall we go."

From the tower, I headed toward the falling Luna at a speed exceeding the speed of sound.

◇

"Kufufu, now then, shall we begin."

Immediately after Selec twitched his fingers, Omega, who saw the movement, twisted his body with minimal movement as if dodging something.

Right after, steel wires shining black in the night darkness sliced through the space we had been occupying just a moment prior.

"Hoh, quite sharp..."

"Steel wires, huh... Quite interesting..."

"Kufufu, I dabble in them a little as a hobby... The wires are laced with a deadly poison, so if you touch them, even for you, protecting her will become quite difficult, I imagine."

"If they hit me, that is."

"Hoh... This is just play, but shall I get a little serious?"

This time, he moved all the fingers on both hands intricately, and slashing attacks from the steel wires assaulted Omega in multiple layers.

However, not a single one hit Omega; even though he was holding me, Omega dodged everything with minimal, nimble movements.

(A-Amazing...!)

While being held by Omega, my heart had been pounding loudly the entire time.

Even though attacks that meant instant death upon contact were assaulting us right now, being held by Omega, I was convinced that this was the safest and most secure place in the world right now.

But because Omega is protecting me—

"Omega, if I'm in the way, put me down!"

"Hmph, I told you earlier, did I not? You are as light as an angel's feather. This level of weight is no hindrance at all."

At those words, my heart pounded even louder, almost to the point of being noisy.

...Jeez... At this rate, he's going to find out...

I felt embarrassed, wondering if Omega could hear my loud heartbeat, and my cheeks grew hot.

"Kufufu, casually having a conversation while dodging my attacks... You certainly have quite a bit of leeway!"

Immediately after, steel wires overlapping like a net surrounded us.

"Now, what will you do, Omega? There is no escape route, you know?"

"Hmph, how worthless. If there isn't one, I just have to make one."

As Omega swung his hand down toward the approaching steel wires, the wires were easily severed.

"Kufufu, as expected of Omega... You exceed my expectations. This looks like it will be quite enjoyable."

"Do you still wish to continue?"

"Naturally. I have been looking forward to meeting you, after all. However, I must also do my job properly..."

The moment Selec muttered that, empty space manifested behind him. From there, two demons appeared.

While each of them did not match Selec, they possessed tremendous power.

"Everyone here is a high-level demon with a title. I think I will leave the annihilation of the city center to them. What will you do, Omega? Will you face me, or the two high-level demons?"

"......"

Without answering, Omega slowly descended to the roof of a building and gently put me down.

Although I had a lingering reluctance, wanting to stay wrapped in Omega's arms just a little longer, I told myself not to be a burden to Omega.

Omega saved me... In that case, I must also...!

"Omega, leave those two demons to me."

"Is that so... Then I leave them to you."

"Yes... leave it to me...!"

I replied to his back.

I will absolutely not betray his expectations!
