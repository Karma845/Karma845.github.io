Chapter 10: The Magical Girls' Day Off

Going back in time a little, to the top floor of a high-rise building—the magical girls' headquarters.

There, we were having a discussion about our future plans.

"I still think Omega might be an ally, don't you?"

I appealed to everyone, arguing that Omega was on our side.

The recent incident convinced me that he is our ally. He defeated two demons with immense power and healed our severe injuries.

"I'm certainly grateful that he saved us... but it's also a fact that Omega caused a lot of damage, right?"

"True, that massive crater was damage caused by Omega. But if Omega hadn't defeated those demons right there, the damage would have been much worse, wouldn't it?"

That demon who manipulated explosions was especially dangerous. If that demon had truly gone on a rampage, the damage wouldn't have stopped at that level.

"Yeah, I think Omega is an ally too."

"Even you, Yui... But that doesn't change the fact that Omega is a dangerous existence, does it?"

"I also want to believe Omega is an ally. But under the current circumstances, it's hard to say for sure."

As I thought, convincing Ayano and Yūna is going to be difficult... But I—

"If we can build a cooperative relationship with Omega, we might be able to reduce the damage caused by demons even further."

"Like I said, that's why—"

Just then, the elevator stopped at the top floor, which was supposed to be accessible only to magical girls.

When the doors opened, a woman in a suit appeared from inside.

"You all seem to be having a lively discussion."

"K-Kiryūin-san!?"

With long black hair reaching her waist, and a stunning figure that perfectly suited her black suit, surpassing even that of a model, was the beautiful Setsuna Kiryūin.

She is the current CEO of the Artemis Group—a global mega-corporation she built in a single generation—and the last remaining member of the legendary first-generation magical girls.

"Good work, everyone. Having a meeting on a day like this... you're as diligent as always."

"Kiryūin-san, is it really okay for you to be here?"

"Hm? Well, my schedule opened up a bit, so I came to check on things."

She said that, but she probably just ran away from her secretary. A person as important as Kiryūin-san never truly has free time.

"So, Kiryūin-san, what brings you here? The information about Omega, who appeared the other day, should have been included in the report..."

Because she is essentially the master of us magical girls, her decisions are absolute.

If this person declares that Omega is an enemy, we will have no choice but to oppose him.

"Ah, I read through it. Well, I suppose we'll wait and see for now. I'd like to see Omega in person sometime as well."

I breathed a sigh of relief at those words.

For now, it seems things will be okay...

"More importantly, are you all going to school properly?"

"Yes, of course!"

"Everyone is attending without any issues."

"I see, that's good then. You are all still young. Cherish your youth... Yes, because nobody knows when that everyday normalcy might come crashing down..."

Kiryūin-san looked off into the distance, as if recalling something.

Her face wore a somewhat lonely, sad expression.

"...Oops, my apologies, I was reminiscing about the past a bit. Anyway, on your next day off, all of you should go out and have some fun somewhere. Here, take this."

Saying that, she pulled a wad of cash from her inner pocket and slammed it down on the table in front of us.

There was probably about a million yen there.

"Eh!? W-Wait!"

"Hm? Was it not enough? Then—"

"""""That's plenty!"""""

As Kiryūin-san tried to reach into her pocket again, we all frantically stopped her.

This person's sense of money is seriously messed up.

"We don't need this either!"

"Hmph! I see... You really don't need to hold back, you know? If I could, I'd give you all billions in compensation for your work."

"No, this is our duty as magical girls."

"...Right. I apologize... But still, you girls definitely need a break. Next time you have a day off, all of you must go out together somewhere. Knowing you, you'd just keep working unless I explicitly said something, so consider this an order."

Kiryūin-san pulled a single black card from her breast pocket and placed it on the desk.

"You can use this freely, so go have fun."

Saying only that, she fluttered her hand in a wave and boarded the elevator.

◇

And so, because of that, we were all meeting up at the shopping mall, but...

"Everyone, this is Hayami-kun, who I met at the donut shop earlier."

"...Hey there..."

"It's Hayami-kun! What a coincidence!"

"Ah, Sota~!"

"Uwaa, Hayami..."

Why is this guy here... and more importantly, why is he getting along so well with Yūna... Aren't I your only friend?

"Has everyone met Hayami-kun before?"

"Hayami-kun sits next to me in class."

"Sota is my bestie."

"...I know him, sort of."

Eh? The others know Sōta too?

"Y-Yo, Reina..."

"I don't know any man like this."

"Hold on just a second! Society won't let you get away with a lie like that!"

"Reina, were you acquainted with Hayami-kun?"

"...Yes... well..."

"Oh my, is that so. What kind of relationship do you have with Hayami-kun?"

For some reason, Yūna is pressing the issue unusually hard...

"...Like an owner and her pet dog, I suppose."

"Hey, hey, hey, who's a dog?"

"Who do you think feeds you every day?"

"...It is you, Lady Reina."

"That's more like it."

Yes, exactly. Sōta is basically my dog.

"U-Um, I should probably be heading out now—"

"Hayami-kun, since you're already here, why don't you shop with us?"

""Eh?""

"It would be helpful to hear a boy's opinion when picking out clothes."

"N-No, I'm—"

"That sounds great! Come with us, Hayami-kun!"

"I don't mind either way."

"Me neither..."

Eh? It's only me left?

"...I suppose it's fine."

"Then it's decided. Let us go."

Since everyone agreed, Sōta ended up tagging along, and the six of us moved through the shopping mall.

"......"

"R-Reina...? Are you... mad about something...?"

"I'm not mad at all."

"You're totally mad!?"

Even though he's my special friend...

Feeling a sense of murky frustration, I walked at a brisk pace.

◇

Sigh, how did things turn out like this...

Currently, I was inside a clothing store with the magical girls. Right before my eyes, the full lineup of magical girls was happily picking out clothes.

"Hey, how about this one?"

"Yeah... looks good."

"Yūna, try this on! I think it'll look great on you!"

"W-Wait a minute!? This outfit is way too revealing!"

Aren't I supposed to be a mob... Why am I shopping with the magical girls in a place like this... Just what the hell am I doing...

While I was steeping in a sense of despair, I felt someone's presence standing next to me.

"Mind if I sit?"

"Hm? Yeah, I don't mind."

"Thanks."

Sounding a bit cold for words of gratitude, Christina sat down next to me.

While pretending to zone out, I cast a quick glance beside me.

Christina was as beautiful as ever, but her face looked slightly displeased.

"......"

"......"

Then, a heavy silence fell.

Whoa... this is kind of scary...

After the silence continued for a while, Christina spoke up.

"...Hayami. You haven't been messing around with my precious friends, have you?"

She threatened me with a low, menacing voice.

...Man... so scary...

It might seem like a personality entirely unsuited for a magical girl, but these words were precisely because she truly cares about her comrades.

Moreover, considering she's usually this scary, her 'kumorase' scene will be... man, absolutely the best.

"I haven't. We just happened to get acquainted."

"...You're not dating any of them?"

"I swear to God."

"...I see, that's fine then."

Saying that, Christina looked away, seeming to be in a slightly better mood.

As expected of the magical girls' guardian...

Just as I thought that, I felt another presence approaching me.

"Hey, got a second?"

"What is it? Reina."

Reina, wearing much more girlish casual clothes than usual, stood there holding a pile of clothes in both hands.

"I need you to keep me company for a bit."

"For what?"

"Just come with me. Ayano, I'm borrowing Sōta."

"...Roger that."

As I was led away by the hand by Luna, Christina glared at me incredibly intensely for some reason.

Eh? I didn't do anything wrong here, right?

◇

"So? What is it?"

I was brought in front of the fitting rooms.

Well, I already had a pretty good idea at this point.

"Isn't it obvious just by looking? You're going to evaluate my outfits."

"I don't know anything about clothes, you know?"

"You don't need to know anything. If I look cute, you say I'm cute. That's your job. Do you understand?"

"...Understood."

"Fufu, good boy. That's all you have to do."

Saying that, Reina disappeared into the back of the fitting room.

『It seems things have taken quite an amusing turn.』

(It's not amusing at all! Why am I in this situation...)

『Kufufu, isn't it fine? This is one of those 'rom-com events', is it not?』

(No, no, I have no desire for a rom-com, I just want to see the magical girls despair!)

『What a strange fellow you are, as always. How amusing.』

After waiting for a while chatting about such things, the fitting room curtain opened.

"How is it?"

"It's cute. As expected of you, Reina."

The pure white dress perfectly complemented Luna's silver hair, and paired with her model-like figure, it gave off an almost goddess-like divine aura.

It truly felt like an outfit made exclusively for Luna.

"You really are beautiful, Reina."

I heard somewhere a long time ago that in situations like this, it's best to just relentlessly praise the girl. As I kept pouring on the compliments, Luna's cheeks gradually flushed red.

Hm? What's this...?

"I-I see... I'm glad to hear that... Then, next one..."

After that, Luna tried on outfit after outfit and showed them to me. To think I'd get to see so many different costume variations for Luna—something I couldn't even see in the magical girl video games... I never thought the day would come when I'd get to witness this... I'm so glad I was born in this world...!

As I was crying tears of joy, Luna looked at me with cold eyes.

"Hold on, why are you crying?"

"Well... I'm just so moved?"

"H-Hmm... Was it really that good...?"

"So? Which one are you going to pick?"

"...I'll buy all of them."

"Hey, wait a minute! Then you didn't even need me here, did you!?"

When I retorted, Luna muttered with her cheeks flushed, looking a bit embarrassed.

"I'm buying all of them because you were here..."

"What does that mean?"

"...Who knows, what could it mean?"

Saying that bewitchingly, Luna walked toward the cash register.

◇

After finishing our shopping, the magical girl entourage and I were playing at the arcade.

"Yay! Scored again!"

"Muuu... you're too good..."

There were those enjoying games the normal way in two-player cabinets.

"These are all way too easy, huh."

"Indeed, are the settings here too lenient?"

Then there were those carrying armfuls of prizes, completely sweeping the machines. The magical girls all had very different ways of playing. Among them, one magical girl was locked in a staring contest with a single machine, playing it over and over.

"No way... my calculations should have been perfect..."

I've never seen anyone try to use math on a crane game before.

"What are you doing?"

"Eh? Ah, Sōta... No matter how many times I try, I can't catch this."

At the end of Luna's gaze was a rather cute bear plushie.

I see, so she's been aiming for this the whole time...

"That's a pretty cute plushie."

"S-Shut up... I can't focus, so go away."

"...Can I give it a try?"

"Eh? I don't mind, but..."

I put some money into the machine and started the game.

『Shall I assist you, Sōta?』

(No, I'm good. I'm actually a master at this type of machine.)

『Hoh, then allow me to observe.』

In the past, I played crane games over and over again to get exclusive magical girl merchandise that could only be obtained that way...

Perhaps thanks to that, my crane game skills had reached a professional level.

I carefully moved the claw right above the plushie's tag and lowered the arm.

The claw perfectly hooked into the loop of the tag, and the lifted prize was carried smoothly over to the drop chute and released.

"A-Amazing... to think you got it on the first try..."

"Naturally. Here."

"Eh?"

When I took the bear plushie from the prize chute and handed it directly to Luna, she looked incredibly surprised for some reason.

"What's wrong? Did you actually not want it?"

"N-No, that's not it. It's just that I didn't expect you to do something like that."

"Really?"

"Yes."

Did she really think I was that useless?

"But—"

Luna hugged the bear plushie tightly against her chest and smiled affectionately.

"Thank you. I'll treasure it."

"Yeah, please do."

◇

"Man, we played so much!"

"I couldn't win..."

"I managed to have a little bit of fun too."

"We ended up winning far too many prizes."

Having left the arcade, we were now at a stylish coffee shop. Apparently, a new seasonal drink had launched today, so everyone was drinking it.

『Hmm, this is also quite delicious.』

(I mean, it costs a pretty penny, after all.)

It's normally too expensive so I don't drink this stuff much, but Luna pulled out that black card and paid for my drink too, saying it was thanks for keeping them company today.

Even so, that black card is amazing...

"That plushie of yours is so cute, Reina! Did you win it?"

"E-Yes, well, you know."

Luna was hugging the plushie importantly with a smug face, as if showing it off.

"It really is cute. Let me touch it for a bit."

"No."

As Christina reached out her hand toward the plushie, Luna hugged it even tighter, as if protecting it.

"Come on, just a little bit."

"I said no."

"Stingy Reina."

"Yes, I'm perfectly fine being stingy."

Seeing Luna holding the plushie so preciously like her own child, Christina seemingly gave up. She returned to her seat and sipped her melon-flavored new drink through a straw with a dissatisfied expression.

Still, did she really like that plushie *that* much?

"So, where should we go next!"

"Let me see... Next we should—"

At that moment, an announcement echoed throughout the shopping mall.

【A demon has appeared in the city! Demon spotted! It is heading in this direction! Everyone inside the shopping mall, please evacuate immediately!】

From the tense tone of the voice, everyone realized a demon had genuinely appeared, and the people around us began to run, scrambling to get outside as quickly as possible.

(...Voldia, is this the real deal?)

『Indeed. I definitely sense the presence of a demon.』

Alrightttttttt!!

A demon appearance is heeeerrrrre!!

Good grief, Demon-kun is always, always so late. I was getting tired of waiting!

Suppressing my excitement, I first observed the movements of the magical girls.

Hearing the broadcast, the magical girls exchanged glances that basically said (Let's go!), and moved out all at once.

"Sōta, it's dangerous here, so hurry up and run away!"

Telling me that, Luna dashed off somewhere.

"H-Hey, everyone~, where are you going~."

Uttering those deliberately helpless words, I watched the magical girls until they were out of sight, and then I left the store as well.

The surroundings were in a complete state of panic, flooded with people trying to reach the exits.

Yep, yep, this is exactly how a panic should look! This is getting fun!

Avoiding the crowds, I found a staff room connected to an exit and slipped inside, only to lock eyes with an ogre-like demon radiating an ominous aura.

"......"

"......"

An awkward silence filled the space, as if I had just witnessed a scene I wasn't supposed to see.

"Ah, my bad."

"Hold it right there, human."

Trying to pretend it never happened and leave, I was stopped by the demon.

"Can I help you?"

"To think I would be witnessed lying in wait to slaughter all the humans inside the shopping mall... Well, whatever. Dead men tell no tales. The plan remains the same."

Saying that, the demon raised a massive sword.

"It is a good day to die by my sword."

Just as the blade aimed at my heart was about to touch my skin, a metallic *clang!* rang out, and the demon's sword was repelled.

"What! My sword was—"

"Man, and I was even thinking of letting you off the hook... Well, whatever."

I chanted.

"【Majin-ka Kaihō: Dark Side】 (Demon God Liberation: Dark Side)"

A black, ominous miasma coiled around me, forming a black robe and an eerie mask.

"Wh-What! You are!"

"Dead men tell no tales... was it?"

"Kukkuku, I see. To think Omega's true identity was just some student... However, you are naive. I am Kuza of the 'Strong Sword'. If you think you can defeat me easily—"

"I have no time to waste on a small fry like you. 【Despair Eye】"

In that instant, without even the time to utter a sound, the demon's body vanished without a trace.

"Just wait for me, magical girls."

Muttering to the now-empty space, I walked out into the darkness.
