Chapter 9: Noah of 'Affection'

On my day off, I uncharacteristically paid a visit to a movie theater in the city.

『Hmm, that was quite interesting, Sōta!』

(Glad to hear it.)

『It was my first time experiencing a 'movie theater'... but it truly is a magnificent thing. The sense of realism as if you could feel the air and the heat, combined with the incredibly impactful visuals and BGM—everything interlocks to form a single masterpiece.』

By the way, the movie we watched today was a work that was quite famous in my previous life as well. It's the one where a robot from the future comes to the modern era and engages in a fierce battle with another robot who also came from the future.

It was also my first time watching a movie that wasn't about magical girls, but I actually enjoyed it quite a bit.

(Yeah, it was really good. There's a compilation movie covering the magical girls' past battles coming out soon, let's go watch it when it does.)

『I get the feeling the inside of your head is going to be incredibly noisy when that happens.』

(Well, that can't be helped.)

Telling me to watch the magical girls' *kumorase* in total silence is asking the impossible.

The sight of them falling into despair in the face of an overwhelming enemy, crying out as their comrades get hurt... it's a beauty that simply cannot be described in words.

『So, where are we heading next?』

(Apparently, Shadow Donuts is having an all-you-can-eat donut buffet right now, so I'm thinking of going there.)

『Ooh... Donuts...!! That pastry where they cut a hole in the center to cheat you out of mass!』

(Well, you might not be entirely wrong... but the hole is what makes a donut good, you know? If you filled the hole, it would just be sweet bread.)

『I see, you have a point... So it is a donut precisely because you can eat right up to the hole... In any case, I am looking forward to it!』

(Your only source of enjoyment lately has been food...)

Are you really okay with that... Oh God of Ruin... Thinking this, I kept walking until we arrived at our destination.

Inside the store, many customers were already challenging the all-you-can-eat buffet. All of them wore ghastly, intense expressions, frantically devouring donuts with the sheer willpower to get their money's worth.

Am I sure this is a donut shop?

Bewildered by a sight you would absolutely never expect to see in a donut shop, I made my way to the reception counter.

"Welcome! Just one person, correct? Would you like the all-you-can-eat buffet or to order normally?"

"The all-you-can-eat buffet, please."

"Understood. Also, I apologize, but it is currently incredibly crowded, and we are having trouble freeing up tables. If you don't mind sharing a table with someone, we can seat you immediately. How would you like to proceed?"

Sharing a table, huh... My conversations with Voldia are telepathic anyway, so it shouldn't be a problem.

"Sharing a table is fine."

"Understood, I will guide you to your seat right away."

It's been a while since I ate out, so I'm going to eat to my heart's content! I'll just start by eating all the expensive ones first.

As I followed the staff member while plotting my all-you-can-eat strategy, a single girl entered my field of vision.

With flaxen hair and beautiful golden eyes, the girl radiated an overwhelming presence, as if she were the main character of this world.

Wh-Why is Noah heeeerrre!?

I screamed with all my might inside my head.

That's right, naturally, this girl was one of the magical girls—Noah's civilian identity... Yūna Kurusu.

She usually serves as the head of the library committee at Ryūsei Academy and is apparently quite popular among both boys and girls at school.

"Don't tell me..."

Just then, I noticed that the seat directly across from Noah was empty, and I got an incredibly bad feeling.

No, no, no way... I'm a mob, right? Just your completely ordinary Mob A, the kind you can find anywhere. There's absolutely no way a mere mob like me would be seated at the exact same table as a magical girl, a main character of this world. Yeah, there's no way—

"Please take this seat over here."

The staff member gestured to the seat right in front of Noah with a textbook customer-service smile.

Why... I'm supposed to be a mob... Mobs aren't supposed to interact with named characters...

While lamenting this in my heart, standing around forever would just make me stand out more, so I decided to sit down quietly.

Th-This is bad...! This development is completely unexpected! I should just go home without eating anything!

『But I want to eat donuts...』

(No, my life as a mob is way more important than that! Anyway, let's get out of here right now!)

『Donuts...』

I feel bad for the depressed Voldia, but I absolutely cannot yield on this.

As I tried to leave my seat, I made perfect eye contact with Noah sitting across from me.

"Are you... So—"

"Eh?"

The moment I immediately averted my gaze and stood up, someone called out to my back, causing me to reflexively turn around.

Wh-Why did she talk to me!?

"Umm... Is something the matter...?"

"...I'm sorry. For calling out to you so suddenly... My name is Yūna Kurusu. I've seen you around Ryūsei Academy before, so... You're Sōta Hayami-kun, right?"

"Y-Yes... I'm Sōta Hayami..."

Eh? Why does Noah know my name?

There's no way a magical girl would know the name of an ordinary mob like me... Wait, could it be that because Noah is a super popular beauty, she basically remembers the names of all the students at the academy?

But even so, to remember the full name of a mob like me... As expected of Noah.

"I knew it! To think we'd meet in a place like this... It seems there's some sort of fate connecting us."

Saying that, Noah smiled gently.

"Hahaha..."

As if there could ever be fate between a mob and a magical girl!

Retorting in my heart, and realizing I couldn't run away anymore, I reluctantly sat down and ordered donuts. The expensive ones, of course.

"Even so... you're really eating a lot..."

On Noah's plate, there was a massive quantity of various donuts, so much that it was hard to believe one person was going to eat it all.

"T-Today is just a special occasion... I don't normally eat this much, you know!"

Saying that somewhat embarrassedly, Noah continued to happily stuff her cheeks with donuts.

Where exactly is this mountain of donuts going inside Noah's body...

"Mmm~! Delicious!"

Noah smiled, looking incredibly blissful.

Well, whatever. I'll eat my donuts too. There's a time limit, after all.

Thinking this, I stuffed my own cheeks with donuts.

『Mhm! Delicious!』

(Seriously, this is so good... As expected of a popular chain...)

Thanks to ordering the expensive ones, getting to eat donuts I usually can't afford is the absolute best.

◇

Having eaten my fill of donuts, I left the shop and walked down the street feeling completely satisfied.

Man... this turned out to be a pretty good way to spend a day off... though it still loses to watching a magical girl's *kumorase*.

"That was delicious, wasn't it! Hayami-kun!"

Walking next to me, Noah spoke with a smile.

"Yeah... it really was..."

No, maybe this isn't a good day off after all... I mean, why are we walking together...

As expected of a main character of this world, the magical girl Noah stands out significantly, and she's been drawing the gazes of everyone around us for a while now.

As a mob, I'd really prefer to avoid drawing attention...

"Are you heading anywhere after this, Hayami-kun?"

"Umm, I was thinking of going to the shopping mall."

"What a coincidence again. I'm actually meeting up with some friends at the shopping mall too."

"Oh... is that so..."

"We can go to the shopping mall together, then!"

"R-Right!"

Hm? Wait a second.

Friends...? Noah's friends? That could only mean those four...

After walking for a while, we arrived at our destination, the shopping mall.

There, a scene exactly as I had predicted was unfolding.
