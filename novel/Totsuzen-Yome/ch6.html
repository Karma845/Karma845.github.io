Chapter 6: The Seven Demon Emperors

The Demon Realm.

A completely dark room where absolutely no light entered. Thick miasma filled the space.

In an environment where an ordinary human would instantly die upon entering, four demons instead looked quite comfortable.

A demon wearing glasses with a soft smile on his face, Selec.

A demon possessing ominous horns on a heavily trained physique, wearing a composed smile, Gran.

A slender demon with lethargic eyes that made him look sleep-deprived, Weitz.

A demon with a distinctly curvy figure, black wings protruding from her back, and wearing a highly revealing black dress, Luminas.

"That concludes my report."

Having finished his entire report, Selec snapped his notebook shut.

"Hey, Selec... is that true?"

A voice of doubt was raised over the contents of a report that seemed far too detached from reality.

"Yes, it is true. Do you intend to doubt my clairvoyance?"

"But that is simply too unrealistic..."

"Is it? I do not think so. Defeating merely one or two high-level demons hardly serves as any sort of benchmark."

"Sigh... but the only ones capable of doing that should be individuals with strength on the level of us 'Seven Demon Emperors'..."

The absolute highest echelon of demon combat power, the 'Seven Demon Emperors'.

A collective term for the seven individuals who stand above even the strongest high-level demons, earning the title of 'Emperor'.

It is said that their power is such that a single one of them could eradicate an entire country without leaving a trace.

"However, we 'Seven Demon Emperors' are emperors in name only at the moment. As it stands, only five out of the seven remain."

"Our numbers haven't changed at all since we were taken out by the first-generation magical girls~"

"Gahahaha! It cannot be helped! Demons of our caliber do not just appear every day!"

"Ahem... Everyone. Let us return to the topic at hand... regarding the mysterious man who suddenly appeared the other day and began saving the magical girls... Omega."

Selec pushed up his glasses and shifted the topic.

"Is that man a demon? I heard he was clad in miasma."

"That is also unclear. He wears a black robe and covers his face with a mask."

"If he were a demon, I cannot imagine what benefit he would gain from opposing us. Could he perhaps be a human?"

"Isn't that impossible~? I mean, that Omega guy is a man, right?"

"Maybe if it were a magical girl, but there is no way a human man possesses such tremendous power."

"Indeed, I agree. For now, let us put the matter of his true identity on hold. What is important is Omega's objective."

Is Omega simply saving the magical girls out of goodwill, or does he have some sort of underlying goal and is using the magical girls for that purpose?

If it is the latter, they might be able to establish a cooperative relationship on the condition that they do not kill the magical girls. However, if Omega's goal is the annihilation of demons, or if his goals conflict with the demons' objectives, they would have no choice but to take the path of hostility.

"As expected, wouldn't his objective be our annihilation?"

"I think so too~"

"I believe he is simply using the magical girls."

"Should we not eradicate the seed of anxiety regardless? If all of us 'Seven Demon Emperors' mobilize at once, it would be a simple task."

"That is shallow thinking. As long as we cannot see the bottom of Omega's strength, we must investigate him first."

"What was that? Then what do you intend to do if he bares his fangs at us—"

As the discussion was growing heated, the heavy doors of the room opened with a profound sound.

*Clack*—*Clack*—The sound of footsteps entered the room.

『How noisy.』

A voice that seemed to echo from the very depths of hell spoke.

That individual sat down on the throne situated at the farthest end of the room.

The moment the Demon Emperors saw the person who had entered, they all immediately ceased their discussion, stood from their chairs, and knelt before the throne.

"We sincerely apologize for offending your mood."

『It is fine... More importantly, what you were discussing earlier—was it about the 'Omega' mentioned in the reports?』

"Yes, that is correct."

『The moment he attacked us, he became an enemy. Whatever his objectives may be is irrelevant.』

""""Yes, my Lord!""""

The Demon Emperors, who had lacked coordination just moments ago, responded in perfect unison.

『Selec, begin preparations for *that*. I have high expectations for you.』

"Understood... Demon God, Lord Venezuphia."

Selec bowed his head deeply.

"Sōta, it's been a while."

"Hm? Reina, huh. It has been a while."

As I came up to the roof during lunch break as usual, basking in the afterglow of the other day, Luna appeared for the first time in a while.

She hasn't been coming to school lately, so she was probably busy dealing with the aftermath of that incident.

"I'm relieved to see you're safe."

"Well, yeah. Despite how I look, I'm pretty confident in my ability to run away."

"Heh, same as always, I see. Here, a bento. I had leftovers again today."

"Oh, thanks."

I accepted the bento from Reina and opened the lid.

Today's was also a brightly colored, Japanese-style bento that looked incredibly delicious.

"My bad."

"It's fine, I'm basically just treating you like a garbage disposal for leftovers anyway."

"Seriously, thank you. Time to eat."

Yep, it really is delicious.

It's been a while since I had a proper meal.

『Indeed. This girl's cooking is truly exquisite...』

(...You can taste this?)

『I can share your sense of taste and smell. I turn them off when you eat disgusting food, though.』

(...)

I really did fuse completely with this guy, didn't I...

"...Hey, Sōta."

"What?"

"Have you ever had someone you lik—"

"No."

"...I asked the wrong person. I apologize."

It seriously troubles me when people ask me things like that.

I haven't had romantic feelings for anyone since my previous life. Well, if I *had* to say, magical girls are my first love.

"...You know, recently, I think I might have found someone I like."

"Hey, picking me for romance advice is definitely a mistake, you know."

"Just shut up and listen. When I was in trouble and completely despairing, he immediately rushed over and saved me."

"Huh..."

There are some awfully kind people out there... If it were me, I'd stare intently from a distance and enjoy the show for a bit first.

"But I feel like he's so far away... he's in a place I could never possibly reach. I'm no match for him."

"...You don't need to put yourself down like that, right? You're beautiful, kind, and cool, Reina. I like those things about you."

"Eh!?"

When I praised her honestly, she completely froze with her mouth hanging open.

Hm? Is she not used to being complimented?

"Wh-wh-l-like!?"

"I meant exactly what I said. I like those parts of you, Reina."

"H-Hmm, is that so... But I'm sorry. The person I like is many times more charming than someone like you."

For some reason, I just got rejected completely unprompted...

"I see, that's a shame."

"Fufu, you got rejected."

"It's not like I confessed to you or anything."

"But I do like you about second best after him... Yeah, you lose to him, though."

"Yeah, yeah."

To be liked this much by Luna... I wonder what kind of guy he is...

I continued eating the bento while thinking about such things.

◇

After finishing my Student Council duties and returning home, I relaxed on my bed after getting out of the bath.

"I'm exhausted..."

My duties as a magical girl and my duties as Student Council President. As expected, balancing both is quite difficult. The wounds from battle don't heal easily, and the responsibilities of a magical girl are heavy.

I traced my finger over the spot where the explosive demon had left a burn mark the other day.

Doing so, I could feel a tiny trace of his magic power.

That magic power is so dense and wicked that it makes the demons we've fought up until now look like nothing in comparison.

But it was a gentle power that healed me.

"Omega..."

Where is he now... What is he doing... For some reason, I can't help but wonder about those things.

I wonder what his face looks like... Is he handsome... Or perhaps just ordinary?

Right before I lost consciousness the other day, I thought I saw him remove his mask... but I couldn't see his true face...

Next time, I want to look at his face properly.

With those thoughts in mind, I closed my eyes.
