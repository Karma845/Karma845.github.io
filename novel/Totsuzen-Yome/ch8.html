Chapter 8: Fiona of the 'Halo'

"We are going to decide on the executive committee members for the upcoming school festival. If anyone is willing to do it, please raise your hand..."

First thing in the morning after arriving at school, our female homeroom teacher addressed the class.

However, naturally, not a single hand went up.

The reason, of course, being that it's a pain in the ass.

Man... this is exactly when being a mob character is the best! There's no way an ordinary, zero-presence mob like me is ever going to get nominated!

"As I thought... it's come to this... hmm, what should we do..."

The female homeroom teacher held her head in distress.

However, I had a very good idea of a certain person who would confidently volunteer themselves in a situation like this.

"In that case... would it be alright if I did it?"

Right then, a single girl sitting next to me raised her hand.

"Shiina-san...!"

As if she had been waiting for this exact moment, the female homeroom teacher directed a look of deep expectation toward her.

Yep, yep, I knew it...! As expected of the magical girl Fiona!

The magical girl of the 'Halo', Fiona—Yui Shiina—is the 'class rep' archetype. She never neglects her studies even during breaks between classes and is always striving to improve herself.

As the magical girl Fiona, she is a leader who never gives up, fighting until the bitter end against any enemy to support her comrades.

However, the scene where she falls into absolute despair and gives up—precisely because her earlier encouragement to fight to the end ultimately caused her team to be annihilated—is ranked quite highly in my personal 'kumorase' ranking.

That scene... I wonder if I can witness it live in this world...

"Well then... can I leave it to you?"

"Yes! Leave it to me!"

Fiona declared with brimming confidence, and the entire class erupted into applause.

"As expected of Shiina-chan!"

"I just knew Shiina-chan would step up for us!"

Fiona bashfully accepted the praise from our classmates.

So blinding... As I thought, magical girls just live in a completely different world compared to a mob like me...!

But, naturally, my classmates don't understand it yet. They don't know that Fiona's true charm lies somewhere else entirely.

◇

"...So sleepy... what a pain..."

Even though it was still early in the morning, I headed to school while letting out a big yawn... *yaaaaawn*.

For some incredibly troublesome reason, it turned out that I was on day duty today. That's why I was heading to school this early.

I honestly wanted to just skip it... but as a general mob, I didn't want to stand out by skipping my day duty.

"Still, I'm so sleepy... I really should have slept a little..."

Yesterday, my imagination regarding 'kumorase' situations was running wild, and as I was thinking up various patterns of the magical girls falling into despair, it was morning before I knew it. But I have no regrets.

As expected, 'kumorase' is the absolute best.

『I had a terrible time yesterday as well... I have never experienced that volume of thought before. I thought my brain was going to burn out...』

(So even your information processing capability isn't all that great, huh.)

『My information processing capability is top-tier. It is simply that your sheer delusional power is strong enough to far surpass it.』

(Well, it's something I've been doing non-stop since I was a kid, after all!)

While chatting with Voldia about such things, I arrived at school before I knew it.

I headed straight to the staff room and retrieved the key to our classroom.

"Let's finish this quickly and get some sleep..."

Having made up my mind, I was walking down the first-floor connecting corridor when...

"Hello, flowers~! Is the water delicious~?"

A voice far too energetic for early morning echoed from the courtyard.

This voice... is it Fiona!?

Looking toward the courtyard, just as I expected, there was Fiona watering the flowers in the flowerbed.

"Eh? What's that? The first water of the morning is delicious? I know, right! Waking up early and drinking water is the best!"

Perhaps completely unaware of my presence, Fiona was muttering to herself as if she were having a conversation with the flowers while watering them.

Heh... I've been honing my stealth skills as a mob every single day, after all...!

"You flowers are full of energy again today... compared to you... all I can do is pretend to be energetic with all my might..."

Muttering that to herself, Fiona let out a depressed sigh.

"I even took on that school festival executive committee role yesterday... even though I know better than anyone that I'm not cut out to be a leader... Someone like me has no right to lead the other four... I shouldn't have that right... a coward like me..."

Wh-What... did she just say...!?

I was genuinely surprised.

No way... no way... To think I'd get to hear Fiona using the boyish first-person pronoun 'boku' live in person...!

Normally, in front of the other magical girls, Fiona hides her true self in order to play the role of the ideal, reliable leader.

When that Fiona reverts to her true self, her first-person pronoun also changes from 'watashi' back to her original 'boku'. Which means, getting to see this side of Fiona is incredibly rare.

I was just thinking that day duty was a massive pain in the ass... but getting to see such a rare Fiona makes it not so bad once in a while. 'The early bird gets the worm' is exactly right.

Leaning against a pillar and crossing my arms, I nodded approvingly.

Yep, yep, Fiona really is cute after all!

"...Sorry, flowers... You probably don't want to hear my boring stories... Ahaha... I really am useless... I just want to disappear..."

Fiona's face gradually clouded over.

That's it! More! Despair even more!

"...I should probably get going—hm?"

In that instant, perhaps sensing my gaze, Fiona turned toward me and our eyes met.

Ah, crap, I've been spotted...

"......"

Realizing that a side of her she didn't want anyone to see had just been witnessed, Fiona's face flushed bright red with embarrassment.

"...H-H-Hayami-kun!? Wh-Why are you here..."

Still, Fiona somehow managed to squeeze out some words.

"Ah, right...!! Hayami-kun, you're on day duty today too, aren't you!! Anyway, the weather is super nice this morning, isn't it~!!"

Desperately trying to cover it up, Fiona forced herself to act overly bright and cheerful.

Since she just showed me such a rare scene, it would be uncouth to trouble her any further.

"...Yeah. Well then, I'll be going now."

Saying only that, I headed toward the classroom.

Arriving at the classroom, I opened the door and took my first step inside a room no one had entered yet today.

Man... getting to see something so good first thing in the morning has put me in an incredibly refreshing mood...!

Basking in the morning sunlight streaming through the classroom windows, I thought about such things while doing the bare minimum required of my day duty.

Fiona... was really cute...!

While cleaning the blackboard and recalling Fiona from earlier, the classroom door rattled open.

"G-Good morning... Hayami-kun..."

Fiona greeted me, looking somewhat awkward.

Ah, right, Fiona was on day duty today too.

"Yo, good morning."

"I-I'll help too...!"

Hurriedly grabbing an eraser, she began wiping the chalk off the blackboard.

After that, we continued handling our day duty tasks. With the highly capable Fiona's help, the work was finished much faster than I expected.

"Good work, we finished early thanks to you."

"Good work to you too! You were a big help!"

We thanked each other for the hard work and sat down in our adjacent seats.

At first, when I found out I'd be sitting next to a magical girl, I thought my existence as a mob was over. However, just as the deepest shadows are cast right beside the brightest light, I didn't stand out at all, and I've actually been enjoying a surprisingly comfortable mob life.

I'm seriously way too sleepy... I'm going to sleep...

"Hey, Hayami-kun?"

Just as I laid my head on my desk to sleep, Fiona called out to me.

"Did you see...?"

"Nope, didn't see a thing."

"You definitely saw it, didn't you?"

She was smiling, but the pressure was terrifying.

"Well, yeah, I saw it."

"I-I knew it... To think someone saw me acting like that..."

Perhaps severely shocked, Fiona looked down, drained of all her energy.

Then, she looked up at me with teary eyes, as if clinging to me.

"Please! Keep this a secret...! If everyone finds out about this, I'll..."

"Okay. Of course I won't say anything."

As a massive fan of magical girls, there's no way I could refuse a request from one.

"Really?"

"Of course."

"...You're not planning to use my weakness to blackmail me or anything?"

"I'm not."

Finally, looking relieved, Fiona let out a sigh.

"You see... I'm actually a huge coward..."

"Is that really something you should be telling me?"

"Since you already found out my secret, Hayami-kun... I figured it doesn't matter anymore."

With a somewhat liberated look, Fiona continued.

"The real me... isn't the kind of person who can get along with everyone. That's why... I have to protect this secret. Absolutely."

"I see."

"It's not that I don't trust you, Hayami-kun, but... let's make a promise."

Saying that, Fiona held out her pinky finger.

I figured I might as well, so I held out my pinky too.

Then, we linked our pinkies together.

"It's a secret just between you and *boku*, okay?"

Fiona smiled meaningfully as she said that.
