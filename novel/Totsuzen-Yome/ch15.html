Chapter 15: Luna's Awakening

"I'll show you... my power..."

With a smile brimming with joy, I faced the two demons.

"Hoh... little girl, I don't think you are capable of taking us on."

"You magical girls can only stand against us by swarming together. What can a lone magical girl possibly do?"

The two demons seemed to be completely looking down on me, wearing complacent smiles.

However, my heart wasn't disturbed by their provocations in the slightest; there was only one emotion residing within me.

That was love. Love for Omega uniformly dominated my heart.

Fufu... so this is what it feels like to love someone... Omega, who made me feel this way, will definitely have to take proper responsibility for it...!

"...Hey, what are you sneering at?"

"Eh?"

Pointed out by the demon who looked creeped out, I realized I had been wearing an evil smile.

"What a creepy brat... I'll quickly erase you along with that unpleasant smile."

"Fufu, true... I want to go to his side quickly too... so I'll end this swiftly."

Then, I chanted.

"【Magical Girl of Absolute Zero: Magical Absolute Zero】"

In that instant, an overwhelming chill, like a blizzard, froze the surroundings.

"Wh-What... is this power...!?"

"This is bad...! This is..."

Then, the freezing air gently enveloped me, transforming my appearance into a jet-black dress.

Ah... this is his... affection... What a wonderful power...! It feels as if I have been reborn as a new person.

Carefully feeling his power within my body, it was as if Omega was always staying together with me, and my entire body throbbed with excitement.

This power that Omega gave me... fufu, I'm just itching to wield it to my heart's content... And there happen to be two perfect test subjects right here. Let's try it out immediately.

I readied my sword and pointed the tip at the enemies.

"I'll specifically let you celebrate my and his new beginning... As for the congratulatory gift... let's see, your cheap lives will do."

"Hah! You talk big, little girl!"

"Our lives belong to that person... We won't give them up so easily!"

The two demons, perhaps affected by my provocation, closed in on me without hiding a shred of their anger.

"I see... then—I'll just have to take them by force."

Immediately after muttering that, I released a freezing chill in all directions.

"Guh...! My body is... freezing...!"

"No, worse than that... this chill isn't normal...! It's freezing me from the inside... out..."

"The closer you try to get to me, the more intense the cold becomes. I wonder how long you can endure it?"

I observed the suffering of the demons closely, sneering at them.

"Dammit! We can't get close like this... Fall back for now!"

The two demons distanced themselves significantly from me as if running away.

"Done already?"

"Don't look down on us, little girl. You're nothing to us... Hey, let's do it!"

"Yeah...!"

The demons each readied their greatsword and axe and took large swings.

They were powerful attacks that would surely be fatal if they hit, but they were meaningless if they couldn't land.

I continued to dodge their fierce assault with minimal movement.

I wouldn't mind just dodging like this forever... but as expected, that would get tiring, so let's reduce their numbers by one first.

"Scurrying around...!"

"Keep dodging, why don't you!"

The demons' irritation reached its peak, and just as one of them fully swung his weapon, I stepped onto the flat side of his sword.

"Nice foothold."

The demon made a surprised expression, but it was too late. I kicked the astonished demon squarely in the face.

"Gbuohh!!"

The demon let out a stupid sound and was blown heavily away.

"Wha! Damn it... You bastard!!"

"You're in the way."

Hitting the other demon with a freezing spell, I chased after the demon I had kicked away.

Catching up to him instantly, I showered him with multiple god-speed slashes.

Slash, freeze. Slash, freeze.

I performed this indifferently, as if it were mere routine work.

"N-No... stop..."

"Right, I'm getting bored already... I'll put an end to this."

I gathered freezing air and unleashed it.

"【Despair Blizzard】"

In that instant, everything in the vicinity froze.

The buildings, the air, and—the demon.

The demon was frozen with its face contorted in fear, but as the block of ice made a cracking sound and began to splinter, the cracks propagated throughout the entire thing, shattering it to pieces along with the demon.

"That's... impossible..."

The other demon stared at the scattered remains of his comrade in sheer disbelief.

"Now then... that leaves just one."

"...Kuh...! You monster...!"

"Monster... How rude. Could you use a slightly cuter name for me?"

"...It seems I let my guard down a little... I will fight with my full strength from here on."

"I see, not that I care... but well, maybe I should try something a little different too."

Right now, I felt like I could do anything. With that confidence in my heart, I floated countless microscopic ice crystals in my surroundings.

Then—

"She disappeared...? Where did you go!"

The demon looked around, but he didn't seem to notice me at all.

Looks like it worked... This might be quite useful.

I am currently imperceptible to anyone. I'm reflecting the darkness of the night with the microscopic ice crystals floating around me, hiding my own presence.

I moved behind the demon and mercilessly slashed his back.

"Gah! Gwaaaah!!"

"I'm right here, you know?"

"Tch!! Don't mock me!!"

He swung his axe in desperation, but I was already gone, and he only hit empty air.

"Next! Where did you gooooo!!"

He frantically swung his axe, but naturally, it didn't hit me.

I slashed and vanished, slashed and vanished, repeatedly.

With each strike, the number of cuts covering the demon's body increased.

"Why... Why can't I see your movements...!"

"Naturally. I'm invisible, after all."

"Invisible... Hah, so that's it. No wonder I couldn't see your movements. But as long as I know the logic behind it, I can deal with it!"

The demon looked around, waiting for a chance to counterattack.

"There!"

In that instant, a powerful blow with his axe was unleashed.

However, it did not hit me.

"I-Impossible... There was definitely a flash of light just now..."

"A decoy, using the reflection of the moonlight. You're such an idiot, it really helps me out."

Succeeding in catching him off guard, I sliced off the hand holding the axe, neutralizing him.

"Guuh... My arm!! You bastard... Are you tormenting me without delivering the finishing blow...!!"

"Fufu, so what will you do? Do you want to keep going?"

"...Tch! Fine then, I will admit defeat for this battle alone... Hah!"

In the next instant, the demon vanished from the spot.

When I directed my gaze upward, the demon was flying through the sky at a considerable speed.

He's already that far... It's true that cornered bugs are fast runners... But if you think you can run away from me, you're dead wrong.

As I was considering how to deliver the finishing blow, I suddenly remembered a certain sight.

"—Fufufu, I just thought of something good...!"

Coming up with a brilliant idea, I floated a fearless smile, thrust out my index finger like a gun, and aimed at the demon flying in the sky.

"I wonder if I can do it too?"

That's right, it was the technique he used back then—

I murmured its name with love.

"【Despair Bullet】"

In that instant, a bullet glowing dark red and black was fired.

◇

"Damn it... that woman... is completely insane...!!"

The demon was flying through the sky, complaining as if unable to contain his irritation.

"But, I somehow managed to escape... Once I regroup with Lord Selec, a woman like that—"

In that instant, a dark red and black light illuminated the entire area.

And the demon certainly saw it.

"What... is that..."

It was a dark red and black bullet... No, it wasn't just that anymore. It was like a dark red and black sun.

It was approaching the demon in a straight line.

"What the hell... is this!!"

The demon fled.

Further, and further away.

But death followed closely behind him without fail.

"D-Don't come near me...!!"

It wouldn't stop. Certain death.

"Don't come any clos—"

Impact.

Swallowing even the demon's dying scream, the bullet of despair vanished along with a dark red and black flash.

◇

"Ah... how wonderful..."

Having buried the demon using his technique, I was entranced by its beauty.

His power really is... wonderful... It makes me realize once again the happiness of receiving his love all to myself.

"But I'm still nowhere near his level... The power is weak, and firing one shot seems to be my limit right now..."

But I will definitely catch up to him... because someday, I want to stand by your side.

"Omega—no, Lord Omega... I pray... that you will...!"

I clasped my hands together and prayed from the bottom of my heart on that beautiful moonlit night.

◇

Wh... What the hell is that appearance of Luna's!!??

Secretly watching Luna's battle against the demons from afar, I was completely captivated by Luna's anomalous black appearance.

Eh? Eh!? Seriously, what is that form!?

I've never seen that form in the anime, let alone in any other media. That means that form of Luna's is a brand new transformation that wasn't in the anime.

Is that form a power-up born from her despair? Or is it something else... Whatever it is, it's absolutely the best!!!

To not only get Luna's ultimate *kumorase* but also the debut of a new form... Ahh... Seriously, how much more exciting can this world get...!

My excitement was currently at its peak.

"Fufufu... Fuhahaha... Hah!!! Hahahah!!!"

Laughter naturally spilled from me.

I feel incredibly refreshed... I feel like I could burst into song right now!!!

"Looking away while in the middle of a battle with me?"

At that moment, Selec appeared in front of me.

...This bastard... Thanks to him, I can't see what Luna is doing!

"To be more obsessed with Magical Girl Luna than with me... It makes me feel a bit jealous."

Selec said it like a joke.

...I don't dislike you, Selec. It's just that right now is not the time to be dealing with someone like you.

"You're in the way. Move right now."

"I cannot do that. I also have a job—"

"Is that so. Then forget it."

In that instant, I kicked Selec squarely in the face with all my might.

"Gaghh...!"

Selec's kicked body violently pierced through a cluster of buildings.

Ooh, he went flying! I meant to kick him lightly, but he really flew... I might have overdone it a bit...

Even if it was to blow away a demon, he crashed through several buildings. I wonder how much the damages will cost... scary...

I was counting how many buildings he pierced through like skipping stones on water, but I stopped at 5 and was about to start watching Luna again when—

"Haa... Haa... You really sent me flying quite far, didn't you..."

The voice I had just heard a few seconds ago came from behind me.

...Ehh... Seriously...

When I turned around, I saw Selec, still alive despite the top half of his head having been blown off.

No, rather than being surprised he was alive... it's incredibly gross... To be alive even with half his face missing, does he have the vitality of a cockroach or something...

Come to think of it, Selec was quite persistent in the anime as well...

"You're still alive?"

"...Of course. Despite how I look, I am one of the 'Seven Demon Emperors'."

Right, he was... What a pain in the ass...

"I'll let you run away if you do it now, you know?"

"Fuffu, surely you jest. If I did something like that, my master would be furious with me."

I want to keep him alive because he provides precious *kumorase*, but... he's really annoying... hmm...

After hesitating for a bit.

Alright, he's in the way, so I'll just beat him up. He's probably tough, so he won't die easily!

"However, you are the first one. To push me this far... I will also go all out from here on. Evolution—Gboh!"

"The unwritten rule of magical girls... 'Attacking during a transformation is strictly forbidden' doesn't apply to you, right?"

I immediately punched Selec as he was trying to transform, interrupting it.

Because not getting attacked while transforming is a privilege reserved exclusively for magical girls. Yep, yep.

"Guuh!! Evolu—"

I relentlessly punched Selec as he tried to transform again, giving him no time to do so.

"It's useless."

Alright, then I'll leave you half-dead properly this time!

I chanted.

"Despair—"

"...Fufu, it's only partial, but I made it in time."

The moment Selec laughed, sharp claws appeared on his right hand.

"Hmph!"

I tried to dodge Selec's attack but barely didn't make it in time, and his claws slightly grazed my mask.

"What a shame, I wanted to see your face..."

That was close... Aiming for the mask, huh... What would I do if it broke?

I hurriedly held my mask in place with my hand while kicking Selec upward into the sky.

Immediately after, a dark red and black magic circle unfolded.

Then—with a sharp *crack*, a piece of my mask chipped off.

"【Despair Night】"

In that instant, particles of light that led to destruction were fired at Selec.

"So this is... the God of Ruin... Omega's—"

Trying to say something at the very end, Selec vanished from the spot along with the light of despair.

Nothing was left there anymore.

Crap, my mask broke...

I hurriedly covered the broken part around my eye with my hand and fixed the mask.

Phew... that was dangerous... If anyone had seen me, my life as a mob would have been over...

"...Now then, back to where I was ♪ —Eh..."

When I looked towards Luna, the battle between Luna and the demons had already ended, and there was nothing left there.

...It's already over!!

No way, I wanted to see more of Luna's new form in action...

...But I got to see the ultimate *kumorase* and Luna's new form today, so I guess I'll call it a win!

Even so, I'm really lucky today. I've never had such a happy day.

Well then... time to head home and reflect on today...

"Wait, Omega."

A voice came from somewhere.

This voice is...

"Luna..."

"Yes, your very own Luna... Omega ♡"

Luna said bewitchingly.

Hm? Luna's appearance is different, sure... but didn't her personality change too?

"How is it? My new form..."

"...It's beautiful."

I spoke my honest thoughts.

As expected, Luna in this black outfit is so cool and cute~! Honestly, it's so beautiful it's practically a work of art.

"Thank you. Fufu, yes... this form is proof... of the depth of my love..."

Love...?

"...Mhm, I see."

For now, I gave a noncommittal reply.

Ah, I really want to go home early today... I have to reflect on today's *kumorase*.

"...It's about time I left."

"Ah... wait, there's one last thing I want to ask you... Omega, are you—"

"Farewell, Magical Girl Luna."

Omega... I want to see you...

Even though we had just parted ways, I already wanted to see Omega so badly I could hardly stand it.

As expected... my mind and body are helplessly seeking Omega.

...But... this time... there was a massive development I didn't expect.

But I have to confirm the facts regarding this matter... by myself.

An ominous, evil power... but it was warm. I could clearly feel his power like that inside me.

This basically means... we are one in body and soul now, doesn't it?

One body and soul with him... Ah... Just thinking about it makes my entire body throb... ♡

"Come to think of it... Luna... that black form... and that incredible power..."

Looking at my appearance, Fiona spoke in wonder.

Yes... Someday, I want to use this power to be of use to him... For his sake, I would even throw away my life.

Fufufu, I'm looking forward to that time...

"Well then... Let's save the detailed talk for tomorrow or later and disband for now. We have school tomorrow, after all."

"Let's... meet again... Omega ♡"
