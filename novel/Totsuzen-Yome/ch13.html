Volume 1 Chapter 13: Gloom

Chapter 13: Gloom

"Sigh... good grief... becoming president is fine and all... but as expected, it's tough..."

Having finished checking the documents, I looked up at the ceiling alone.

I have obtained everything.

Power, wealth, and authority.

At first glance, it seems I am fulfilled in every way, but not once has my heart ever been truly satisfied.

I have merely tried to somehow fill the void of the countless things I've lost, and no matter how much I try to fill that massive loss, it can never be filled. A sense of emptiness, like a gaping hole, still remains in my heart.

"Everyone... I want to see you..."

I tried speaking to my former comrades in the photograph displayed on my desk, but there was no reply.

Everyone in the photo looks exactly as they did back then, while I alone continue to age. Now, I'm already 28.

They teased me, asking if I wasn't going to get married soon... but well, I'm going to stay single like this.

Doing this strongly makes me realize that I am alone. That I will never be able to meet my comrades again.

Along with the feeling of missing my comrades, a pitch-black emotion that I had kept locked away inside me welled up.

"Those guys alone, I will absolutely deal with by my own hands... Those two demons who murdered my precious comrades and my cute juniors...!"

The second-generation magical girls... their end was truly tragic... By the time I rushed to the scene, they had already been annihilated.

That guy's sadistic smile from back then is vividly burned into my mind.

I never want to see a sight like that ever again.

So far, those girls, the third generation, have somehow made it this far without losing a single member. I want them to survive.

I stood up from my chair and gazed at the nightscape spreading beyond the glass.

"...It hasn't changed."

The nightscape we all watched together in the past continues to shine unchangingly even now. This is proof of the people's peace, and what we fought to protect.

We must continue to protect this scenery from here on out as well.

Heh... Even I feel embarrassed calling myself a magical girl at this age... Besides...

"*Cough, cough*... I am also... nearing my limit..."

However, I will use this life given to me by my comrades to the very end to protect those girls and this world that everyone defended.

"Watch over those girls... everyone..."

◇

"Phew... I'm really tired from yesterday..."

Because I stayed up late doing Student Council work, fatigue had been accumulating.

Also... I wonder why everyone was missing yesterday...

After finishing my Student Council work, I immediately headed to the meeting, but everyone was already gone by the time I got there.

I wonder if the meeting ended earlier than expected... If so, I feel like Yui would have definitely contacted me, though...

I tried contacting everyone, but no one replied.

Everyone... what happened...

While walking to school feeling puzzled, I saw a familiar figure from behind.

That's... Yui...!

I approached Yui to ask her directly about the situation.

"Yui, good morning."

When I called out, Yui turned around.

Eh...

However, something was off about her appearance, and her eyes held an empty void.

"...Are you okay? You look like you're not feeling well..."

"I'm fine. I'm as energetic as usual."

However, her voice completely lacked its usual cheerfulness.

"I see... if that's the case, then fine, but..."

"If you don't need anything, I'm going."

"Ah, wai—"

Without giving me a chance to stop her, Yui walked away.

...Yui must be tired too... that must be it... it must be...

I convinced myself of that, having no choice but to accept Yui's unusual behavior.

Carrying those murky feelings, I attended my classes and eventually, the lunch break arrived.

First, I need to find everyone...

As I walked around the campus looking for everyone, I quickly found Yūna.

"Ah, Yūna—"

"Excuse me. I have library committee work to do."

When I called out, Yūna walked right past me and headed off somewhere.

Again...

For some reason, she wouldn't deal with me when I called out. It felt as if I was being avoided.

This time... I decided to go see Mio. I generally know where Mio goes.

When I went to the courtyard benches, just as I expected, Mio was there.

"Mio."

"......"

"Mio, are you listening?"

"......"

I called out to Mio, who was staring blankly while sitting on an outside bench, but there was no reply.

She was just gazing off somewhere with hollow eyes.

...Mio is... hard to understand... she's always kind of like that...

After a little while, Mio silently stood up and walked off somewhere, just like the others.

Why... everyone...

I racked my brain trying to think if I had done something to my comrades, but nothing came to mind.

Maybe I accidentally did something terrible to everyone without realizing it... I'm... clumsy, after all.

Because of that, I couldn't make a single friend in elementary and middle school.

"Sigh... I couldn't talk to anyone in the end..."

I tried looking for Ayano too, but I couldn't find her.

Everyone... why are you leaving me... all alone...

I tried to endure the sorrow of loneliness, but as expected, it wasn't working.

I didn't want to lose the friends I had finally made. I didn't want... to return to being alone again.

Thinking that, I headed for the roof.

As if clinging to my last ray of hope.

To my friend... Sōta.

I came to the roof yesterday too, but Sōta wasn't there.

If he's not here today either...

Feeling such anxiety, I opened the door.

◇

"Sōta."

"Hm? Oh, it's just you, Reina."

While I was on the roof, Luna made a rare appearance.

"So you're here today. Where were you yesterday?"

"Hm? Yesterday I ate in the cafeteria with Kurusu-san—wait, why are you making such a scary face!"

"I'm not doing anything of the sort."

"You totally are!"

『So you were getting along with female friends other than me again...』

Muttering something quietly, she sat down next to me.

I feel like she's in a bad mood today...

"...Well, whatever. Let's just have lunch. Here, accept this gratefully."

"I feel kind of conflicted about this... well, thanks, this helps."

I took the bento and unwrapped it.

Inside, there was a lineup of Japanese dishes that looked even more elaborate than usual.

"Ooh... this looks delicious again... thanks for the food!"

Yep, it's good!

I've been eating nothing but convenience store food lately, so this really hits the spot... I guess I've actually grown quite fond of the taste of Luna's bentos.

"Hey, Sōta."

"Hm, what is it?"

"Will you... stay my friend forever?"

"Hm? Yeah, as long as you keep giving me bentos, we're friends for life."

"Oh? Then instead of a friend, wouldn't that make you my pet? I wonder what I should name you..."

"Guh... well, I guess that's fine... no, it's not fine! Who are you calling a pet!"

"Fufu, I'm joking. You're the same as always, which is truly reassuring. Please stay exactly like that from now on."

"Hm? Uh, yeah..."

What does that mean?

"......"

"......"

What's up with this silence... it's so awkward...

We haven't spoken a word to each other for nearly 10 minutes now, and the silence was continuing. Honestly, this silence is as uncomfortable as hell.

But the saving grace for me was that Luna looked less energetic than usual, somewhat lacking spirit, as if she were despairing.

Ah... this is nice...

After letting me enjoy this for a while, Luna finally opened her mouth.

"Hey... do you think I'm a terrible person...?"

"...Where did that come from all of a sudden?"

Just as I thought... it's finally starting.

While my heart pounded with the premonition of *kumorase*, I calmly listened to her.

"I've been thinking lately... that because I'm so cold to everyone around me..."

"I mean, there's definitely truth to that."

"I knew it... you're right..."

Her expression clouded over even more.

This is the absolute best.

"Did something happen...?"

"Not really... nothing..."

"There's no way it's 'nothing' with a face like that. Talking to someone might make you feel a bit better. Right?"

"...I suppose... you're right..."

Yes! Now I can hear the details of her despair!

I did a mental fist pump.

"Actually, I feel like my friends have been avoiding me lately... even when I talk to them, they immediately walk away..."

There's no mistaking it.

This really is *that* massive *kumorase* event!

"I see. It's not like you did anything to them, right, Reina?"

"No... nothing comes to mind... I genuinely don't know why I'm being avoided..."

At first glance, the magical girl Luna of the 'Silver', Reina, seems like a perfect, flawless, and cold-hearted beauty with brains and looks, but that's actually wrong.

That unapproachable aura and sharp tongue are just weapons to protect herself. In reality, she has a heart as delicate and fragile as sugar candy. She appears to be the most mentally resilient among the magical girls, but it's actually the exact opposite—she's the most mentally fragile and unstable.

What would happen if such a girl were avoided by the comrades she treasures more than anyone else... Luna's current state tells the whole story.

"I see, that sounds rough."

"It's not like you'd understand anything anyway..."

Immediately after glaring at me and muttering that as if taking her anger out on me, her expression instantly filled with deep regret.

"Ah...! I-I'm sorry...! What is wrong with me...! Please... don't abandon me... If even you leave me... I'll... I'll...!"

"I don't really mind. That's just how you usually are, isn't it?"

Perhaps relieved by my usual demeanor, her frightened expression softened a bit.

"Right... it was... Hmph, I'm feeling down and you don't even have a single word of comfort for me?"

Ah, it's the usual Luna...

"Do you want me to comfort you?"

"Not really, I don't need comfort from someone like you."

"Ehhh... isn't that contradicting what you just said?"

"*Sigh*... Talking to you makes me feel like an idiot for being so depressed..."

"That's good to hear. ...But, well... if you ever reach your limit, call me. I can't offer any good words of comfort, but I can stay by your side."

Exactly, because I want to admire the *kumorase* from the front row, after all!

"...Thank you, I'll do that."

She muttered that with a slight blush on her cheeks.

◇

After school.

The sky had already begun to darken, and the students with club activities were gradually heading home.

"I suppose I should head home soon."

Having been working in the Student Council room, I set down my pen and stood up from my chair.

When I concentrate on something, I can forget my anxieties during that time, so I ended up working late.

"Sōta... says some cool things once in a while too..."

I muttered, recalling what happened during lunch break.

He looked more dependable than usual back then. Almost like Omega...

"No, they're completely different."

Sōta and Omega are complete opposites. There's no way a sloppy guy like him could resemble Omega.

...And yet, I find myself drawn to Sōta for some reason.

Omega, who has saved my life countless times, is overwhelmingly cooler... but I realized I had come to like Sōta just as much as I like Omega.

I wonder what this feeling is... but thanks to Sōta, I feel a little more courageous.

"Tomorrow... I'll try talking to everyone one more time."

Just as I resolved to do that, my phone's notification chimed.

I wonder who it is...

When I picked up my phone and checked the notification, there was a single message from Ayano.

『Can we meet right now?』
