【Digital Exclusive Bonus SS】 Daily Life of the Development Team

"Alison, I want to modify the Thunderbolt, so could you arrange a budget for that?"

Dr. Smith, clearly having come up with an idea, approached Alison with the excitement of a child.

With the tone of someone asking for a new toy, he instructed Alison to pull funds from the organization, causing her to drop the documents she was holding under her arm onto the floor with a thud.

While Dr. Smith spoke casually, applying for additional budget was no small task.

She would need to convince the higher-ups why the extra funds were necessary, and even if the budget was approved, it would take months… It meant a lot of back-and-forth with the upper management.

Already burdened with her own work, Alison let out a deep sigh, not wanting to take on more unnecessary tasks.

"What have you thought of this time?"

However, since he was her superior, Alison couldn't refuse without hearing him out.

Just to be sure, she asked for details, and Dr. Smith began to explain as if he had been waiting for this moment.

"It's about expanding the cockpit! Until now, we've been trapped by fixed concepts. Even if we replace all of Envy's body with parts from Gijuu, the amount of magic power released will only be limited. But that’s only if we maintain a human form, right? It wouldn’t matter if there were four arms instead! In fact, wouldn’t having more arms be more convenient for piloting a humanoid weapon? So, as a preparatory step to increase Envy's arms, we should expand the cockpit and reconsider the piloting methods—"

The more parts from Gijuu there were, the greater the release of magic power, so there was no denying the validity of Dr. Smith's proposal.

There was room for consideration in his suggestion.

Except for the fact that it was inhumane.

As a scientist herself, Alison could agree with some aspects of Dr. Smith's idea.

However, she was more realistic than he was.

"That's a no-go. First of all, such modifications would require significant changes on both the hardware and software sides. It would take time, and even if we added more arms, I doubt he could handle them well."

Humans do not have brains designed to manage four arms from the start.

It would be great if Ren, the pilot, could adapt well, but if he couldn't, it would be disastrous.

There was a risk of the project being halted altogether.

Calculating the merits and demerits, Alison firmly rejected Dr. Smith's proposal.

Dr. Smith slumped in defeat.

"I see... I thought it was a good idea, though."

◇

When Dr. Alison arrived at the Thunderbolt hangar, she found Ren activating the simulator in the cockpit.

It seemed he was training on his own initiative.

"You’re as diligent as ever."

When Alison called out, Ren popped his head out from the open hatch and saluted.

"Good work, Dr. Alison."

His earnestness and formality were unchanged, but after dealing with Dr. Smith's unpredictable nature, his response felt within the bounds of normalcy.

Recalling her earlier conversation with Dr. Smith, Alison decided to check with Ren.

"By the way, Corporal."

"What is it?"

"…Have you ever thought that having more arms would be convenient?"

Alison's sudden question startled the people around them who were working on the project. They exchanged worried glances, wondering, "Is she planning to increase the pilot's arms?"

After a moment of thought, Ren responded to Alison's question.

"I haven't thought about it, but I doubt I could handle more arms like my dominant one."

"I see. That’s a good way to think about it."

Ren didn’t suggest that having more arms would be fine, and Alison felt relieved.

The onlookers, observing the exchange, silently concluded, "Dr. Smith must have said something again."
