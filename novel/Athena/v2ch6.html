Chapter 6: Seiryuu Kiyone

It was during the break between lunch and the next training session.

I found myself at the outdoor fenced basketball court.

Lieutenant Hayase had invited me to play basketball, and I was being forced into a one-on-one match.

I was struggling to defend against Lieutenant Hayase, who was dribbling the ball, as I hadn't played basketball in ages.

In contrast, Lieutenant Hayase was so relaxed that she could engage in casual conversation while playing.

"This afternoon, we'll be using the actual machines for training, but how's your beloved machine doing?"

"The parts have arrived, so I've finished repairing the main body, but the armament is still to come."

"I was hoping to show it off, but that's a shame... right?"

Lowering her stance, Lieutenant Hayase skillfully slipped past me and executed a dunk, showcasing the physical abilities of a Valkyrie.

However, Lieutenant Hayase was currently in her skirted uniform.

As she jumped, her skirt lifted, and I hurriedly turned my face away from her.

"Uh... Lieutenant Hayase, there are issues with jumping around in a skirt."

Lieutenant Hayase retrieved the basketball and tossed it back to me.

"It's fine. I'm wearing spats under the skirt."

"Oh, in that case, it's alright. I was mistaken."

As I reflected on whether I was overly conscious, Lieutenant Hayase laughed.

"Just kidding."

I immediately turned my face away as Lieutenant Hayase attempted to lift the hem of her skirt.

"Please don't tease me, Lieutenant Hayase."

"It's just amusing to see you turn away every time, so I couldn't help it... Hmm?"

When Lieutenant Hayase looked beyond the fence, she spotted Nishitani-san and Asai-san... along with Instructor Kagami.

Instructor Kagami had her usual emotionless expression, but the other two seemed tense.

Lieutenant Hayase, making a face that suggested annoyance, seemed to sense something and asked Instructor Kagami.

"Did Suzuki do something?"

I thought it was a bit too familiar for Lieutenant Hayase to act that way toward Instructor Kagami, but since Kagami seemed to tolerate her attitude, it probably wasn't an issue.

After glancing at Nishitani-san and Asai-san, Instructor Kagami turned her gaze toward me.

"These two have something to discuss with you regarding a different matter. I was looking for you, Hifumi Ren. Please accompany me for a moment."

"Is it about me?"

When I shot a glance at Lieutenant Hayase, she nodded slightly, so I left the basketball court and followed Instructor Kagami to another location.

◇

I followed Instructor Kagami down a dimly lit corridor.

The windowless passageway left me wondering what purpose it served.

Some fluorescent lights were out, and the flickering ones were concerning.

How long had it been neglected?

I still hadn't been told the reason for my summons.

"Instructor Kagami, where are you taking me—"

"—Ren."

Instructor Kagami stopped and turned back, prompting me to halt as well.

I wondered what was going on, and it seemed that my way of addressing her was not to her liking.

"Weren't you taught this in Class Five? This is an academy. Even if you're a Valkyrie, the female students are expected to live their daily lives as students. Unless it's an emergency, refer to us as 'Instructor.'"

"I apologize for my rudeness. I'll be more careful from now on."

As I saluted, Instructor Kagami wore a faint smile.

"With that attitude, I can't say you've fully understood. By the way, the reason I called you here is about Maya."

"About Lieutenant Hayase?"

Instructor Kagami, now referred to as Kagami-sensei, started walking again, and I followed her.

It seemed she was concerned about Lieutenant Hayase as her homeroom teacher.

"That girl is talented but isolated in class. To be honest, I wish she would get along better with others, but I can also empathize with her perspective."

Lieutenant Hayase prioritized her own opinions and was strict with those around her.

Her disruptive behavior was also quite noticeable.

"Are you not going to guide her?"

"Maya tends to be stubborn. In that case, the best approach is to have her beloved subordinates support her."

As an older male, I was considered a beloved subordinate by Lieutenant Hayase.

That perception was likely accurate.

"I suppose I am a caring superior."

"You tend to be lenient with those you acknowledge. Once you get into her good graces, you'll likely get along well. However, that's not enough. If she doesn't break out of her shell and learn to interact well with others, Class Three will always be a burden to the academy."

"A burden..."

"With a lack of numbers and insufficient ability... To be frank, without Maya, Class Three would be considered ineffective. Boots Cat is the weakest among the four classes."

I had heard that the candidates from Class Five avoided Class Three, but if they were rated as the weakest among the four classes, it was understandable.

"...I am not a skilled person. I don't believe I can adequately support Lieutenant Hayase. Perhaps I should scout for a female student who is better at providing support."

"Aren't you running away by using clumsiness as an excuse? If there were someone capable of providing support, I wouldn't be asking you."

We could see the exit of the corridor ahead.

Instructor Kagami used the key she had to open a rusty door that had been neglected for years.

What lay before us was—

"So this is where it leads."

—The vicinity of the hangar where Thunderbolt was stored.

Using this passage would allow for quicker access between the academy building and the hangar compared to the usual route.

Instructor Kagami tossed a key to me.

"This is a shortcut. Consider it an advance on your reward."

"Did you get permission from the academy?"

"Of course. I have a strict superior, after all. I always make sure to follow the proper procedures so she can't complain."

I gripped the key I received tightly.

"As I mentioned earlier, I don't believe I can provide adequate support. All I can do is assist, albeit in a limited capacity."

"That's fine. Now, while the important matter is settled, we have something else to discuss."

Instructor Kagami said this and began walking toward the hangar where the Prometheus Project's machines were stored.

"Kagami-sensei? That area is off-limits to anyone not involved."

I tried to stop her, but Instructor Kagami waved her hand without turning around.

"I told you, right? I'm the type to have all the necessary procedures completed in advance."

When we arrived at the hangar, Dr. Allison was waiting for us.

Dr. Allison, who was sweating, frowned when we showed up.

"The machine is ready. It can participate in training at any time."

For a moment, I couldn't grasp what was happening with Dr. Allison's words.

"Are you saying Thunderbolt will participate?"

The Prometheus Project was supposed to be classified, yet it seemed it would be involved in training with the Valkyries.

As I was taken aback, Dr. Smith, who was approaching, answered me.

"You'll be participating in training with the Valkyrie Dress, right? It should yield some good data. I'm all for it."

While Dr. Smith seemed to welcome the opportunity for valuable data, Dr. Allison shot a disgruntled look at Instructor Kagami.

"That teacher has been egging Dr. Smith on. We haven't even finished proper adjustments yet, and she's acting so recklessly."

Instructor Kagami replied to Dr. Allison.

"This is a request from the academy. We want to see if the rumored humanoid weapon can truly withstand actual combat—through the eyes of the active Valkyries."

While Thunderbolt had performed well in actual combat, many of the active Valkyries viewed it with skepticism.

Indeed, there was a track record, but having only one instance could be attributed to chance.

For the active Valkyries to acknowledge it, this joint training was a convenient opportunity.

However, it seemed Dr. Allison believed they were unprepared.

"Are you disregarding our circumstances? Besides, even if it participates in a mock battle, it has no weapons. Do you have a paint gun for Thunderbolt?"

Even if it were to participate in a mock battle, Thunderbolt had no weapons.

Using live ammunition was out of the question.

Without a paint gun, participation would be difficult.

Instructor Kagami crossed her arms beneath her ample chest and answered Dr. Allison's question.

"That's unnecessary. Our academy can conduct mock battles using MR technology. As long as we have the data for the machine and its armament, we can perfectly recreate it in a virtual space."

Dr. Allison let out a small sigh.

"Mixed reality, huh... It's nice to see that well-funded places have a different flavor of training."

While they were struggling with their budget, the Valkyries were using the latest technology for their training.

I could understand Dr. Allison's desire to express her frustration, but Dr. Smith was visibly delighted.

"It's great to see that the training facilities are well-equipped. Now then, let's hand over the previous armament data and have it recreated."

As Dr. Smith attempted to hand over the data, Dr. Allison looked at him wide-eyed.

"Are you out of your mind? Thunderbolt is a bundle of secrets."

"The important parts are black-boxed. But most of it is based on technology from ten years ago, so I wouldn't mind if the data were taken."

"You're really something..."

Dr. Allison placed her hand on her face, exasperated, but Dr. Smith's opinion prevailed in this situation.

In preparation for the afternoon training, the female students of Class Two were changing in the locker room.

Kiyone, who was favored by Reona, was using the locker next to hers.

Once Kiyone finished changing into her pilot suit, she struck up a conversation with Reona, who was in her underwear, about the mock battle.

"I heard there will be a class competition in the mock battle."

Kiyone's straightforward approach to the topic prompted Reona to respond while changing.

"That's right. Yoshizawa-sensei is all fired up since we're up against Class Three. It seems she wants to teach them a lesson since she doesn't agree with Dainichi-sensei's methods!"

Reona said this nonchalantly, as if she were caught up in the rivalry between the teachers, but Kiyone didn't laugh.

"Please let me face Hayase. She will definitely show up, right?"

Reona, who had stopped changing, glanced sideways at Kiyone.

"She will show up. After all, Hayase-chan is the ace of Class Three."

"Let me fight her. I will crush Hayase."

Kiyone's declaration, filled with determination, was heard by the other female students.

Naturally, their gazes turned toward the two of them.

"Sorry, but I'll be facing Hayase-chan. Come on, a battle between aces is bound to be exciting, right? Kiyone-chan, why don't you take on that girl who's being favored by Sorakujima? It wouldn't be a bad matchup for two ace candidates, right?"

Reona resumed changing, signaling that the conversation was over, but Kiyone wouldn't back down.

"I can definitely win against Hayase."

Reona felt a sense of admiration for Kiyone's strong competitive spirit against Maya.

(This strong will to win is fitting for an ace. But, it's probably not the right time.)

Reona wiped the smile off her face and approached Kiyone, pressing her hand against the locker with a thud!

"Are you implying that I would lose to Hayase-chan?"

Realizing the implication in her own words, Kiyone quickly clarified.

"...No, that wasn't my intention."

"Even so, that statement is a no-go. If you really want to face Hayase-chan, you should first take the ace position from me."

"...Understood."

As Reona distanced herself from Kiyone, she scanned the surroundings.

From the gazes of the female students in Class Two, it was clear they were irritated by Kiyone's rude remark.

Reona resumed changing, letting out a sigh in her heart.

(Kiyone-chan has the potential to be an ace, but she’s not fit to lead a squad. I guess I’ll have to prepare a separate leader for the next generation.)

Once Reona finished changing, she closed her locker door and turned to the female students of Class Two.

"Alright, let's do our best this afternoon. And my lovely little kittens—don't fall behind Class Three, or there will be consequences."

"Yes!"

Everyone, except for one, responded cheerfully.

The one who didn’t respond was Kiyone, who was biting her lip in frustration.

Reona noticed but chose to ignore it.

◇

When using the Valkyrie Dress, the training ground is located outside the academy—up in the sky.

Even though it's spacious, the academy grounds are too small for the Valkyrie Dress to fly around.

Thus, training is typically conducted outside the academy.

The Valkyries arrived at a deck set up on the outskirts of the academy.

There, the Valkyrie Dresses that had been transported were lined up, secured by a single arm.

As the Valkyrie Dresses from Class Two and Class Three were displayed, I noticed the differences in their designs.

"Looking at them like this, each class has a different design, huh?"

"Of course, the supporting companies are different."

Lieutenant Hayase, now dressed in her pilot suit, answered as she admired the Valkyrie Dresses.

"Apparently, companies are actively developing dresses these days. I don't know the details, but from our perspective, it's just that each class has different supporting companies."

"I see. I didn't know anything about that."

It was a luxury to see the Valkyrie Dresses up close, something I could only look up at during my time as an infantryman.

Class Five used mass-produced training machines, while Class Three utilized Valkyrie Dresses characterized by their rounded designs.

In contrast, the Valkyrie Dresses of Class Two had a more angular and rugged appearance.

They incorporated designs reminiscent of the large sleeves that protect a samurai's shoulders, giving them a strong Japanese impression.

The pilot suits of the Valkyries also incorporated Japanese elements.

As I curiously gazed at the lined-up Valkyrie Dresses, Lieutenant Hayase was looking elsewhere.

"Everyone else seems really interested in your machine."

The standout among the lined-up Valkyrie Dresses was Thunderbolt from the Prometheus Project.

Without any securing arms, it simply stood on the deck.

Members of the development team bustled around, and the female students watched their activity.

"It's quite large."

"Does it have no weapons?"

"It looks clumsy, but can it really keep up with our battles?"

The female students voiced their thoughts.

Lieutenant Hayase's eyes sparkled with excitement.

"Your machine definitely has more of a robot feel to it. I don't dislike the Valkyrie Dress, but I prefer robots."

Lieutenant Hayase was the only one who seemed to have a favorable impression of Thunderbolt.

"That's surprising."

"I've always liked hero stories more than magical girl ones. Personally, I preferred the sentai shows with giant robots. How about you?"

"I personally favored the rider stories—"

As I thought about the shows I liked as a child, which were more about sentai than anything else, Lieutenant Usami approached from a group of Class Two female students.

Behind Lieutenant Usami was Seiryuu Lieutenant.

"Humanoid weapons really stand out, huh? My Valkyrie Dress pales in comparison."

Straightening up when Lieutenant Usami appeared, I noticed he waved at me.

"So this is your beloved machine? Or should I call it your prince's steed? It has a rugged and cool vibe, but personally—"

Before Lieutenant Usami could continue discussing Thunderbolt, Lieutenant Hayase stepped forward.

"Don't you want to talk about the mock battle? Sorakujima-senpai is over there."

Sorakujima-senpai, who was in charge of the female students of Class Three, was standing some distance away from us.

Lieutenant Usami shrugged.

"I just wanted to chat with the rumored male pilot. You don't need to worry; I won't steal her away from you, Hayase-chan."

Lieutenant Usami smiled meaningfully, and Lieutenant Hayase frowned.

Seeing her react to his provocation, it seemed Lieutenant Usami was the more mature one.

"Class Two can't handle this guy anyway. Besides, Yoshizawa-sensei, our homeroom teacher, is a male-hater, right? You like girls, and that unfriendly Seiryuu is a male-hater. See? You don't match at all."

I hadn't expected Class Two, which disliked men, to pull me in.

After glancing at me, Lieutenant Usami tried to clear up Lieutenant Hayase's misunderstanding.

"I don't dislike men."

"Class Two's male-hating tendencies haven't changed. More importantly—Seiryuu has been glaring at me since earlier. Can you tell her to stop?"

When Lieutenant Hayase shifted her gaze, Seiryuu Lieutenant wore a stern expression.

Her white suit had purple lines matching her hair color.

Lieutenant Usami let out a small sigh.

"Sorry about that. I heard she really wants to fight Hayase-chan in the mock battle."

Upon hearing this, Lieutenant Hayase provocatively challenged Seiryuu Lieutenant.

"Sure, I can fight you. It sounds fun to show the absolute difference between an ace and a candidate."

Lieutenant Hayase exuded overwhelming confidence.

She believed she was the ace and that she wouldn't lose.

Such arrogance was enviable to me.

She had undeniable skill and could trust herself.

Lieutenant Hayase appeared dazzling to me.

Seiryuu Lieutenant's expression grew even more severe as she was provoked.

"An overconfident big fish—"

As Seiryuu Lieutenant's words grew harsh, Lieutenant Usami, looking a bit exasperated, admonished her.

"Don't use foul language, Kiyone-chan. Sorry, Hayase-chan. I'll talk to Kiyone-chan about this."

In light of Lieutenant Usami's apology, Lieutenant Hayase seemed willing to back down.

"I'll fight you if I become the ace. So, for the mock battle, I'm fine with facing Usami, right? I won't hold back."

When Lieutenant Hayase said this, Lieutenant Usami burst into laughter.

The two of them smiled at each other, seemingly fueling their fighting spirit for the mock battle.

As a highlight of this joint training, Class Three and Class Two would engage in a rivalry.

On the surface, it was meant to assess each other's abilities, but in reality, it was an opportunity to demonstrate that their class was stronger.

It seemed that both sides were ignited with fighting spirit.

I had witnessed similar scenes during my time as an infantryman on the ground.

There were many occasions where units competed against each other, and each time, excitement ensued.

It appeared that this dynamic remained unchanged even in the sky.

I found myself feeling nostalgic about the atmosphere around me.

...Though I wondered if it was acceptable for the Valkyries to have the same vibe as the infantry on the ground.

Lieutenant Usami responded to Lieutenant Hayase's provocation.

"You're really suited to be an ace, Hayase-chan. But don't underestimate me. If you embarrass yourself in front of your cute subordinate, it'll be quite the sight."

"If I were to lose to you now, I'd gladly hand over the ace position to someone else."

"...You really say that."

When Lieutenant Usami narrowed his gaze, Seiryuu Lieutenant, who had been standing behind, reached her limit.

"Do you really think you can defeat the ace of Class Two? There's no way someone who makes a subordinate out of a useless man like you could defeat Reona."

When Seiryuu Lieutenant interjected, Lieutenant Hayase shot her a glare.

"Shut up, you non-ace. This is a matter between Usami and me."

Lieutenant Usami also admonished Seiryuu Lieutenant.

"Kiyone-chan, I would have preferred if you left this to me."

Seiryuu Lieutenant retorted.

"I can't just stand by while the ace of Class Two is insulted. Besides—while I was told not to fight Hayase, I wasn't told not to target her subordinate, was I?"

Seiryuu Lieutenant shifted the conversation toward me.

If she couldn't fight Lieutenant Hayase, she would aim for me, her subordinate.

I spoke up.

"I'm sorry, but I can't unilaterally decide on opponents for the mock battle. This should be discussed with each of the instructors."

When I countered Seiryuu Lieutenant's opinion, she responded with blatant disdain.

"You're really foolish. A complete idiot who knows nothing—listen well. The ace of Class Two, Reona, is right here. If Reona acknowledges it, it will be accepted, you foolish idiot."

I was called an idiot three times, but I wasn't well-versed in the rules of the Valkyries.

I turned my gaze to Lieutenant Hayase.

"While they say that, is it really alright?"

Lieutenant Hayase, irritated with Seiryuu Lieutenant, answered.

"Usami will probably get Yoshizawa-sensei's approval. I can't imagine our instructors would object."

"In that case, there shouldn't be a problem."

I decided to accept this offer.

So, I sought permission from Lieutenant Usami.

"If it's alright with you, I would like to serve as Seiryuu Lieutenant's opponent."

Lieutenant Usami looked troubled.

"Well, that's... hmm, I would have preferred to see Kiyone-chan fight someone else. But, you know..."

Lieutenant Usami seemed reluctant, but upon seeing Seiryuu Lieutenant's eager expression, he reconsidered.

Lieutenant Usami checked with Seiryuu Lieutenant.

"Data on combat with humanoid weapons is too valuable, and it's uncertain how it can be utilized in the future. Are you sure you really want to do this?"

"Please let me do it. I want to see for myself how capable the subordinate chosen by Hayase is."

Seiryuu Lieutenant's gaze was directed not at me, but at Lieutenant Hayase.

Her attitude indicated that I was of no concern to her.

Lieutenant Hayase seemed displeased with that attitude and attempted to decline Seiryuu Lieutenant's offer.

"Don't ignore me and proceed as you please. I will decide who this guy fights."

Lieutenant Hayase closed the distance between herself and Seiryuu Lieutenant.

"Are you trying to be a nuisance? Your personality is really bad."

"I don't want to hear that from you. The one who failed to choose a competent subordinate is at fault. I'll make sure to show you how your precious subordinate gets wrecked."

"Who would recognize a match with you—"

Before Lieutenant Hayase could firmly refuse a match with me, Sorakujima-senpai, the chairwoman of Class Three, approached us.

It seemed she had come to check on the commotion, but she had been listening to the conversation.

"Sounds interesting. I'll submit a request to Miou-sensei as well."

Lieutenant Usami looked a bit surprised at Sorakujima-senpai.

"Is that okay? You have promising newcomers in your class too."

"I've been curious about the capabilities of humanoid weapons as well."

As the two chairwomen discussed, Lieutenant Hayase raised her voice at Sorakujima-senpai.

"Don't decide without my permission!"

"I made the decision based on my judgment as the squad leader. There's no such thing as 'without permission.'"

When Sorakujima-senpai said this, Lieutenant Hayase fell silent, and now all eyes were on me.

Sorakujima-senpai addressed me.

"So, you'll be having a mock battle with Class Two's Seiryuu Lieutenant. Since it's a good opportunity, let's have you go first. Everyone is probably curious about the humanoid weapon and your capabilities."

As I scanned the surrounding area, I noticed the gazes of the female students from Classes Two and Three were fixed on me.

Those were not particularly friendly gazes.

They were evaluating me, and some even looked down on me—something I had seen many times before.

I saluted Sorakujima-senpai.

"Understood. I will hurry with the adjustments to the machine."

"Great, I'm looking forward to the capabilities of the one chosen by Hayase."
