Chapter 12: The Ace's Pride

The sky, filled with Gijuu, had unknowingly turned a deep crimson.

Against the oncoming second-class Gijuu, Kiyone showcased her honed skills.

With her body already nearing the limits of fatigue, she swung her sword to cleave through an enemy—only for the blade to reach its breaking point and shatter.

Seeing the broken sword, Kiyone immediately discarded it and deployed her subspace container.

"Next!"

As she extended her hand, a container suddenly appeared, and when it opened, a new sword flew out from within.

Catching it mid-air, Kiyone swiftly sliced through the Gijuu that had charged at her.

"?!"

She immediately took flight to evade the next attack.

In the spot where Kiyone had just been, beams were being unleashed in succession, engulfing the second-class Gijuu.

Looking closely, she noticed a group of Eagle Boars targeting her.

A Baooga was also closing in, preparing to strike.

Meanwhile, Kiyone was drenched in sweat, having exhausted her limit release.

Though she appeared in her normal form now, the overuse of the limit release—pushing the Valkyrie Dress to its limits—had left cracks on her frame, despite not having taken any hits.

The performance of the dress had clearly diminished.

"This is the last of my swords... Fine, I'll keep fighting until the end!"

Realizing she had exhausted all the weapons stored in her subspace container, Kiyone was left with almost no strength to continue fighting.

Yet, her spirit remained unbroken as she resolved to fight on.

As the Baooga approached, Kiyone attempted to strike, but it was the dress that faltered first.

With propulsion lost, it became difficult to even stay airborne.

"At a time like this!?"

The Baooga seized the opportunity, bringing its massive hands down to slam Kiyone.

She was knocked down, landing on the corpse of a special-class Gijuu.

Crashing against the hard remains, pain surged through her.

"Damn it..."

By the time Kiyone tried to rise, she was already surrounded by first-class Gijuu.

The Baooga drew closer, while the Eagle Boars behind it glared with eyes ready to unleash beams.

There was no escape. And there was no force field left to defend against the attacks.

──The magic power of the Valkyrie Dress had run out.

Kiyone, weighed down by the heavy Valkyrie Dress, still held her sword, showing her will to fight.

"If Reona and the others managed to escape... then my fight had meaning. One day, you all will be defeated by Reona and my juniors..."

Kiyone was putting on a brave front, but inside, she was screaming that she didn't want to die.

(Ah, I wanted to live longer. I wanted to show my family that I was strong, and more than anything... I wanted to become even stronger.)

Suddenly, the image of Ren, who had once asked her to teach him swordsmanship, flashed in her mind.

Kiyone couldn't help but chuckle at herself.

Ever since her father chose her brothers as successors, she had despised men, yet here she was, recalling Ren at the very end.

(In the end, I couldn't teach him anything. But when was the last time someone asked me to teach them? It made me happy...)

Being asked to teach her swordsmanship had brought Kiyone joy.

It had been a long time since she heard such words, and it felt like her skills were being recognized.

Just as the Gijuu were about to pounce on Kiyone, time seemed to slow down for her.

It was then.

Several Eagle Boars exploded, pierced by something.

They had been attacked while concentrating their magic to unleash beams, resulting in a spectacular explosion.

"That attack was...?"

Glancing instinctively at the source of the attack, she saw the Thunderbolt, wielding a large weapon.

The communication line used during the match was still open between Kiyone and the Thunderbolt.

『Effective hit confirmed with the electromagnetic projectile cannon. First-class Gijuu has been defeated.』

The weapon the Thunderbolt was wielding was a railgun, just like the one Kiyone had used.

"W-You copied my weapon!!"

As Kiyone shouted, Maya charged into the surrounding Gijuu.

『I knew you were still alive, Ace candidate.』

"Hayaseee!!"

With the joy of having an ally arrive, Kiyone couldn't help but yell in frustration at the sight of the very person she had mixed feelings about.

◇

Inside the cockpit of the Thunderbolt, I was checking the usage status of the railgun.

In its normal state, the railgun was a rectangular box, but it needed to unfold when firing.

The internal and upper rails extended, and a handle emerged to enhance stability.

The forceful connection method was problematic, but the size of the main cooling mechanism had increased due to the pursuit of power.

"The power is more than sufficient. However, it's cumbersome to handle."

The downside was that both hands would be occupied when in use.

Moreover, each shot required time for cooling and charging.

There were significant issues with its usage.

Still, despite these drawbacks, it had yielded results that far outweighed them.

"It's effective against first-class Gijuu. The question is whether we can survive this situation."

Looking around, I found myself surrounded by enemies.

At the moment, my role was to support Hayase Lieutenant.

While holding the light machine gun in my left arm, I reported to Hayase Lieutenant.

"Lieutenant Hayase! Thirty seconds until the next railgun shot is ready!"

Hayase Lieutenant had dived into the swarm of Gijuu to save Kiyone, taking down enemies one after another.

『Understood. When you're ready to fire again, take out the troublesome ones first. Dealing with the boars is a hassle.』

As she said this, Hayase Lieutenant charged straight towards the Eagle Boars, which she referred to as boars.

As Hayase Lieutenant approached, the Eagle Boars began their attack from the gaps in their shells.

Dozens of curved beams surged towards her.

I thought she would dodge, but she continued to charge forward.

"Lieutenant Hayase!?"

I couldn't help but shout as she didn't take evasive action.

『In situations like this, charging straight ahead is the best option.』

Saying that, Hayase Lieutenant dove into the incoming beams, weaving through the gaps to close in on the Eagle Boars.

With her star-shaped shield in both hands, she sliced through the enemies, then set her sights on the next target.

"What a reckless way to fight."

I was astonished, but Hayase Lieutenant seemed unsatisfied.

『It's better than you, who dives into the battlefield without defense! I have a proper force field to protect me!』

However, that force field had not been generated earlier since she had evaded all the attacks.

I aimed the light machine gun at the approaching Gijuu.

Pulling the trigger at the forehead of a second-class Gijuu, I hit it, but the shot was deflected by the force field.

"Is it really a lack of power?"

In the past, I would have used a gatling gun, and with plenty of ammunition, I could have fired dozens of rounds and overwhelmed them with sheer volume.

However, that wasn't the case with the light machine gun.

"While it's easier to aim than the gatling gun, it lacks both power and ammunition... But still!"

Accelerating the Thunderbolt, I closed in on the second-class Gijuu and thrust the bayonet attached to the light machine gun into its vital point.

I forcefully sliced through the force field with the blade and pulled the trigger.

The Gijuu was attacked from inside the force field, easily pierced through its vital point, and died.

"This method is manageable."

Withdrawing the bayonet from the defeated Gijuu, I scanned for the next target while checking the railgun's cooldown.

"Next shot ready. I will provide support from here."

Hayase Lieutenant said nothing, but she was searching for troublesome enemies while flying around.

She seemed particularly wary of the group of Eagle Boars, so I decided to aim for them.

"There is a limit to the number of projectiles. The low ammunition count will be a future concern."

The magazine filled with projectiles had not been prepared, and I could only fire the number that was loaded.

Six shots... that was the total for this railgun.

"Five shots left..."

As I carefully aimed, Kiyone shouted at me.

『You're being targeted!』

It seemed one of the Baooga had decided to come after us, thinking the Thunderbolt was a threat.

In the previous battle, it hadn't even been a contest.

In fact, even with the current Thunderbolt, it would be dangerous to engage it based on its performance.

The difference in magic power was too great; even if I focused entirely on defense, I wouldn't be able to withstand a Baooga's attack.

If I took even one hit, the Thunderbolt would be easily destroyed.

I repositioned the Thunderbolt and prepared to aim with the railgun.

After confirming the timing when the Baooga was concentrating its magic to unleash a beam──.

"......Fire."

Pulling the trigger of the railgun, the projectile pierced through the Baooga, which was gathering magic power, creating a large hole in its chest.

As if the magic power had gone berserk, the Baooga exploded and fell.

After confirming that I had taken down the Baooga, I looked to the other side.

I had successfully pierced through the Eagle Boar behind the Baooga, which was obstructing Hayase Lieutenant.

"Four shots left."

With this, the number of first-class Gijuu I had defeated reached five.

As for achievements, it was more than satisfactory, but the problem was ensuring the Thunderbolt returned safely.

Even if I took down five, it would be meaningless if I got shot down.

My mission was to bring back valuable data.

While I flew around, evading the Gijuu, I pondered how to use the remaining four shots while buying time until the next shot was ready.

During that time, the countdown until the next shot exceeded thirty seconds.

"......The time until firing is unstable? I should compile this in the report later."

Was it that the cooling wasn't keeping up, or was it an issue with energy supply?

I considered various possibilities while firing the third shot, but there was nothing I could do in the current situation.

"I should consult Dr. Allison about this once it's all over."

Dr. Smith was likely too excited about the fact that I had defeated a first-class Gijuu to care about the railgun anymore.

Maya, who had been dealing with the Gijuu, was impressed when she saw two Eagle Boars explode behind her.

"Wow, taking out multiple enemies in one shot, huh? Nice work."

Ren, providing support with the railgun, had positioned himself to take out multiple enemies in one shot.

He had simultaneously defeated two first-class Gijuu, and several second-class Gijuu had been caught in the blast afterward.

Since Valkyries counted their achievements based on first-class Gijuu or higher, the number of second-class Gijuu defeated wouldn't be counted.

Even so, Ren's sniping ability was astonishing.

As second-class Gijuu swarmed around them, Maya aimed her sub-arm's gatling gun and unleashed a barrage of bullets.

Unlike the Thunderbolt, the bullets, empowered by magic, easily pierced through the second-class Gijuu, taking them down.

One after another, the Gijuu charged in.

When a Baooga got too close, she shot its head with her right-arm sub-arm rifle to stun it, then decapitated it with her blade.

"So far, I feel like a pretty good artillery piece."

While fighting, Maya kept her focus on Ren and Kiyone, who was sitting atop the corpse of a special-class Gijuu.

The Gijuu were targeting the two of them, but if Kiyone, who was unable to resist, was attacked, it would be troublesome.

Judging that the time was right, Maya activated her limit release.

"Hayase Maya... Limit release request."

Once she requested the limit release, it began to transform without issue, as she had already received permission.

The function to enhance output and draw out maximum performance also increased the internal heat, causing parts of the internal structure to become exposed to enhance the cooling function.

This transformation was not just for the Valkyrie Dress; it also released the limits on the Valkyrie herself.

As Maya's red hair began to glow and shimmer, her entire body felt hot.

Her physical abilities were enhanced beyond the norm, affecting her reflexes as well.

As the Gijuu attacked, they appeared to be moving slowly to her now.

Even her blue eyes glowed, allowing her to see the movements of the Gijuu clearly.

The force field and magic power seemed to radiate colors, and their flickering made predicting the enemies' movements possible.

What would they do next...? From the movements of the Gijuu, Maya instantly determined the most efficient way to move and annihilate them.

"You're in my way, so be prepared if you interfere."

Maya coldly declared to the Gijuu, moving as if time flowed normally for her in the slow-moving chaos.

Approaching a group of Eagle Boars that were preparing to launch mid-range attacks at her, she dodged between the beams as she moved.

Once in range, she aimed her weapons fixed to her sub-arms and began firing.

Even the bullets she fired seemed to move slowly, and as soon as she shot, she set her sights on the next target.

Pulling out the gatling gun from her subspace container with her right hand, she took down the second-class Gijuu one after another.

The places Maya passed through were marked by trails of red light.

In the slow-moving chaos, Maya calmly checked the elapsed time.

"......That's enough. Limiting the limiter. Switching to normal mode."

After thirty seconds had passed, she restricted the limit, and white steam erupted from the heat dissipation plates of the Valkyrie Dress.

At the same time, a wave of fatigue and burden crashed over Maya.

"!? This is exhausting."

As soon as she finished speaking, Gijuu around her exploded, scattering their bodily fluids.

With the number of Gijuu around her rapidly decreasing, an Eagle Boar that had been targeting Maya with long-range attacks was shot down by the Thunderbolt.

『Lieutenant Hayase, are you okay?!』

Maya found it a bit amusing when Ren called out to her.

"Mind your own business. So, how many rounds do you have left?"

『I have over two hundred rounds left for the light machine gun, but only one shot remains for the railgun.』

Ren reported calmly, but perhaps sensing Maya's fatigue, he moved closer to provide protection.

Seeing that, Maya felt a bit comforted.

"If you only have one shot left, it’s better to retreat. You can fall back now."

It was dangerous to leave the Thunderbolt on the battlefield any longer in its current state.

Though it was a kind-hearted suggestion, Ren refused.

『If we’re retreating with Kiyone, then I’ll accompany you.』

"You're quite diligent. Or maybe you're just too rigid? Well, I don't dislike that."

Maya took a short break, but even during that time, Gijuu continued to pour out from the gate.

Watching that scene, Maya narrowed her eyes.

"There seem to be an unusually high number of reinforcements this time."

『Has there never been any before?』

"There might have been precedents, but since I became a Valkyrie, there hasn’t been a single instance. ──Now, I want to destroy that gate quickly."

『At this distance, we’re within the effective range of the railgun.』

The Thunderbolt aimed the railgun, but Maya stopped it.

"You can't destroy the gate with that."

『Then how do you plan to destroy it?』

"I think cutting it with a light blade would be the most effective. There are destruction weapons for the gate, but they’re not very user-friendly."

While confirming that her body was regaining its condition, Maya spoke to Ren.

"If you're going to join me, provide support. Getting close to the gate and then releasing the limit to attack would be the best plan for me."

『......I will accompany you.』

Ren's hesitation in responding was likely due to the fact that he only had one shot left for the railgun.

The light machine gun wouldn't be effective against the Gijuu, and he wouldn't be able to provide much support.

However, he was earnestly trying to accompany Maya.

Maya realized that this was a sign of his trauma from losing his former comrades, pushing him to take risks.

"You really aren't suited to be a test pilot."

『I think I understand that myself.』

"But—"

Maya looked up at the Thunderbolt.

"──As my partner, you pass."

Just as Maya was about to move to destroy the gate, the Valkyries of Boots Cat burst into the communication disruption area.

『Hayase, are you still alive?』

When Suzuka's voice reached her, Maya let out a small sigh.

"You're late! What have you been doing until now?"

Maya's exasperated tone prompted Suzuka to raise her voice.

『I came after supporting the Gringarm's retreat! I rushed here as fast as I could, and you can't even say thank you!』

Though Suzuka couldn't tolerate Maya's rude attitude, Mai intervened.

『That's enough for now. Destroying the gate is our top priority. ──Hayase, you can do it, right?』

When asked if she could destroy the gate, Maya confidently replied.

"Of course. I'm the ace."

『You're as overconfident as ever. In that case, take these kids with you.』

As Mai said this, the newcomers gathered around Maya.

"Are you serious?"

Maya's eyes widened in surprise as Kanako raised her fist energetically.

『I'm serious! I'm going to blow that sphere away and make a grand debut with my Boots Cat!』

Mami looked tense but was gazing at the Thunderbolt.

It seemed she had a competitive spirit.

『I will show that I have more value than Ichinose-san.』

Lastly, Megumi had a determined expression.

『If this is going to be my last chance, I want to go out with a bang!』

Maya let out a small sigh before smiling and addressing Ren and the newcomers.

"Follow me. I'll show you the best moment."

◇

As the main force of Boots Cat engaged the Gijuu, we—what should we call ourselves, the Hayase Squad?—the temporary squad began to move towards the gate for its destruction.

We headed straight for the gate, but of course, we encountered resistance from the Gijuu.

Leading the charge was──Nishitani-san.

『I'll blow every last one of you away!!』

Her Valkyrie Dress was equipped with large rifles on both arms.

Being an optical weapon, she charged forward while firing beams, and when Gijuu came close, she would swing down the great sword she held in both hands.

Her combat style was heavily skewed towards raw destructive power, much like her drastically changed personality.

That fearless demeanor was reassuring, but Asai-san, who was following behind, furrowed her brows and shouted.

『Use your head a little, you idiot!』

As she called her an idiot, her Valkyrie Dress launched all the missiles attached to her left arm.

The missiles flew forward, tracking the Gijuu and exploding one after another.

Asai-san herself was aiming with a scoped rifle.

Perhaps prioritizing ease of handling, it was of normal size, but she was targeting the Gijuu that had slipped past the missiles and those trying to latch onto Nishitani-san.

I also began shooting at the enemies that had slipped through with my light machine gun.

"If they're weakened, this should be effective."

As I took down the enemies whose force fields had weakened from being hit, Hayase Lieutenant, using the Thunderbolt as a foothold, laughed at the two's performance.

『Nice. You've got more guts than I expected.』

At one point, I wondered how things would turn out since it seemed Hayase Lieutenant had half given up on training the newcomers, but the two were proving to be reliable.

Instinctively trying to protect the gate, the Gijuu began to gather in our path.

As they gradually surrounded us and the enemy attacks increased, I spotted an Eagle Boar aiming at the Thunderbolt.

Just as I was about to decide to use the railgun, it began amplifying its magic power in preparation for an attack.

『Don't think you can easily destroy the ace's ride!』

Suzuki-san, treating the Thunderbolt as Hayase Lieutenant's vehicle, stepped in between and spread her sub-arms.

Deploying a large shield, she protected the Thunderbolt from being hit.

Once the attack ceased, Suzuki-san readied a rather large rifle with both hands.

After initially scattering beams, she quickly switched to sniping with the rifle.

It seemed to be a multi-purpose or switchable rifle.

Hayase Lieutenant, watching Suzuki-san's fighting style, was impressed.

『You've changed your style in a short time. ──Well, it suits you better now.』

As I pondered the meaning of her statement, Hayase Lieutenant spoke up.

『It's about time. I'm going to release the limiter, so you all better not die until I destroy the gate.』

On top of the Thunderbolt, Hayase Lieutenant was preparing to engage the limit release.

I could see her from the camera above, but I felt something was off about the angle and didn't look.

...It was an angle that seemed to peek from directly below, and the Valkyrie pilot suit accentuated her body line.

I recalled how the MC, hair stylists, and losers had stared at the beautiful women as they passed by, only to be warned by the gamblers.

Before I could get lost in those memories, something unusual happened to the Thunderbolt.

"The magic power output of the machine is increasing?"

Though the amount of magic power I was supplying hadn't increased, the instruments were reacting.

I looked up in surprise.

"Is it Lieutenant Hayase?!"

Then, the monitor displayed Hayase Lieutenant from a low angle.

Her hair and eyes were glowing, and particles of light were being emitted from the heat dissipation system of the Valkyrie Dress──her appearance was so beautiful that I couldn't help but be entranced.

The alert from the instruments snapped me back to the battlefield.

The magic power was swelling, causing some of the instruments to malfunction.

An unprecedented amount of magic power flowed into the Thunderbolt.

It surged through the machine and the cockpit's control stick, flowing back to me.

The magic power flowing in from my right arm was entirely different from the one produced by my transplanted limbs and organs.

Though it was similar magic power, my body recognized it as an alien force.

Hayase Lieutenant's magic power coursed through my body, rampaging violently.

It felt as if it represented Hayase Lieutenant's fierce personality.

"!? This is..."

In an instant, I saw an image of what Hayase Lieutenant was trying to do──what she was thinking.

She charged towards the gate, intending to slice through it with a blade of light infused with massive amounts of magic power.

However, there was an obstructive presence in her way.

(That Gijuu's position is a bit troublesome.)

When I connected Hayase Lieutenant and the gate in a straight line, one Baooga was in the way.

Whether I was to defeat the Baooga or slip past it, it felt bothersome.

Receiving the image, I realized that even if this was just a hallucination, the presence there was still a problem.

Before I knew it, I had the Thunderbolt aiming the railgun.

A magnetic field generated around the railgun, but due to the excessive energy and magic power supplied, discharges began to occur.

『Envy?』

Hayase Lieutenant was about to tell me what she wanted, but I had already pulled the trigger on the control stick.

The projectile fired with more output than ever before pierced through all the Gijuu in front of us.

It created a massive hole in the Baooga's torso and then pierced through to the gate──and was sucked in.

The gate itself wasn't damaged.

However, the railgun had created a clear path straight to the gate.

『......Nice. It feels like we're in sync.』

As Hayase Lieutenant quietly murmured, the Thunderbolt shook violently.

Using me as a foothold, Hayase Lieutenant took off, flying straight down the path created by the railgun like an arrow, closing in on the gate in the shortest distance.

From the small shield she held in her hand, a blade of light emerged.

『Stop sending annoying things my way!!』

With Hayase Lieutenant's shout, the light blade was swung in a cross pattern.

The elongated blade of light sliced through the gate.

The cross-sectioned gate spewed liquid and began to disappear as it fell.

Before it could reach the surface of the sea, it vanished without a trace, ensuring that no more Gijuu would emerge.

At the same time the gate disappeared, the communication disruption was lifted, and the cheerful voice of Professor Dainichi echoed.

『You did it, Boots Cat! We confirmed the destruction of the gate on our end as well. The safe ones can proceed with the cleanup. And Maya-chan?』

『......What?』

『You've overused the limit release. You can use the Thunderbolt as your vehicle, so come back.』

『Huh? I can still fight.』

Professor Dainichi seemed worried about Hayase Lieutenant, but she appeared determined to continue fighting.

Her usually bright voice turned low.

『......That's an order. If you want to stay a Valkyrie for a long time, come back.』

I felt a sense of undeniable authority.

Even I, who knew Professor Dainichi well, felt a shiver down my spine for a moment.

It seemed Hayase Lieutenant had given up on continuing the fight.

She approached the Thunderbolt and climbed aboard.

『Is this okay?』

『Good girl. And make sure to bring the newcomers back with you. We'll leave the cleanup operation to Mai-chan and the veterans!』

Professor Dainichi's voice returned to its usual brightness──what had happened to that earlier intensity?

"Then we will withdraw from the combat area."

『Please do. And... Envy, about earlier... it's fine. We'll talk about it slowly when you get back.』

"Understood."

As we prepared to return, the Boots Cat squad approached us.

A Valkyrie, presumably the squad leader, spoke to us.

『Liu Squad will provide escort until you withdraw. I wouldn't want any of the newcomers to die during the retreat. Hayase is probably useless after the limit release.』

It seemed they were kindly offering to escort us.

Hayase Lieutenant looked a bit annoyed, but...

『Even now, I'm stronger than you all.』

With that extra comment from Hayase Lieutenant, the members of Liu Squad began to complain.

『You're still as unlikable as ever.』

『Don't let the newcomers copy your personality.』

It was a conversation that felt perfectly normal.

...However, I couldn't shake the feeling that Hayase Lieutenant didn't truly find them bothersome.

For some reason, it felt like she was grateful.

What on earth was going on?

With symptoms of hallucinations and auditory hallucinations, I pressed my left hand against my face and shook my head.

It seemed I would have to consult Dr. Smith once we returned.

『Liu, make sure to bring Kiyone along too. If she's forgotten, she'll cry.』

Hayase Lieutenant instructed Liu Squad to pick up Kiyone as well.

『......Ah~...』

Liu Squad Leader's drawn-out response made it clear that she had forgotten about Kiyone's presence.
