Chapter 7: The Rivalry Match

Adjustments were being made to Thunderbolt for the rivalry match between Classes Three and Two.

As the pilot, I entered the cramped cockpit and began testing the MR that would be used in the match.

MR stands for Mixed Reality, a technology more advanced than Augmented Reality (AR).

In AR, digital objects are added to the real world, but you need a device to manipulate them.

However, in Mixed Reality, you can interact with the added digital objects as if they truly exist in the real world.

It's a more sophisticated technology.

Using this, it seems that Valkyries can simulate firing real ammunition when using weapons in mock battles.

Even though no actual shots are fired, bullets and beams appear to fly around, and if they hit, a hit detection is registered.

We were trying to incorporate that technology into Thunderbolt as well.

I heard Dr. Smith's voice through the intercom.

"Hmm... How is it, Envy?"

Since I was involved in the Prometheus Project and had boarded Thunderbolt, Dr. Smith called me by my callsign.

Envy, which means envy.

It seems fitting for me, who admired the strength of the Valkyries.

"I've checked all monitors. There are no issues. I can visually confirm that Thunderbolt's arms, shoulders, and legs are all armed without any problems."

The cameras placed around Thunderbolt displayed the weapons.

It truly looked as if Thunderbolt was armed.

I moved my arm experimentally, and the large-caliber rifle in my right hand moved as well.

Upon confirming the armaments, I found that Thunderbolt was equipped the same way as during its last sortie.

The number of rounds was displayed, accurately recreating an armed state.

"While it may not compare to reality, the displayed armaments are quite close to the real thing. I was surprised, as I expected the digital data to be much rougher."

I expressed my personal opinion, but it seemed like a rather mundane technology for Dr. Smith.

"While it's nice that the details are well thought out, it's not a groundbreaking technology. I admire how realistic it looks, but it doesn't replicate actual conditions. Ah, this won't do. Thunderbolt's weight is still that of an unarmed state. This won't allow us to simulate a weight limit situation. Even if I hold the rifle, it lacks weight, leading to slight discrepancies in actual movement. It won't be suitable for testing the magic power converter either. It's really just a setup for playing a game."

It seemed that Dr. Smith was not as impressed with the completion level as he had hoped.

However, standing next to him was Dr. Allison.

She showed irritation and exasperation at Dr. Smith's complaints.

"We went through a lot of trouble to incorporate that playful technology into Thunderbolt, you know?"

Dr. Smith didn't respond to Dr. Allison's sarcasm at all.

After all, Dr. Smith typically left this kind of work to his subordinates, including Dr. Allison.

He usually only worked in fields that interested him, so he often just observed the work.

"Well, being able to fight against active Valkyries will provide good data, right? Moreover, I hear the opponent is an ace candidate. Let's endure it, thinking we'll obtain valuable data."

Even though he wasn't particularly busy, he said he'd endure it because valuable data would be obtained.

Dr. Smith's words only served to further irritate Dr. Allison.

However, it seemed that Dr. Allison understood that complaining would be futile, as she said nothing more.

Her interest seemed to shift to me.

"The opponent is a top-tier warrior who will fight against a Gijuu classified as Special Class, not just First Class. Can I ask if you have any chance of winning?"

I replied while making adjustments to the machine.

"I have none. In the first place, I don't have enough information. All I know is that the opponent excels in close combat."

"Well, I do have a short sword prepared as a real weapon behind the knee armor, but how effective will it be in a sword fight against a Valkyrie? They seem to have better maneuverability."

It seemed that even Dr. Allison couldn't predict the outcome.

The opponent was about half our size, agile, and fast.

It might be safer to avoid close-quarters combat, but the truth was, you wouldn't know until you tried.

"I will do my best."

"Please do. But—there's no doubt this is a valuable opportunity. Do your best to accumulate combat data against the Valkyries as much as possible. Understood, Envy?"

Her tone was slightly lower than usual.

It felt like she was emphasizing that this order must be fulfilled.

"Understood."

Once the communication ended, I decided to check the machine until it was my turn.

As for the Valkyries, it seemed they would be conducting regular training until the rivalry match began.

They were going through the scheduled training while riding the actual machines.

It was then that I heard a voice.

"Can you hear me?"

Lieutenant Hayase's face appeared on the monitor.

Her hair was blowing in the wind, and it seemed she was flying around.

"Lieutenant Hayase? Aren't you supposed to be training right now?"

Worried, I checked, but she replied casually.

"I'm just playing around, chasing the rookies. By the way, you don't have detailed data on Seiryuu, do you?"

"No. We haven't been granted access to the Valkyries' personal information at this point."

Indeed, I had been assigned to Class Three, but fundamentally, I was a customer prioritizing the Prometheus Project.

Therefore, I didn't possess all the privileges that Valkyries were supposed to have.

"That one is from the Kougetsu Soutenryuu, right? Apparently, she was raised in a traditional martial arts dojo and has been trained in ancient martial arts since she was young. —Hey, if you don't run away for real, I'll kick you into the sea."

Along with the information about Lieutenant Seiryuu, I could hear the voice instructing the training.

It seemed that one of the rookies had been caught and kicked.

I could faintly hear a scream, so it seemed like they were having a rather rough training session.

"It seems she has a particular attachment to her sword. She wields it herself. Is it a sword or a tachi? Until recently, she had a gatling gun mounted on her sub-arm for suppression. Also, she's quite skilled at dual-wielding, so be careful."

Dressed in a Valkyrie Dress, she apparently had two blades fixed as her primary weapons.

The gatling gun on her sub-arm was used only for dealing with opponents she couldn't handle at close range—shooting didn't seem to be her focus.

"If we keep our distance, we can fight at an advantage?"

"You're foolish. If that were the case, she wouldn't be an ace candidate. You can assume her shooting skills are excellent as well. The problem is that the current one has an unfamiliar weapon mounted on her left sub-arm. Because of that, she's also changed the weapon on her right sub-arm. I don't have any information about the new equipment either."

While conversing with me, Lieutenant Hayase's communication was filled with the screams of the rookies.

It seemed she was still chasing and kicking them.

"Is it alright for you to share information about Lieutenant Seiryuu with me? I'm worried that it might put Lieutenant Hayase at a disadvantage."

If I overly supported someone like me, who was out of place in the academy, wouldn't it put Lieutenant Hayase in a bad position?

That concern lingered in my mind, but she sighed.

"You need to do this much to make it a fair fight. I can win, but the opponent is an ace candidate. In Class Three, there might be only one other person besides me who has a chance of winning."

According to Lieutenant Hayase's prediction, it seemed that Lieutenant Seiryuu had a higher probability of winning.

"I'll challenge the rivalry match with the intention of borrowing Lieutenant Seiryuu's strength."

"…Yeah, but that wording feels a bit off. It's not wrong, but still."

Lieutenant Hayase looked unconvinced, but she smiled.

At that moment, I heard the screams of the three rookies again, and I wondered what kind of situation they were in now.

"Show everyone here your strength. Um..."

She hesitated to call my name, so I told her my callsign.

"During missions, it's Envy, Lieutenant Hayase."

"That's not what I meant... Well, whatever. Go out there and make a splash, Envy."

"Yes, ma'am!"

Once the training with the actual machines came to a halt, Reona fixed her Valkyrie Dress and headed towards Kiyone.

"Kiyone-chan, are you really planning to go all out against Ichinose-kun? I don't think you're going to hold back."

If I had to sum up Kiyone, the Valkyrie, in one word, it would be "clumsy."

She was serious and never held back, actively participating in training.

She had also produced results in actual combat.

While she had the skill to dual-wield in battle, her awkwardness in social interactions stood out.

Kiyone was checking the condition of her dress, filled with enthusiasm for the rivalry match.

It was as if she had forgotten that it was just training.

"I haven't loaded any ammunition for shooting weapons. There's no need for you to worry, Reona."

"Are you joking? But even if you say that while equipped with blades as usual, what are you planning to do? Are you intending to slash Ichinose-kun to pieces? I want to avoid my benefactor having a terrible experience."

If Ren were to get seriously injured, I would bear responsibility as the class representative of Class Two.

Moreover, personally, I wanted to establish a friendly relationship with him.

I didn't want to complicate things here.

However, it was uncertain whether Kiyone understood that.

"I won't slash and break such a toy. But I do need to show the difference in our abilities."

"A toy, huh."

Feeling Kiyone looking down on her opponent, Reona became worried for a different reason.

Kiyone was completely oblivious to Reona's concerns.

"Since it's an inhumane Prometheus Project, don't you think it would be more socially beneficial to crush it here? —In the first place, it's strange for a man to be on the same battlefield as us. The sky is the domain of Valkyries."

Kiyone's true feelings lay in the latter.

Knowing Kiyone's circumstances, Reona couldn't easily interject.

"…Keep it within the bounds of training. Alright? It's a promise, Kiyone-chan?"

"Yes, that's my intention."

Kiyone agreed, but her dark eyes were fixed on the Thunderbolt, which was preparing.

Reona let out a small sigh in her heart.

(It's resentment that she couldn't inherit the dojo because she's a woman. I understand that there are deep-rooted issues, but if she doesn't switch gears, I won't be able to retire with peace of mind.)

The rivalry match was about to begin.

The representatives from both classes participating in the match faced each other in a location away from the academy.

Numerous drones were flying around, delivering the footage to the academy.

Giant images were projected in the air, and the Valkyries on the deck were looking up at them.

It was a system that allowed them to watch real-time footage captured from various angles.

Mio was watching the scene with great delight.

"Ahaha, the size difference is huge. Just from the visuals, it looks like the Class Two girls are the challengers, right? Hey, Ichikawa-chan?"

The person she addressed was Ichikawa Ichika, the homeroom teacher of Class Two.

With her voluminous long red hair tied back into a ponytail, she exuded an intimidating aura with her sharp gaze.

A woman with a well-toned, muscular body, her appearance evoked the term "warrior," and she was indeed a master who practiced martial arts.

She wore a flight jacket over her gi.

At the Third Academy, she trained female students as close combat specialists and had provided instruction to the students of Class Three several times.

Her personality was as strict as her appearance, and the female students she led in Class Two both feared and respected her.

She looked much more like a Valkyrie instructor than Mio.

"Kiyone is the strongest in close combat in our class. The challenger is clearly the toy from the Prometheus Project."

"Oh? Don't you like Thunderbolt? It's so cool~."

Mio's cheerful demeanor made Ichika uncomfortable.

"It's just a flashy toy."

"Come on! You guys are too harsh on men just because you don't like them. I think there's potential here. The idea of utilizing male combatants is quite exciting."

"What are you thinking by accepting something like that? You heard the pilot's story, right? Creating limbs and organs from Gijuu cells is insane. Even if there were compatibility, it's a plan that can only be described as madness."

Ichika shot a fierce glare at Mio, but she was focused on the two machines facing each other.

Not wanting to continue the conversation, Mio said, "We need to announce the start signal soon, or everyone will be confused."

"…Ah, you're right."

(I still can't figure out what that girl is thinking.)

Inside the cockpit of Thunderbolt, I faced Lieutenant Seiryuu through the monitor.

Lieutenant Seiryuu's Valkyrie Dress was also classified as Special Equipment, just like Lieutenant Hayase's.

Her pilot suit was distinct from the general Valkyries of Class Two, incorporating a unique design that gave it a special feel.

The most notable feature was the large shields connected to both shoulders.

Each shoulder had a large shield that seemed to house booster mechanisms on the back.

With four shields in total, it appeared to be specialized for defense, but the booster mechanisms hinted that it wasn't just for simple defense.

"Just as Lieutenant Hayase said."

As expected, Lieutenant Seiryuu's armaments had been changed from two gatling guns.

On her right arm was a small missile pod, and on her left arm was something that appeared to be a rifle… I had little information to make a judgment.

Looking at the structure, it seemed to be extendable?

From her back, a supplementary arm extended like a tail, holding two swords that were too long for a person to wield, secured in their sheaths.

My impression was of an armored warrior with pale navy blue large sleeves.

As I waited for the signal to start, Lieutenant Seiryuu began to speak.

She gestured with her hand to indicate a few numbers, and realizing they were frequencies, I adjusted the settings to open a communication line just between the two machines.

"You're slow, you sluggish one."

Her sudden insult brought back memories.

During my infantry days, being called a bug by the instructors during training was a daily occurrence.

Those harsh days filled with vulgar insults… However, I couldn't help but feel a sense of refinement in Lieutenant Seiryuu's insults, making them feel somewhat lacking.

"My apologies. So, why are we communicating privately?"

"Huh? Are you an idiot? Why would I want to have a private conversation with you?"

"Uh, no, I opened the communication line just between the two machines, so I thought—"

"Do you think I want to talk to a man like you? I opened the line because I had something I wanted to say to you."

Having been yelled at during training with commands to "shut up and answer 'yes' to all my questions!" I wasn't surprised by Lieutenant Seiryuu's yelling.

She glared at me through the monitor.

"I hate men. I detest them to the core. I can't stand your presence in this academy. Therefore, I've decided to drive you out."

The mention of "bug" made me react slightly, but it was already clear that Lieutenant Seiryuu disliked men.

"If I have offended you, I apologize, but I'm here on orders. I believe you lack the authority to expel me or the development team on your own."

It seemed Lieutenant Seiryuu understood that my point was valid, as she trembled slightly.

"I hate you."

It appeared that not only was I disliked as a man, but now I was also disliked for being right.

As I was about to apologize, the start signal from Teacher Ichikawa was announced.

"Then, let the first match of the rivalry begin."

As the buzzer signaling the start rang out, I moved Thunderbolt backward from its initial position while facing forward.

I fired the gatling gun fixed to my left arm to prevent her from closing the distance, but Lieutenant Seiryuu's reaction speed was even faster.

She immediately moved directly beneath me, attempting to slip under Thunderbolt and circle around to my back.

"Impressive speed!"

I felt no vibration when firing the large-caliber gatling gun, and despite the usual ease of maneuvering Thunderbolt, she easily escaped.

"You're slow, you sluggish one!"

As Lieutenant Seiryuu circled beneath me, she aimed the small missile pod held in her right sub-arm at me.

Small missiles recreated in the MR were scattered, launching an attack on Thunderbolt.

"Ugh!"

I took evasive action to escape the small missiles while simultaneously firing three missiles from the small missile containers mounted on both legs.

In total, six missiles were launched toward Lieutenant Seiryuu's Valkyrie Dress.

Both sides had low tracking capability for the small missiles, and if she tried to shake them off, she could evade them.

Since Lieutenant Seiryuu focused on dodging, her attention shifted away from Thunderbolt.

I opened the hatch for the large missile and fired one shot.

Its tracking ability and power were superior to the small missiles.

Even if I shot her down, she wouldn't escape the blast radius at close range.

When Lieutenant Seiryuu saw the large missile, she let out a small sigh.

"How naive."

I thought she would focus on evasion, but Lieutenant Seiryuu charged toward the incoming missile.

"Are you planning to take a direct hit!?"

Since it was a missile recreated in MR, even if she got caught in the explosion, her machine wouldn't take damage.

However, if I just brushed it off because it was MR, it wouldn't serve as training.

Lieutenant Seiryuu extended her arms to the sides, and a container appeared in the space ahead of her.

"Is that the subspace container I've heard about!?"

It was my first time seeing it in action.

One of the advantages of the Valkyries' Valkyrie Dress was that they could carry supply containers in subspace.

Normally, they stored weapons and ammunition there and could retrieve them when they ran low.

It was a function that enhanced their sustained combat capability.

What flew out of the container was a sword—she seemed to be avoiding using the tachi prepared on her back.

Gripping one in each hand, she quickly took a dual-wielding stance.

While I was surprised at what she was thinking, Lieutenant Seiryuu demonstrated her answer right in front of me.

"Kougetsu Soutenryuu… Nagare Mizukagami."

Was that the name of her style and technique? As her words reached my ears, the missiles were sliced apart as she passed by with her Valkyrie Dress.

The large missile didn't explode; it was cut and fell… and then the footage disappeared.

"Did she slice the sensor part while passing by!?"

I couldn't help but exclaim at the impossible sight, and Lieutenant Seiryuu flicked her sword.

"I've already checked your data. Your pathetic attacks won't work on me."

I had provided data for the MR recreation, so she must have checked it.

"I'm not saying it's unfair. It's a rule of combat to gather information on your opponent beforehand."

Lieutenant Seiryuu's opinion was correct.

It was correct, but there was a significant problem.

"You are right. It's not unfair; it's an effort to win. The issue is that sensitive information related to the Prometheus Project has been leaked. I will be lodging a formal protest with the academy."

"A toy doesn't have any secrets. Besides, armaments aren't classified."

"So that means you don't have complete information on Thunderbolt, then?"

"…Can't you speak without provoking me? This is why I hate men!"

"No, I'm just stating the facts—!?"

As Lieutenant Seiryuu accelerated and closed the distance, I aimed the gatling gun at her, but it was too late.

The Valkyrie Dress suddenly vanished from in front of me, and I had a bad feeling, prompting me to take evasive action.

However, the monitor displayed that the large drum magazine connected to my left shoulder hardpoint had been hit.

As a countermeasure for being hit, the large drum magazine was purged.

The number of rounds in the gatling gun decreased drastically.

I had no time to consider what had just happened, and Lieutenant Seiryuu's voice came through the communication line.

"You have good instincts. But you won't be able to dodge like that forever."

I tried to distance myself and escape with all my might, but Lieutenant Seiryuu's Valkyrie Dress was still pursuing me.

"Are you turning your back and running away? Hayase's chosen servant is nothing special! This is why men are truly hopeless. All they do is boast, and when it comes down to it, they run away—what a truly despicable creature!"

It seemed Lieutenant Seiryuu had a personal dislike for men, but I wondered if something had happened in her past.

I was considering what I could do in this situation.

"At that speed, I won't have time to aim with the large rifle. Using the large missile one by one is meaningless. My best course of action is—"

If there was any chance of winning in this situation… I decided to execute my plan.

"I'm going all out!"

I opened all the hatches of the large missile container on my right shoulder and pulled the triggers of the large rifles and gatling gun aimed at Lieutenant Seiryuu.

I also launched all missiles from the missile containers on my legs.

Nine large missiles were fired… It was a fighting style that couldn't be used in actual combat, but in this match where I didn't have to worry about magic power, it should be fine.

I unleashed a full barrage of firepower… It was a clumsy way to fight without considering the consequences, but it was the only way to catch Lieutenant Seiryuu's Valkyrie Dress in a wide net.

Lieutenant Seiryuu wore a bitter expression as she faced the rain of Thunderbolt's full attack.

"You've cornered yourself and taken a gamble. But from the start, you had no chance of winning against me."

Lieutenant Seiryuu evaded the full-on attack from Thunderbolt and sliced through the approaching missiles.

Some of the large missiles exploded when they got close without being dealt with.

Having exhausted all my ammunition, I was left with no weapons.

"I've fired all my rounds. I will now purge my armaments."

I purged the fixed missile containers and gatling gun, letting go of the large rifles.

The MR recreation of those disappeared, and I checked the remaining armaments.

Looking at Lieutenant Seiryuu, it seemed she had only received a slight hit from my full-on attack.

It appeared that even that attack couldn't bring her down.

"All that's left to rely on is this."

A short sword that had been stored behind my knee armor flew out, and Thunderbolt caught it.

By the time I took a dual-wielding stance with the short sword, Lieutenant Seiryuu had closed the distance to Thunderbolt.

Her two swords lunged at Thunderbolt.

I blocked each with my short sword, and Lieutenant Seiryuu's eyes widened.

"That movement… You learned somewhere, didn't you?"

"I taught myself. I picked it up in actual combat."

"Don't think you can match me with a makeshift dual-wielding technique!"

I had gathered that Lieutenant Seiryuu had a strong attachment to her sword from the prior information, but after seeing my dual-wielding with short swords, she seemed to want to compete.

Even though she had a large rifle and a small missile pod on her sub-arms, she didn't seem to want to use them.

Instead, she gave up on using her sub-arms, folding them behind her back to widen her range for swinging her swords.

It seemed that the sub-arms were in the way of her swordplay.

As I deflected her flowing series of strikes with my short swords, I felt cold sweat trickling down my back.

Though she attacked with a furious intensity, there was a beauty in her swordsmanship that flowed like water.

While my dual-wielding was a clumsy and rough display of brute force I had learned on my own, she clearly had accumulated technique.

It was no wonder that Lieutenant Seiryuu considered her strikes to be lethal.

That was precisely why I felt there was an opening to exploit.

"Your swordsmanship is beautiful."

"…Huh?"

For an instant, Lieutenant Seiryuu's movements faltered.

Whether she was tired or there was another reason, I couldn't tell.

But for me, it was an opportunity.

"I'll take advantage of that opening you've left."

Thunderbolt delivered an awkward kick toward Lieutenant Seiryuu.

Having been engaged in a sword duel until now, she was surprised and delayed in her response.

This was it. This was my chance to seize victory.

If I missed this opportunity, it would be over.

"You've become too fixated on swordsmanship, Lieutenant Seiryuu."

I pressed the short sword against the hardpoint on my waist, magnetically securing it, and grabbed Lieutenant Seiryuu's right arm with my free left hand.

"Wha—what the!?"

With the short sword, my legs, and Thunderbolt's mass… I closed the distance to Lieutenant Seiryuu and forced my way in.

Lieutenant Seiryuu tried to fend me off with the sword she held in her movable left hand, but she couldn't manage it.

My fighting style might be considered underhanded.

However, at this moment, I didn't know any other way to win.

Reona didn't believe that Kiyone would let it end here.

(She was chosen as an ace candidate. Show us one more push, Kiyone-chan.)

Kiyone felt a sense of urgency as she deflected Thunderbolt's fierce attacks with her sword.

(Don't mess with me… Don't mess with me, don't mess with me! There's no way I can lose. I absolutely cannot lose to a man. I cannot lose to men!)

Kiyone's past experiences before coming to the academy flashed back in her mind.

Kiyone was born in a traditional martial arts dojo, where they taught the Kougetsu Soutenryuu style.

It wasn't a single heir style; it was a school that opened its doors to gather students.

Of course, adults and local children were taught the Kougetsu Soutenryuu.

From a young age, Kiyone had learned the style under her father alongside her two older brothers.

However, Kiyone had the most enthusiasm of anyone.

From childhood, she swung her wooden sword with passion, finding joy in mastering the techniques of Kougetsu Soutenryuu.

While the other girls around her gradually became interested in various things, Kiyone remained focused solely on Kougetsu Soutenryuu.

The fact that Kougetsu Soutenryuu emphasized technique suited Kiyone perfectly.

Even though she was young and weak, there were many techniques to overcome brute strength, allowing her to avoid losing to stronger opponents.

Before she knew it, her skills surpassed those of her brothers, and her father recognized her as the best.

"That's amazing, Kiyone. To defeat your brothers, you're a genius."

She was delighted to be praised by her father.

That was why Kiyone held onto her dream.

"Um, Dad. I want to get stronger and inherit the dojo someday. And then, I'll become the strongest and promote the dojo. I'm sure we'll get a lot of students!"

Kiyone's dream was to inherit Kougetsu Soutenryuu and become its official successor.

Her father gently ruffled Kiyone's hair, not denying her dream.

"That sounds promising. I think Kiyone will make a fine successor."

"Yeah!"

Her father didn't deny Kiyone's dream—because he didn't believe she was serious.

As time passed and Kiyone reached the upper grades of elementary school, she learned that her two older brothers were preparing to inherit the dojo seriously.

While they were subjected to strict training at the dojo, nothing changed for Kiyone.

Watching her brothers, who lacked talent compared to her, receive harsh guidance from their father and improve made her sad.

So Kiyone spoke to her father.

"Wasn't I supposed to inherit the dojo!? You said I was stronger than my brothers and worthy of being the successor!"

It seemed he was choosing his words carefully, unsure how to decline.

"I didn't think Kiyone was serious about it. Besides, if it's just about honing your skills, there's no need to inherit the dojo, right?"

"No! I want to inherit the dojo! I want to be the head of the Kogetsu Sōten-ryū!"

She wanted to carry on the Kogetsu Sōten-ryū and develop it further to become the strongest.

She wanted the name of the school to resonate far and wide.

Yet, the successors chosen were her brothers, who were inferior to her.

Her brothers, aware of their lack of talent, said nothing in response to Kiyone.

Her father said to her, "You have many other possibilities. There's no need to be bound by the school or the dojo."

It sounded as if he wanted her to distance herself from the Kogetsu Sōten-ryū...

(I have nothing else... I don't want anything else...)

To Kiyone, it felt like a betrayal.

And then, she arrived at a certain answer.

"Is it because I'm a girl? Is that why I can't be the successor?"

"...No. That's not it."

Her father said this with a pained expression, but Kiyone could tell he was lying.

(Because I'm not a boy, I can't inherit the school. There's nothing I can do about that...)

She felt she had no choice but to give up her dream over something she couldn't change, and it frustrated her to no end.

However, a turning point came for Kiyone.

Around the time she was about to enter middle school, a mass aptitude test for Valkyries was held.

There, her aptitude was recognized, and her admission to the Third Academy was decided.

Valkyries... the universally acknowledged protectors of humanity became something special for Kiyone.

(Even if I can't inherit the school, I have the position of a Valkyrie. No, the affairs of the lower world don't matter. No matter what anyone says, I am the rightful successor of the Kogetsu Sōten-ryū... If I become a Valkyrie and achieve results that everyone acknowledges, it won't matter if my father denies it. My opinions will be recognized.)

Kiyone demonstrated not only her talent in ancient martial arts but also high potential as a Valkyrie.

She was even called an ace candidate.

Yet, now Kiyone was on the verge of defeat.

Against the man she had sworn never to lose to again.

(I won't lose. I don't want to lose. There's no way I can lose here!)

In an instant, she recalled her painful past and wished strongly not to lose.

"Don't get carried away, you toy!!"

The folded sub-arm began to move, aiming its gun at Thunderbolt in response to Kiyone's will.

Attached to the left sub-arm was her prized new weapon.

An electromagnetic launcher—a weapon that projected elongated stakes using electromagnetic force, becoming a lethal weapon due to its firing speed and power.

The barrel extended, generating electromagnetic force within the MR, causing a discharge phenomenon.

Kiyone named it "Tonbo Giri."

It was named after a spear favored by a famous Warring States general.

Originally, it was intended for use against large Gijuu that couldn't close the distance, but with her determination not to lose, she pressed the gun's muzzle against Thunderbolt.

If it hit, a downed judgment was guaranteed.

Or so she thought.

"Warning: Handle with care."
