Chapter 3: Valkyrie's Day Off

"Don't you have any casual clothes?"

"I'm sorry. I didn't anticipate needing them."

During my time as an infantryman, I was trained to minimize my belongings, and in my case, I usually got by with military uniforms or training gear, so I didn't own any casual clothes for going out.

"Still, you should at least have one set of casual clothes."

"...I'll be more careful from now on."

Lieutenant Hayase scolded me, but the truth is, I was involved in a top-secret project.

At first, I wasn't even informed about the details of the base where the project was taking place, so I never thought I would be able to go out freely while being part of such a secretive plan.

My right arm and left leg... and even my organs and left eye were made from cells regenerated from Gijuu.

In other words, I was a top-secret existence myself, so I had unconsciously assumed that situations requiring casual clothes would never arise.

"By the way, Lieutenant Hayase, what are we waiting for?"

The place Lieutenant Hayase brought me to was a small boarding area that seemed too small for a train station.

It was larger than a bus stop, and looking at the ground, I could see tracks laid down.

As I looked around in confusion, Lieutenant Hayase wore a surprised expression.

"You've never seen a streetcar before?"

"No, I haven't."

I answered immediately, and Lieutenant Hayase looked troubled as she explained.

"Just think of it as a small train. Our academy is quite large, right? We can't just walk everywhere, so they apparently set this up as a means of transportation."

The academy's true identity was a fortress floating in the sky.

It was easy to forget, but we were currently above the clouds.

Lieutenant Hayase turned her face toward the sound of a high-pitched noise.

Chirin, chirin... what approached was indeed a vehicle that looked like a shrunken train.

"Here it comes."

The streetcar was a single-car unit, and when we boarded, there were only three other passengers.

It had cantilevered seating along the sides.

Lieutenant Hayase sat down and urged me to sit next to her.

Just as I took my seat, the streetcar began to move.

"By the way, Lieutenant Hayase, where are we headed?"

I hadn't been informed about our destination.

Lieutenant Hayase looked up at the ceiling, seemingly contemplating where to go.

"Shall we play or go shopping? If you had shown a bit more interest in going out earlier, you could have applied for permission to go down to the surface. ...Well, that’s if permission was granted."

"To the surface? If the application is approved, we can go outside? That's just like when I was an infantryman."

During holidays, almost everyone except me and Shy Boy would obtain permission to go out.

We had been invited out a few times, but I struggled to keep up with everyone’s vibe.

"I think it’s tougher than you imagine. There are guards assigned, and there are many restrictions. A lot of kids got tired of it and stopped going out."

"Guards? Not escorts, then?"

"You think we would lose to regular civilians in a fight?"

Valkyries were the only beings capable of fighting Gijuu head-on.

They manipulated magical power and fought using specialized weapons called Valkyrie Dresses.

There was no doubt they were strong.

That was true even in their physical forms.

I had once trained in hand-to-hand combat with a Valkyrie candidate, and I was astonished by the strength of my opponent, who was slimmer and smaller than me.

I couldn't imagine them falling behind regular civilians in terms of strength.

However, there were countless ways to approach a situation, so I pointed out that we shouldn't let our guard down.

"...Still, I think an escort would be necessary."

"You’re as serious as ever. Anyway, it’s purely for surveillance. Adults are keeping an eye out because they’d be troubled if we were to play with the opposite sex. Even when I went home, they barged into my house, and it was a hassle."

"Improper relations with the opposite sex..."

"Exactly."

The academy director had said before that such things were absolutely unacceptable.

In that case, I wondered if situations like this one would be permitted.

To begin with, I didn't fully understand the scope of what constituted improper relations with the opposite sex.

It was fine to interact daily, but would it be acceptable to have a relationship where we went out together on holidays?

My anxiety began to swell.

I realized that the current situation might fall under the category of what the academy director was concerned about.

"Lieutenant Hayase!"

"What is it?"

"Is this situation considered improper relations with the opposite sex? The academy director only approved it for socializing, so I think it would be better to confirm this once."

I was feeling frantic.

I feared that this incident might anger the academy director and lead to the expulsion of the Prometheus Project development team.

However, contrary to my tense demeanor, Lieutenant Hayase burst into laughter, clutching her stomach.

She was flailing her legs about.

"You were that foolish? No wonder I understand your callsign now."

"Huh? No, but..."

As the streetcar came to a stop, Lieutenant Hayase stood up.

"Anyway, don't worry about it. Just follow me. The academy director won't be angry, so don't fret."

"Is that so?"

"To begin with, improper relations with the opposite sex are sexual in nature... No, never mind. Why should I have to explain this to you, an older person? It would definitely feel like a punishment game."

I followed Lieutenant Hayase as she got off the streetcar, asking her to enlighten me on the details.

"I would appreciate it if you could teach me for future reference?"

"You really have no common sense, do you?"

Lieutenant Hayase, looking exasperated, pointed with her thumb at a large building visible from the stop.

"Let’s have some fun for now. Wasting a holiday just on training is extremely unhealthy."

"What kind of facility is this?"

"An indoor leisure facility. Recently, I've been spending a lot of time at my desk, and today I just want to have a good time."

Lieutenant Hayase, who had said she wanted to play, stretched right there.

She was wearing black skinny jeans, indicating she had planned to move her body from the start.

She seemed to be having fun.

"You should join me. Oh, this is a command from your superior."

Her insistence on joining reminded me of my former squad leader.

He often took me out under the pretext of a command, worrying about my reluctance to socialize.

Feeling nostalgic, I didn't mind at all.

"I'll accompany you, Lieutenant Hayase."

"Oh, you smiled. So you can smile too."

It seemed I had unconsciously smiled.

I hurriedly touched my face with my hand to check, then corrected my expression.

"My apologies."

"At least smile on your day off. You must get tired of always having a serious face."

◇

Led by Lieutenant Hayase, we arrived at a corner filled with game machines.

Music and voices flowed from the machines, overlapping to create a unique cacophony.

Various genres of game machines were set up in the spacious indoor area.

It was more fulfilling than the game center I used to visit as a child.

However, the few customers present gave it a lonely impression.

"There aren't many people here."

"Of course. It's a weekday."

Lieutenant Hayase was seriously selecting a game machine to play, and when she found one she liked, she pulled my arm.

"Let's go with that one."

"This is... a gun shooting game?"

What she brought me to was a gun shooting game machine with a large screen.

After Lieutenant Hayase scanned her card to pay, she handed me one of the gun-shaped controllers.

"Have you played this before?"

"Just a little when I was a kid..."

"That should be enough."

The countdown began on the large screen, and Lieutenant Hayase faced forward, ready.

I looked at the instructions posted on the game machine to check how to reload.

It seemed to be the type that would reload automatically once I ran out of bullets, but during that time, I couldn't attack.

I checked the number of bullets, the weight of the gun-shaped controller, and the optimal stance... I would learn as I went along.

When the countdown finished, Lieutenant Hayase called out to me.

"This is my first time playing two-player, but if we're doing this, we’re aiming for a high score. If we get taken out easily, you’ll have to face a punishment game."

"I'll do my best."

As I said this, I focused on the approaching enemies... were they zombies? I began shooting.

The toy gun's trigger was surprisingly light.

I pulled the trigger with just the right amount of force to avoid breaking it, but next to me, Lieutenant Hayase was firing away rapidly, making me anxious.

"Hey! I'm not the only one scoring here! If you don't pull your weight, it’s going to be a bigger burden on me!"

Watching how Lieutenant Hayase played, I noticed she only used one bullet for weak enemies.

I had been taught to finish them off with at least two shots, so my method seemed inefficient.

"...Understood. From here on, I will meet your expectations."

The most damaging area for the enemies that appeared on the screen was their heads.

I aimed for the head and pulled the trigger, then set my sights on the next target.

The area I was aiming at was displayed on the screen, and since it hit accurately without any interference, I didn't even need to confirm if I had taken them down.

Scores were displayed separately for each player, and my score was starting to catch up to Lieutenant Hayase's.

"Wait!? You suddenly moved like a different person!"

"I'm adjusting and aiming for optimal responses."

"Do you even understand that this is a game? Oh, while I'm saying that..."

On the screen, the characters controlled by the players progressed into an area where a boss awaited.

The boss, large and grotesque in appearance, appeared, so I immediately aimed for its head and began attacking.

However, I didn't expect it to go down with just one or two shots, and I felt like I wasn't dealing effective damage.

"...Isn't it too tough?"

As I questioned the boss's strength, Lieutenant Hayase cheerfully explained next to me.

"This one has weak knees."

With that, Lieutenant Hayase began shooting at the moving boss's knees.

Her shots were more effective than mine aimed at the head, and the boss was visibly pained, slowing its movements.

I followed her lead and aimed for the knees as well.

"You seem to know a lot about this game."

I intended it to be a casual conversation.

"I play here every time I come."

"I see."

She often played alone, it seemed.

I spent the rest of the time at the indoor leisure facility with Lieutenant Hayase.

◇

After separating from Lieutenant Hayase to take care of my needs, I noticed a woman in the game corner wearing a deep hat and mask, who was keeping a low profile due to the sparse crowd.

Dressed in a casual suit, she might be affiliated with the academy?

Her vigilance about her surroundings was concerning.

She was even wearing sunglasses to hide her face, and I could tell at a glance that she had a well-trained physique, even with her clothes on.

Her movements suggested she had learned some form of combat skills.

From the back of her hat, a thick mane of hair spilled out.

She had an unusual hair color, so while there was a possibility she was connected to the academy...

For a moment, I considered reporting her as a suspicious person, but then she began to take out a device and started talking to someone while hiding in the shadows.

Not carrying any weapons, I approached quietly to eavesdrop without being noticed.

At first, I was on guard, but upon hearing her voice, I relaxed.

"Mom is working hard to get the doll you wanted, Muscotan. It was tough to find time between work. You better praise me!"

The tall, muscular woman seemed to be talking to someone who sounded like a young child through the device.

"—Yeah. Yeah. I'm sorry. Mom can't come home right away because of work. But I think I can come back next month, so be a good boy and wait with Dad. —Yeah, it's a promise."

It seemed she was on a solo assignment at the academy.

It appeared she had come to the game center to get a doll for her child.

This made me realize that various people worked at this academy.

The woman seemed to be coaxed into doing something by the child.

"Do you want to come visit Mom's workplace? No, no! Absolutely not! This is a place where cute kids like Muscotan can't come! There are lots of scary people at Mom's workplace! Mom is worried sick about you—No, it's not that. I want to live with you too, but the workplace is absolutely off-limits!"

Being separated from her family while on a solo assignment seemed quite tough.

Turning my back to the woman's almost tearful voice, I decided there was no need to report her and quietly left the area.

◇

Around noon, Suzuki Megumi arrived at the indoor leisure facility and headed straight for the game machine she had her eye on without glancing around.

Her target was a punching machine, but since it was a game center used by Valkyries, it was sturdier than those meant for the general public.

After roughly slamming her card onto the punching machine to pay, Megumi put on the provided gloves and got into position.

She wound up her fist and imagined the face of someone who annoyed her before striking.

"That damn woman!"

As she struck the pad, it shook violently and moved backward, calculating her punching power.

The score displayed was one hundred eighty-nine points.

While she had set a new personal best, looking at the rankings showed that many others had higher scores, so Megumi couldn't feel happy about it.

"Ugh, I'm so annoyed. I finally became a real Valkyrie, and now I'm stuck with that woman for training. I wish I had a more caring senpai."

The person Megumi was frustrated with was Maya.

She had been assigned to Class Three, but she never expected to be left alone for an entire week just because it was a holiday.

"Seriously, the teachers are ridiculous too. They said it was fine to leave it to Maya-chan, but I'm definitely not fine with it!"

Megumi was anxious for a reason.

While it was a joy for candidates to become real Valkyries, it also came with the obligation to participate in actual combat.

Unlike the battles they had participated in as candidates, they would now face life-and-death struggles against first-class Gijuu and above.

With anxiety and impatience, her desire to become a full-fledged Valkyrie as soon as possible grew stronger.

Yet, Maya wasn't providing any training for the newcomers.

It was understandable for Megumi to feel like she had drawn the short straw.

"How can someone like that be the ace of Class Three... huh?"

As Megumi turned her face toward something that caught her attention, she spotted a couple in the indoor leisure facility, which should have been empty on a weekday.

When a man and woman acted together in the academy, it was already eye-catching, but on top of that, the man was in military uniform, and she recognized him.

"Hifumi? And the other one is... Hayase!?"

Megumi felt like she had seen something she shouldn't have and hurriedly hid behind a nearby object.

Since they were in casual clothes, it took her a moment to recognize that the person next to Ren was indeed Maya.

The two passed right by where Megumi was hiding.

Unlike in the classroom, Maya seemed to be in a good mood.

"I'm hungry. Shall we go eat now?"

"Understood."

The content of their conversation wasn't much different from what Megumi had seen before, but what she found problematic was that Maya, who usually had a sulky demeanor, was smiling.

Once they were gone, Megumi seemed to find a weakness of Maya's and wore a mischievous smile.

"I've found the ace's weakness."

◇

"Ah, that was fun! I can't believe we played until after two o'clock. When was the last time I forgot about lunch while playing?"

The indoor leisure facility had many dining options.

Among them, Lieutenant Hayase chose a Japanese restaurant.

The interior had a calm atmosphere with wooden furniture, and we were the only customers.

While the lack of other patrons was concerning, it might have been understandable given the time of day.

More importantly, I was troubled by Lieutenant Hayase.

"Lieutenant Hayase, you don't have to treat me again. I have my salary deposited, and since I rarely have the opportunity to spend it, I'm fine."

Lieutenant Hayase intended to treat me to lunch.

Additionally, since the academy was located in a floating fortress, the transportation costs seemed to inflate the prices at every restaurant.

It felt like she was more focused on the fact that I was older than her rather than the fact that I was a special subordinate.

"Don't worry about it. It's not like a subordinate or servant should be concerned about such things. Besides, I'm the senior here."

"Considering you paid for all the games earlier, I think it's fine for me to cover this meal."

Each game cost a few hundred yen, but after playing for hours, it added up to a considerable amount.

So, I felt it would be better for me to pay for this meal.

Lieutenant Hayase seemed displeased by my insistence and frowned.

"Do you dislike being treated by me? Or are you unhappy because today wasn't fun?"

"N-No, that's not it. It was a good change of pace, and it brought back nostalgic feelings."

"Oh, is that so? Then there's no problem. Feel free to order whatever you like without worrying about the price."

Lieutenant Hayase displayed a generous attitude toward money, but of course, I knew that her salary as a Valkyrie was incomparable to ours as infantrymen.

I understood that, but I couldn't shake the feeling that relying on her too much was unhealthy.

"However..."

Seeing my hesitance, Lieutenant Hayase sighed deeply before speaking.

"When a servant treats me, it makes me feel miserable, you know? Do you enjoy humiliating your commander?"

I had been deceived by Lieutenant Hayase's appearance and had forgotten something important.

I had underestimated her dignity as a commander.

"Ah! My apologies. Then I will gladly accept your offer."

"You’re still so stiff. Well, maybe it’s better for you to be like that."

With that, Lieutenant Hayase picked up the tablet on the table and quickly selected what she wanted to eat before handing it to me.

She ordered a set meal.

Looking at the price, it seemed quite inflated, but bringing up the cost here would only reopen the previous discussion.

I operated the tablet, seriously considering my options.

"...I've decided."

"You took quite a while to decide. So, what did you choose? ...Huh?"

Lieutenant Hayase froze for a moment when she saw what I had selected.

She quickly turned her face toward me.

"Are you serious?"

"Yes. I would like that, please."

"But this is..."

It seemed I had perplexed Lieutenant Hayase.

I explained why I had chosen it.

"I'm not being modest or anything. I just felt a strong desire to eat it at first glance. Moreover, it's a limited-time menu at this restaurant, so I don't know when I might get to eat it again."

I earnestly conveyed my feelings, but Lieutenant Hayase didn't seem convinced.

"But a parfait for lunch? What is that!?"

"No, what I chose is the seasonal fruit and shiratama anmitsu parfait. While I was also tempted by the standard parfait, I prioritized the limited-time option."

"Both are basically the same!"

"They're different. The standard one is a simple parfait with just shiratama anmitsu."

It was obvious from the pictures.

Personally, I wanted to tackle the standard parfait first, but if I missed this opportunity, I might not get to eat the limited-time parfait again.

I concluded that I should change my basic strategy and focus solely on the seasonal fruit and shiratama anmitsu parfait.

Even so, Lieutenant Hayase still didn't seem convinced.

"Just eat a proper meal. It's unhealthy!"

"There's no need to worry. I cover my necessary nutrients with breakfast and dinner. As for the calories, I can manage them through training, so please don't worry."

If I exceeded the necessary calorie intake, it might not be the most efficient method, but I could burn it off through exercise.

It might not be rational, but that parfait held that much value for me.

Seeing that I wouldn't budge, Lieutenant Hayase seemed to give up.

"...I said you could order whatever you wanted, so I won't say anything more. But don't do the same thing in front of others."

"Understood. I will confirm in advance from now on."

"Why is that the only thing you won't give in on?"

◇

In the evening.

Lieutenant Hayase seemed satisfied as we left the indoor leisure facility and headed back.

On the way back along the route we had taken to get there, Lieutenant Hayase said she wanted to make one more stop.

"Just a little longer, okay?"

"That's fine, but where are we headed?"

"To a park."

The place Lieutenant Hayase led me to was a park on a hill overlooking the academy.

The view from the park made it clear why the academy was referred to as a floating fortress.

Buildings crowded together on top of this fortress floating in the sky.

As I took in the scenery, Lieutenant Hayase spoke up.

"Isn't it a terrible sight? They keep expanding it, thinking they can just add more if it’s not enough, so it ended up looking all haphazard. Well, personally, I don't dislike it."

Beyond the fortress, white clouds spread out, dyed orange by the setting sun.

The clouds didn’t enter the fortress because they were protected by a thin shield.

"I don't dislike it either."

"Oh, that's good to hear."

Lieutenant Hayase seemed a bit pleased.

Seeing her like that, I decided to ask the questions that had been on my mind.

"Lieutenant Hayase... why did you take me on as your subordinate? Moreover, why did you pick me up as a special servant?"

A servant—that was a special system allowed for Valkyries.

Since their emotions influenced their combat, many systems were created that differed from the military.

One of those was the special subordinate position of a servant.

Valkyries who were squad leaders had the right to have dedicated subordinates.

They could recruit candidates from Class Five, ignoring the opinions of the class.

This was apparently to strengthen their squad by pulling in candidates they felt had good compatibility.

Once chosen as a servant, the Valkyrie would generally work as a subordinate to the Valkyrie who recruited them until they were released or became independent.

Lieutenant Hayase looked down at the academy as she spoke.

"I felt like I could work well with you. It was just a gut feeling."

It seemed like a rather vague reason to choose a subordinate, but I couldn't dismiss gut feelings since they could be quite reliable.

In my former squad, there was a gambler with excellent intuition.

I had witnessed how he saved the squad multiple times, so I didn't underestimate intuition.

"You're the type with sharp instincts, then?"

"Who knows? But if I make a mistake, at least I can accept it if it was based on my own intuition."

I understood that Lieutenant Hayase had her reasons for choosing me.

However, I also thought there were issues with her attitude toward the other three.

"...Did you judge that those three wouldn't be able to work well with you intuitively?"

It seemed Lieutenant Hayase understood immediately who I was referring to.

And she seemed to care that I was concerned about them.

"Did someone say something to you?"

"No, it's just that I was curious about your attitude toward them."

Lieutenant Hayase didn't even try to look at me.

"That's unexpected. I thought you were the type who didn't care about those around you."

Considering my usual behavior, it would be hard to say I was attentive to my surroundings.

I didn't even think of myself as someone who cared about others.

"Indeed, I often hear that I'm not very considerate. But..."

...I had been told before.

"My former squad leader said that creating a good environment for the squad was also part of my job. He often encouraged me to speak up to those who were struggling to fit in."

It was the squad leader's job to gather the infantry assigned by orders and fight on the battlefield.

To increase the chances of survival even slightly, he had always looked out for each individual.

Lieutenant Hayase seemed to understand what I was trying to say.

"In other words, you want me to manage and guide those girls well, right?"

"...Yes. I believe you could do it, Lieutenant Hayase."

In the current situation, even if a squad were formed, it was highly unlikely to function well.

Though I was a novice in Valkyrie combat, my opinion would only be that of an amateur, yet there should be commonalities across all fields.

And it was clear to anyone that the current situation was problematic.

Lieutenant Hayase turned her body to face me and looked me in the eye.

"You sound just like everyone else. Telling me to be more considerate of my subordinates or to think about their feelings. But that's wrong."

"...Huh?"

"The ones who should be considerate are those three. After all, I’m the one in a position to choose, while they are in a position to be chosen. They should be the ones to show how they can contribute to me."

According to Lieutenant Hayase, it seemed those three were the ones in the wrong.

While that opinion had some merit, her thinking was rather self-centered.

What could they do for the ace of Class Three? What could they provide? How could they contribute?

She seemed to be expecting answers from the newly hired candidates who had just been promoted from being mere candidates.

That felt excessively harsh.

"I understand Lieutenant Hayase's perspective. Still, I think you should have explained that to them at the time."

Perhaps finding my persistence annoying, Lieutenant Hayase furrowed her brow.

"Those girls are Valkyries the moment they are assigned to Class Three, even if they are new. They should be able to think and act for themselves."

"That's certainly true, but..."

As I struggled to find words to persuade her, Lieutenant Hayase began to speak.

"At the very least, that's how I've operated. What do I lack? What do I need? I've been thinking and acting on those questions, which is why I am where I am now. You’re the same, right?"

"...Huh?"

I was a bit taken aback when she said I was the same as her.

If anything, I was the type to move according to instructions.

I had been trained to follow orders, so I struggled with acting independently.

Seeing my confusion, Lieutenant Hayase let out a small sigh.

"You had a training menu in your room, right? It had a program set up to build your basics for the future. That’s enough for you right now. After all, you’re still in a trial phase with the experimental machine, and I can’t tell you what to do either. —But those three are different."

Lieutenant Hayase had not overlooked the complacency of those three.

"Once assigned to Class Three, they can access my data. If they understand my armament and fighting style, they should be able to figure out how they can contribute, right? I never expected to have all the answers from the start. But those girls came unprepared."

"..."

When I fell silent, Lieutenant Hayase began to talk about the upcoming break.

"If they at least do that much by the time the break is over, that’s a passing grade. If they can’t even realize that, then they were just candidates to begin with."

While it seemed a bit harsh, they were Valkyries—people on the chosen side.

In other words, they were elite.

They were expected to deliver results.

However, if they could only manage to scrape by with the bare minimum, I couldn't help but wonder where the passing mark lay.

"Is that the passing mark? I'm curious about what it takes to actually pass."

Lieutenant Hayase's expression changed.

It turned into one of interest, and while she seemed expectant... it was also a combative smile.

"If you can say you’ll defeat me and become the ace, that would be a passing mark."

Only those with the ambition to defeat the ace of Class Three would receive a passing mark in that situation.

In other words, I was merely scraping by.
