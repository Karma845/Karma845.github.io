Chapter 14: Hayase Maya

The Second and Third Academies, having successfully completed their mission to destroy the medium-sized gate, were granted a holiday considering the circumstances. The ones who drew the short straw this time were the Fourth Academy, which had been put on standby for an urgent deployment. There were complaints from the Fourth Academy, but in the end, they accepted their standby status—at least that’s what Maya had heard. The actual situation was unclear, but since Maya had little interaction with the Fourth Academy, she had no way to confirm it.

It was during this holiday that Maya was called out. The one who summoned her was the class president, Mai. When Maya arrived at the classroom, she found Mai alone, typing a report on her laptop.

Mai faced the screen of her laptop, but directed her gaze toward Maya as she entered. "Sorry for calling you out on your day off."

"You say that as if you actually mean it. So, what do you need today?" Maya asked, taking a seat next to Mai and inquiring about the reason for her summons.

Mai smiled wryly. "Since I entrusted you with the training of the newcomers, it’s only natural for me to check on their progress, right? They’ve been in actual combat, after all. I’d like to hear your opinion."

What Mai was seeking was Maya's evaluation of whether the newcomers were capable. She likely intended to use Maya's insights as a basis for her judgment. Maya assessed the newcomers at this point.

"Basically, they lack ability. If they weren’t in the Third Academy, I think they’d be considered ineffective."

The newcomers had trained in the Fifth Academy as well, but from the perspective of a combat unit, they were still lacking. Mai agreed with this assessment.

"There are some less capable kids in the Fifth Academy, so perhaps the training content is insufficient when considering the overall balance. Well, the talented ones will still improve in the same situation, so it should be enough as it is."

"I think they should increase the training time, though."

"…Wouldn’t that just lead to more kids running away? It’s better for those who are self-aware and can push themselves to rise to the top."

Thinking the conversation had strayed into casual chatter, Mai continued writing her report while asking, "Can you give me individual evaluations?"

Maya stated her evaluations of the newcomers without looking at any materials. "The most interesting one is Nishitani Kanako. Is her personality change during combat a form of self-suggestion? I appreciate her decisiveness. However, if she doesn’t use her head a bit more in battle, she won’t survive. She’d do well under someone smart."

"…What about Asai Mami? I personally rate her the highest."

"She’s managing to get by, but her actual ability is the lowest. She seems to have moderate command skills, so she could probably become a squad leader. However, she simply lacks strength as an individual."

Mai turned her face from the screen to look at Maya. "That’s surprising. I thought you were indifferent, but you’ve been paying attention."

"I even requested materials to research, and I’ve put together a proper training plan."

Mai looked astonished to see this unexpected side of Maya. "So you’ve been properly instructing them. Though it seems that your efforts didn’t reach those being instructed."

Maya hadn’t intended to convey her feelings. Mai returned to the topic at hand.

"…I don’t think we’ll hire her, but could you give me Suzuki Megumi’s evaluation while we’re at it?"

Megumi had committed an act of betrayal against Maya, so her evaluation among the Third Academy was the lowest. Maya had heard about this but didn’t let it affect her judgment.

"Her strong-willed behavior stands out, but she’s actually cowardly. It seems she was causing a ruckus against second-class opponents while in the Fifth Academy, but in actual combat, she was heavily guarded with a rifle."

Maya had seen through Megumi’s cowardice. Mai sighed softly.

"A traitor and a coward. Even with a shortage of personnel, there’s no way we’d hire her."

---

As evening fell, while walking around the academy grounds, Maya spotted Suzuki sitting on a bench in the courtyard, looking dejected. Worried by her drooping posture, Maya approached her.

"What’s wrong?"

Suzuki looked up, her face clearly showing her displeasure. "Are you Hayase’s favorite or something?"

While it might not be entirely inaccurate, it seemed that Lieutenant Hayase’s interest in Maya was more about her skills with the robots.

"I can’t confirm whether I’m a favorite without asking Lieutenant Hayase."

"That’s a pretty uninteresting answer."

Since Suzuki fell silent, Maya did too. The first to break the silence was Suzuki, who looked at Maya with irritation.

"What’s your deal?"

"You seemed really down, so I thought I’d ask why."

If her guess was correct, perhaps she could help resolve the issue. However, it wasn’t going smoothly. After a deep sigh, Suzuki explained her situation.

"…I took a picture of you and Hayase having fun and shared it with others. Then they said they didn’t like traitors. I tried to make up for it in my own way, but my treatment hasn’t changed after going into actual combat. I’ll probably just be sent back to the Fifth Academy."

"You were feeling down about being sent back to the Fifth Academy, huh?"

"You wouldn’t understand, but being abandoned by the Third Academy is the same as being useless. I’ll just be left to rot. If I’m lucky, I might get assigned to the Sabat team… I’ve fallen off the elite course."

Maya realized she was out of touch with the Valkyries' circumstances. Still, she felt that Suzuki had a chance left.

"Why don’t you try talking to Lieutenant Hayase?"

"With her? She doesn’t care about us. She’s nice to you, but she hardly pays us any mind."

Even so, Maya believed Suzuki should talk to Lieutenant Hayase. There had been a moment on the battlefield when she felt a connection with Hayase. She felt she could understand Hayase’s thoughts then. If that wasn’t just a hallucination, there should still be a chance.

"I’m scheduled to be called out by Lieutenant Hayase and head to the outdoor basketball court. Why don’t you talk to her there?"

Hearing Maya’s suggestion, Suzuki retorted, "So I’m stuck with you even on my day off? It must be nice to be favored. I’m really envious."

The last word, "envious," sounded like a genuine expression from Suzuki.

---

When they arrived at the basketball court, Lieutenant Hayase noticed Suzuki. She seemed to understand what Maya was thinking and wore an exasperated expression.

"Why does my attendant always do unnecessary things?"

"I apologize. I just thought it would be better for you two to talk."

Seeing Suzuki looking anxious, Lieutenant Hayase passed her the basketball. Suzuki received it, her eyes wide with surprise. Lieutenant Hayase invited Suzuki onto the court.

"…Alright. Suzuki, you’re in the basketball club, right? Let’s play."

"Eh?"

Once Suzuki entered the court, the two began their match. Lieutenant Hayase quickly scored a goal, and then it was Suzuki’s turn to attack. As the match continued, Suzuki suddenly stopped moving.

"…I betrayed you. I took a picture of you with Hifumiduring the holiday and showed it to Sorakujima."

Lieutenant Hayase didn’t seem interested in Suzuki’s apology.

"Oh, is that so? I don’t care, so it’s your turn to attack next."

"Wait!? You don’t care… I betrayed you!"

Lieutenant Hayase let out a small sigh at Suzuki’s outburst.

"I heard about you from Nishitani and Asai beforehand."

Suzuki’s eyes widened. "You knew… Then why didn’t you kick me out?"

She must have thought she would be expelled the moment her betrayal was discovered. Lieutenant Hayase took the ball from Suzuki and began practicing her shots.

"Did you think my position would waver over something like that? If you were shooting me in the back on the battlefield, that would be a different story, but if I expelled you over something like this, it would be endless."

Lieutenant Hayase spoke to Suzuki. "Today, Sorakujima asked if we were going to keep you three in the Third Academy."

"!? "

Suzuki, thinking she would be sent back to the Fifth Academy, looked down, tears welling in her eyes. However, Lieutenant Hayase continued.

"I told them to keep all three of you. Your formal hiring was decided on the spot."

"…Huh? W-Why? I betrayed you!"

Confused and unable to comprehend, Suzuki listened as Lieutenant Hayase explained why she hadn’t expelled her.

"You’ve been doing your best in training, even if you’re not capable, and you changed your combat style in actual combat, right? You threw away your strange pride and fought with a focus on defense, didn’t you?"

Suzuki, who had previously been aggressive in her style, seemed embarrassed to have prioritized defense and looked down.

"…I was scared. I didn’t have the courage to step forward and fight. So I thought I was worthless."

"That’s perfectly fine."

"Huh?"

Maya picked up the ball and threw it to Lieutenant Hayase. She resumed her shooting practice.

"If you had prioritized your strange pride, I would have sent you back to the Fifth Academy. Even if you survived a few times, you would eventually lose your life. But you thought for yourself and made the decision to survive… well, that’s a passing grade."

"S-So then!"

Tears of joy streamed down Suzuki’s face as Lieutenant Hayase turned and smiled at her.

"Welcome to the Third Academy. You’ll probably regret many times that it would have been better to stay in the Fifth Academy, but I welcome you. Make sure to do your best."

"Y-Yes!"

---

After enjoying basketball with Suzuki, Lieutenant Hayase dismissed everyone and took Maya for a walk. They headed toward a plaza overlooking the academy.

"Suzuki seemed happy."

"I’m not sure why I had to convey that to her when she’ll find out after the holiday."

Lieutenant Hayase had likely been considering the girls in her own way. However, it seemed her clumsy approach had caused misunderstandings. Remembering how she had helped Maya before, he thought the same was true then.

"Is there meaning in you conveying it?"

"…Are you telling me to be normal?"

As the squad leader, she wanted to be normal, but she understood that it was impossible. Lieutenant Hayase was competent, cheeky, and—kind.

"No, I don’t think you can be normal, so I was thinking it would be good if you could get close to normal."

After Hayase widened her eyes in surprise, she lightly kicked Maya’s leg.

"You really do say too much unnecessary stuff."

"I apologize."

"Well, if you’re going to be my partner, that much is probably fine."

Lieutenant Hayase turned her body toward Maya. The sunset illuminated her face, making her expression serious.

"I will stand at the top of the Third Academy—no, the top of this academy—in my own way. What I need for that are partners and comrades, not subordinates. HifumiRen… do you intend to be my partner?"

"Partner, you say? Is that different from being a servant?"

Being told to be a servant one moment and a partner the next… there must be a clear distinction in her mind.

"It’s different. I want someone I can respect as an equal. In that regard, you’re passing. Above all, the most important thing is that you’re piloting a robot."

"…Is that a condition for being a partner?"

Lieutenant Hayase broke into a smile.

"I won’t deny it. But don’t you think it’s necessary to have aspects of humanity that are respectable? I acknowledge you, after all. You volunteered for a ridiculous experiment to avenge your fallen comrades."

Hearing her words, Maya felt a tightening in his chest. Yes, the true reason he participated in the Prometheus Project was to avenge his important comrades. He became a pilot for the Thunderbolt to defeat that humanoid Gijuu.

Lieutenant Hayase expressed her feelings.

"Besides, I don’t like the current situation. It feels too arranged. But since you seem unrelated…"

Lieutenant Hayase extended her right hand.

"Will you help me stand at the top?"

Maya extended his right arm—his right arm made from Gijuu cells. He grasped Lieutenant Hayase’s hand tightly.

Why had he taken her hand? This promise could potentially cause disadvantages for the Prometheus Project. It wasn’t a contract he could make on his own given his position.

However, during their battle together… the image of Lieutenant Hayase when she activated her limit drive burned into his mind and wouldn’t leave.

"I am the test pilot for the Prometheus Project. If that’s acceptable…"

Lieutenant Hayase gripped his right hand firmly.

"…Sounds good. From today on, we’re equals. You can drop the honorifics and just call me Maya. Also, drop the ranks when you address me."

"…Maya… is this acceptable?"

"It’s still awkward, but I’ll let it slide."

When Lieutenant Hayase released his hand, she turned her back to him.

"I’ll call you Ren, so if you call me Lieutenant Hayase again, I’ll make sure you don’t get any sweets during the celebration for surviving."

Lieutenant Hayase, now Maya, said it jokingly. However, the price for making a mistake seemed far too heavy!

"Wait! Isn’t that a bit much? I’d like at least a few mistakes to be forgiven!"

Before he realized it, he was protesting. Lieutenant Hayase—Maya—looked at him coldly.

"How much do you like parfaits? Well, fine. As a commemoration for becoming partners, I’ll treat you to dinner. You can have a parfait for dessert."

Maya found her back, as she started walking, to be quite reassuring.

"Even though I’ve been promoted to partner, my position of being treated remains unchanged, huh?"

"Because on the surface, you’re still my servant."

"Then there’s nothing I can do about it. I’ll accompany you, then—Maya."

Maya stopped, turned around, and smiled as she said, "I’ll consider that last mistake a close call."

---

After being treated to dinner, Maya was beginning to feel less uncomfortable about being treated so often at the academy. While questioning himself, he pondered what he should do moving forward.

"Is it alright for me to continue being treated like this? To have someone younger treat me and even let me eat parfaits… Can I face my former squadmates?"

Would the captain be exasperated if he heard this? The gamblers and losers would probably laugh. The shy boy would likely look troubled. The MC and hair set might envy him, or would they scold him for not treating others? Comic would probably say it would make for a good story and bombard him with questions.

The surroundings had already grown dark. As he walked along a street with streetlights, he stopped to look at his clenched hand.

"I’ve finally come this far… defeating a first-class Gijuu."

He had experienced many things since coming to this academy, but he had finally achieved the defeat of a first-class Gijuu. There were many issues with the Thunderbolt, but he was undoubtedly moving forward.

Someday—yes, someday—by the time he met that humanoid Gijuu again, he would acquire the power to defeat it.

As he made that resolution, he noticed someone standing under the streetlight. There hadn’t been anyone there just a moment ago.

The figure facing him wore a black jumper. The hood obscured her face. However, she wore a white uniform skirt, so there was no doubt she was a female student from the academy.

"Is something the matter?"

Curious, he called out, and the girl in the black jumper approached him. As she drew closer, he realized she was about his height. When the girl removed her hood, her gray hair and blue eyes stood out even in the darkness.

Standing right in front of him, she stared intently at him.

"…Um?"

"HifumiRen, twenty-two years old. Former special reinforced armor suit infantry. Multiple reports of defeating Gijuu alone. Currently the dedicated pilot of the Thunderbolt in the Prometheus Project."

As she suddenly began stating his information, he was taken aback but remained attentive.

"Please refrain from speaking not just personal information but also information related to the project in a place like this."

In response, she tapped the rank insignia attached to her jumper with her fingertip. He hadn’t noticed the insignia on what he thought was just a fashion jumper.

Immediately, he saluted.

"My apologies, Major. I’m very sorry for the oversight."

Seeing his reaction, she expressed her thoughts without changing her expression.

"You’ve changed your attitude based on military rank, just as the rumors said. It’s a problem that you’ve been in military life for so long that you haven’t fully adapted to academy life."

"…Thank you for your guidance."

He could only feel confused, not understanding what she wanted.

After all, if a student at the academy held the rank of major, the possibilities narrowed down significantly. She reached behind her back and revealed a yellow robe.

"The class president of the Fourth Academy."

If she was both the class president and the ace, she was similar to Usami Major.

"So you’re from the Fourth Academy. I don’t have much acquaintance with you, but I appreciate your support at this academy. Please take care of me from now on."

Having completed his greeting, the class president of the Fourth Academy continued to stare at him emotionlessly.

Staring at him with her blue eyes, the class president of the Fourth Academy said, "…I want you."

"Excuse me?"

As she closed the distance between them, their noses almost touched. For a moment, it felt like her hand might reach out, but he managed to hold back.

She stared into his eyes with her blue gaze.

"You are more suited to our academy than the Third Academy. …The Fourth Academy wishes for HifumiRen."

…Is this me being scouted at the academy? After being recognized as a partner by Maya, now I was being scouted by the Fourth Academy.

**(End of Volume 2)**
