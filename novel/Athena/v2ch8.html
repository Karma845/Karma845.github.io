Chapter 8: Hope for Rematch

The last training session ended in a half-hearted manner, but the first day of the training period finally came to a close.

To reflect on this session, the students from Class Three headed to the classroom still in their pilot suits.

However, it was quite noisy.

As I walked down the hallway, the girls from Class Three were all abuzz with talk about Seiryuu Lieutenant.

"Do you usually use that in a match?"

"If we're not careful, they might take away our Special Equipment."

"It wouldn't be surprising if she got disqualified from being an ace candidate."

Listening to the girls' conversation, it seemed that Seiryuu Lieutenant had engaged in quite a dangerous act.

I was curious and decided to confirm with Hayase Lieutenant.

"Hayase Lieutenant, what exactly happened? I didn't notice anything unusual other than my opponent's performance improving suddenly."

Hayase Lieutenant, walking beside me, seemed to be quite upset with Seiryuu Lieutenant.

Without even looking at me, she replied, "Limit release. It's like a special move that drastically boosts performance."

"A special move? Given that performance, I can see why it would be called a special move."

"Normally, you would need to apply for permission from the teachers, but there are times in actual combat when you don't have the luxury to do that, right? So, for just a few seconds, I can forcibly boost the machine's performance using my authority."

"I thought the performance was higher compared to general machines, but I didn't realize Special Equipment had such a function. However, the fact that it's limited is concerning. Does it put a lot of strain on the machine?"

I had personal interest, but I also thought it was useful information for the Prometheus Project, so I asked Hayase Lieutenant for more details.

However, Hayase Lieutenant shut her mouth.

"Hayase Lieutenant?"

Perhaps I was being too persistent, as Captain Sasaki, who was walking behind me, kicked me lightly.

I leaned slightly forward but managed not to fall.

She must have held back to some extent.

"You don't need to know. Just shut up and head to the classroom."

"Ah, my apologies."

As I walked, I apologized, and Hayase Lieutenant muttered beside me.

Not to me, but to someone who wasn't present.

"...That idiot."

◇

In Class Two's classroom, the sound of a slap delivered by Reona echoed throughout.

Thanks to the strength of a Valkyrie, Kiyone was sent flying from the slap.

As Kiyone tried to get back up from the floor, none of her classmates surrounding her offered any help.

On the contrary, they looked down at her with cold gazes.

This time, Reona's attitude was different from her usual self.

"Do you understand what you did? Using limit release in a mere practice match is an unthinkable act for a Valkyrie."

Kiyone, standing alone, spoke while looking down.

"I didn't realize I was using it."

"Thanks to that, you managed to win against him. Still, you absolutely should not have used limit release in that situation. It's incredibly reckless."

Kiyone's actions were recognized by Reona and the other Valkyries as "something that shouldn't be done."

In other words, Kiyone had essentially committed a foul to win.

Watching from the sidelines, Ichika pushed her way through the girls and approached Kiyone.

She grabbed Kiyone's long, silky hair with her right hand and pulled her face closer.

"Ah!"

Kiyone grimaced in pain, while Ichika frowned.

"I am disappointed in you."

"…I’m sorry."

The gazes of the girls in Class Two toward Kiyone were filled with disappointment and disgust.

Even Reona harbored anger toward Kiyone.

Kiyone said, "I will accept any punishment."

Ichika roughly threw Kiyone away.

"Normally, I would take your Special Equipment away, but unfortunately, I couldn't get the approval of the academy head from a strategic standpoint. Instead, you are being placed under house arrest."

It seemed Ichika had intended to take Kiyone's Special Equipment, but the academy head intervened to save her.

The instruction from the academy head was house arrest.

Ichika said to the fallen Kiyone, "Apologize to Boots Cat before your house arrest."

With that, Ichika left the classroom, followed by Reona and the other girls.

No one spoke to Kiyone.

Left alone in the empty classroom, Kiyone let out a sob.

"...I lost. I was forced to use my trump card... How could I lose to a boy?"

Though she had won the competition, for Kiyone, her battle against Ren felt like a defeat.

That was a trump card that should not have been used in the first place.

Especially in a practice match... As Reona and the others said, it was absolutely not the right moment to use it.

Yet, unable to accept her defeat, Kiyone had used it.

From the moment she was forced to use it, Kiyone felt she had lost, and frustration and shame welled up within her.

"I swore I wouldn't lose to a boy again..."

Kiyone continued to cry in the classroom.

◇

In Class Three's classroom, a reflection meeting was taking place, but suddenly, Teacher Oonichi Minami blew a party horn.

In the spacious classroom, only one party horn sounded... It echoed with a lonely sound.

She seemed completely unfazed and was in high spirits.

"Let's give a round of applause to the newcomers who survived the first day of training! And thank you for your hard work during the joint training!"

Beside the cheerful Teacher Oonichi, there was Kagami-sensei, who was clapping without expression and looking unmotivated.

She took over the proceedings of the reflection meeting from Teacher Oonichi.

"Well, there were some issues, but I'm just glad the joint training ended safely. I'm relieved that there were few students who got into fights with Class Two."

Not "none," but "few," huh?

There must have been some fights that I didn't see.

It's common for tempers to flare when units are competing against each other.

Fighting is a daily occurrence, and it's a set routine for those who fought to receive punishment afterward.

It reminded me of my time as an infantryman.

Kagami-sensei began to outline the points of reflection from this session.

"We don't have much time, so let's wrap this up quickly. While watching your joint training with Class Two, I noticed something. Do you know what it is?"

Kagami-sensei's gaze was directed at Major Sorashima, the class representative.

"Quality... and training level, perhaps? Even in physical training, Class Three was overall inferior."

"I'm glad you noticed. The number of students in your class, and the quality, are currently not up to par with Class Two. It's a situation where you could be called a burden class."

For some reason, Teacher Oonichi shrank back at Kagami-sensei's merciless evaluation.

She seemed to feel responsible.

"Well, we are a class that was hastily put together, so there are aspects that can't be helped. Kagami-chan, please don't be too hard on them. My life is at zero right now."

Kagami-sensei glanced at Teacher Oonichi but seemed unwilling to stop.

"We respect your autonomy because of Mio's policy. I believed that as long as you completed the basic training, you would fill in the gaps on your own. But—what's the result today?"

It seemed that Teacher Oonichi had left things to the girls' autonomy, as her appearance suggested.

She had only imposed the minimum training and hadn't interfered with the girls' methods.

Each of them would fill in their own gaps or strengthen their areas of expertise—there could be various reasons, but she had let them do as they pleased.

However, the results of this joint training had come to light.

Kagami-sensei spoke as if addressing everyone.

However, her gaze was directed at Hayase Lieutenant, who was sitting there looking bored.

"Does anyone have any opinions on this result? Not just stating points for improvement, but anyone who wants to lead the class—Boots Cat?"

It sounded like she was inviting anyone who wanted to lead the battalion to step forward.

I wanted to believe it was just my imagination.

However, since this was an academy where military rules didn't apply, there was a possibility that Kagami-sensei's words would carry weight.

When I looked at Hayase Lieutenant, she noticed my gaze and turned her face away, closing her eyes.

It seemed she had no intention of stepping forward.

Kagami-sensei wore a faint smile.

"Such a notable lack of ambition. Well, that's fine. Sorashima, how would you lead the current Boots Cat?"

Major Sorashima, who was also the battalion leader, pondered for a moment before answering.

"I have no intention of changing Teacher Mio's policy."

Upon hearing that, Teacher Oonichi beamed with joy.

"I believed in you, Mai-chan!"

However, Major Sorashima didn't seem satisfied with that.

"However, I can't leave those who are lacking in ability unattended. Those who do not meet the standards should train even more during the training period and also during breaks."

The girls from Class Three visibly grimaced at that.

Only the rookie, Suzuki, looked downcast and dejected.

Major Sorashima stood up and scanned the girls with her gaze.

"Starting tomorrow, we will revise the training menu. Everyone should be prepared for that."

Seeing this, Kagami-sensei seemed to decide there was nothing more to say and crossed her arms, falling silent.

Teacher Oonichi, who was pouting with her fingers pressed together, looked sad that her policy was being changed.

"I personally prefer a stronger emphasis on individuality for Class Three..."

It was hard to tell who was the homeroom teacher as Kagami-sensei dealt with Teacher Oonichi's dissatisfaction.

"If they are trying to fill in their own gaps, isn't that also a form of autonomy?"

"You guided them, didn't you?!"

"Did I?"

Kagami-sensei feigned ignorance, and Teacher Oonichi puffed up her cheeks.

Just as the reflection meeting was about to conclude, Major Sorashima took out her terminal.

It seemed she received some sort of message, and upon checking the content, she looked over at Hayase Lieutenant, who seemed uninterested.

"Hayase, stay behind."

When told to stay, Hayase Lieutenant turned her face toward Major Sorashima.

"Ugh, how bothersome."

"Don't say that, just stay. It seems someone is coming to apologize about today. You can say whatever complaints you have."

From the context, it seemed there would be an apology from Class Two.

However, the fact that they were going out of their way to apologize suggested that this matter was more serious than I had thought.

Limit release... I couldn't shake the feeling that there was something more to it.

Hayase Lieutenant let out a deep sigh.

"...It's not fun to torment a weakened opponent."

I couldn't help but let my true feelings slip out.

"Did you enjoy it?"

By the time I pressed my mouth shut, it was too late.

Perhaps I was still high from the excitement after the competition, or maybe I had relaxed after the training... In any case, that comment was a mistake.

Hayase Lieutenant was looking at me with a smile.

"You'll join me. Oh, this is a squad leader's order."

"...Understood."

It was a moment where it wouldn't have been surprising if she had lashed out, but it seemed she was willing to let it slide.

Still, it appeared she had relaxed quite a bit at this point.

I needed to tighten up my focus again.

◇

After the reflection meeting ended, only Hayase Lieutenant and I remained in the classroom.

The girls who had been reprimanded during today's training were reportedly being held by Kagami-sensei until they completed their punishment—additional squats and push-ups.

As for me, I was prepared to do over a thousand additional squats and push-ups as a collective punishment for Hayase Lieutenant's conspicuous chatter... Was this also preferential treatment for the ace?

As I pondered various thoughts, the classroom door opened.

In came Seiryuu Lieutenant, her eyes red and swollen, and when she noticed us, she wore an awkward expression.

Silently approaching us, Seiryuu Lieutenant bowed her head to Hayase Lieutenant.

"I sincerely apologize for what happened this time."

Hayase Lieutenant crossed her arms and sat in her chair, beginning to grade Seiryuu Lieutenant's apology.

"You haven't explained what was wrong, so do it again."

"Ugh!? I got carried away..."

"Hurry up. I’ve been waiting for you without even taking a shower. Did you not think to come up with an apology before coming here?"

—Hayase Lieutenant was lively as she pointed out Seiryuu Lieutenant's issues.

I had encountered this type of superior many times, so I couldn't help but feel sympathy for Seiryuu Lieutenant.

However, it wasn't my place to intervene, so I decided to remain silent.

They had their own rules.

This was the Third Academy—the domain of the Valkyries.

It wasn't a situation where someone like me, who knew nothing, should interfere.

Seiryuu Lieutenant averted her gaze and mumbled, "I deeply regret using limit release in a practice match and almost injuring someone from Class Three."

Upon hearing that, Hayase Lieutenant wore a wicked smile.

"You should be apologizing to the boy you dislike, not me. Besides, is that really the attitude of someone who is apologizing?"

"...Y-You..."

Seiryuu Lieutenant was gradually becoming irritated.

When she looked at me, her demeanor was almost angry.

"I apologize for using limit release during the match! How's that? Is this good enough?"

After finishing her statement, she turned her face to Hayase Lieutenant, but it seemed she wasn't satisfied.

"Do you not understand what you did? Throwing a tantrum like that is pathetic."

When Hayase Lieutenant spoke seriously, Seiryuu Lieutenant flinched.

Then, Seiryuu Lieutenant turned to me and bowed deeply.

"I put you in danger by using limit release during the match. ...I'm truly sorry."

When I glanced at Hayase Lieutenant, she seemed to have no objections, so it was fine.

I accepted the apology.

"No, I no longer mind. I consider it a valuable opportunity, so please don't worry about it."

Then, Seiryuu Lieutenant raised her face and protested.

"I do mind! I lost to you! And yet, I won through underhanded means... You must be frustrated too, right?"

When she said "frustrated," it felt a bit off.

Certainly, I had done my best at that time, but if I checked the data and analyzed it, other solutions would likely emerge.

In the end, whether I won or lost, my tasks remained unchanged.

Even if I won, I would seek more efficient ways to deliver effective strikes in the next battle.

Our goal is to fight the Valkyries and gather data.

The objective was achieved.

"There is no meaning in winning or losing, and I don't mind."

If I had won, the evaluation of the Prometheus Project would have likely increased.

However, a victory by chance holds no significance.

Chance lacks reproducibility, and any evaluation that rises will eventually fall again.

I had no intention of getting swept up in the highs and lows of fluctuating evaluations.

However, it seemed that Seiryuu Lieutenant did not find my answer satisfactory.

"What do you mean by that... Are you seriously saying winning or losing has no meaning? You're someone who fights with your life on the line, yet you think it's okay to lose?"

The look on Seiryuu Lieutenant's face as she looked at me was one of undisguised disgust.

Winning, continuing to win... That's why they stand in this place.

From their perspective, my statement was likely intolerable.

When I glanced at Hayase Lieutenant, she was silently listening to our conversation.

I had no idea what she thought of my statement, but I had no intention of changing my opinion.

"What matters on the battlefield is survival, and I agree with that. While it is a problem to accumulate vague defeats, a defeat that can be leveraged for the next time holds meaning."

As I maintained my stance, Seiryuu Lieutenant shook her head.

Then, she turned her gaze to Hayase Lieutenant, who was still silent.

"I can't believe this. Hayase, are you seriously considering making this guy your subordinate?"

She likely wanted to convey that having someone like me by her side would negatively impact Hayase Lieutenant's evaluation.

Hayase Lieutenant replied nonchalantly.

"Not really? I have no intention of correcting someone else's thoughts. What matters is whether it's beneficial to me or not. That's all there is to it."

Seiryuu Lieutenant was momentarily at a loss for words at Hayase Lieutenant's pragmatic response.

"…No matter what I say, it will just sound like the whining of a loser. But stop saying it's okay to lose. It's better not to voice such thoughts, even if you think them."

Having finished her business, Seiryuu Lieutenant turned her back to us and began to walk away.

I stood up and called out to her.

"Thank you for today. If given the chance, I would like to request a rematch."

Seiryuu Lieutenant stopped and turned her upper body slightly.

"...You technically won, right? There's no point in rematching me."

"I don't believe I won. Moreover—your swordsmanship, no matter how emotionally charged, showed little disorder. It must be movements ingrained in you from daily training. They were efficient and very instructive."

Seiryuu Lieutenant's eyes widened, and her face turned red.

"I-I don't care if a boy praises me... I only care about Lord Sanada!"

Lord Sanada? From the way Seiryuu Lieutenant spoke, he seemed to be a man, possibly a member of the academy?

I didn't know who this Sanada was, but there was no lie in her current statement.

"I'm not trying to curry favor. Your swordsmanship was truly beautiful."

"Hyah!?"

Seiryuu Lieutenant turned her face away from me and hurriedly exited the classroom.

"I use my own style of short sword, but I've realized there are many areas where I lack. I was hoping for your guidance... Did she leave already?"

I reached out for a handshake and requested her guidance, but she had escaped.

It seemed I had become disliked.

In the past, I had been told by MC and hairstylists that I couldn't lie, so I should only compliment women when I genuinely felt that way... but it didn't go well.

"I would like to request your teachings, but if I've become disliked, that will be difficult."

Feeling genuinely disappointed, Hayase Lieutenant looked displeased.

"Could you not flirt in front of me? At least do it when we're alone."

Flirt? It seemed Hayase Lieutenant had misunderstood.

"No, that's not it. Flirting refers to inviting a woman to a meal, and it doesn't apply when seeking guidance."

I explained this politely, but Hayase Lieutenant sighed and didn't seem convinced.

"Forget it. I was foolish to expect common sense from you."

"...My apologies."

While I had some unresolved feelings, since she was my superior, I decided to endure the unreasonable situation.
