Chapter 4: Joint Training

The night before Class Three's vacation was about to end.

Kagami, the vice homeroom teacher of Class Three, visited the principal's office with Mio, the homeroom teacher.

In the office, there was a sofa and a low table set up for receiving guests, and Mio was sitting across from Principal Kase Natsuko.

Kagami stood slightly behind Mio, listening to their conversation.

Principal Natsuko was in high spirits, smiling brightly in front of her beloved Mio.

"Oh my, you always have such a good appetite. It was worth preparing this for you!"

Mio was enjoying the tea and cake that Natsuko had prepared.

Both were high-quality items, though Kagami wondered where they had been sourced from.

Natsuko looked pleased as she watched Mio savor the cake.

Mio, too, was acting cheerful, clearly enjoying the treat.

"The cakes prepared by Principal Natsuko are always delicious!"

"I'm so glad you like them. There's more, so eat up!"

"Yay!"

In front of the happy duo, Kagami reluctantly opened her mouth to move the conversation along.

"Mio, we should hear the reason we were called before anything else."

Prompted from behind, Mio suddenly remembered and stopped eating, blushing as she turned to Natsuko.

"That's right. Principal Natsuko, what was the reason you called us?"

With Mio tilting her head, Natsuko tightened her relaxed expression and began to explain.

"Actually, I want to promote interaction between the classes."

"Interaction? Are we going to have some kind of recreation?"

"That would be a wonderful idea, but I believe that training is the best way for Valkyries to understand each other."

Natsuko proposed joint training to Mio.

"To start, I would like Class Three to participate in joint training with Class Two. I've been coordinating this, and it has finally come together. Now, all that's left is to get Class Three's approval, and there shouldn't be any problems."

Kagami felt a sense of unease at the sudden proposal for joint training.

This wasn't something that should materialize out of nowhere.

It should have been planned months in advance or at the beginning of the school year.

Natsuko must have forcefully squeezed this into the schedule, but Kagami couldn't think of a reason why.

"Is there some significance to this sudden joint training?"

When Kagami asked Natsuko, her expression changed dramatically, clearly displeased.

"Significance? What do you care about that? Hurry up and start preparing for the joint training!"

Unlike with Mio, Natsuko was cold towards Kagami.

Seeing Kagami being treated so harshly, Mio stepped in to help.

"Eh~! I want to know too! Principal Natsuko, please tell me~!"

Mio's playful gestures softened Natsuko's bitter expression, turning it into a smile.

"There's no deep meaning behind it~. I just think we need to increase interaction between the classes. You see, the girls at this academy... since they fight as Valkyries, many of them tend to be strong-willed, right? There are quite a few who harbor hostility towards each other, and it worries me~."

While expressing her concern, Natsuko glared at Kagami.

When Kagami subtly averted her gaze, Mio stood up from the sofa.

"Leave it to me, Principal Natsuko! I'll make sure the joint training for everyone to get along is a success!"

Seeing Mio's energetic declaration, Natsuko wiped her tears with a handkerchief, filled with joy.

"That's my Mio-chan! I have high hopes for you."

◇

What awaited Class Three after their vacation was a training period.

There were four classes in the Third Academy's combat division, and it seemed each class took turns on vacation.

The academy kept a class on standby for deployment while allowing the other classes to take vacations and conduct training in the meantime.

A cycle was established to avoid overburdening any single class.

Some classes were on standby for deployment, while others were enjoying their vacations.

Because of this, opportunities for interaction between classes—between units—were said to be rare.

"I didn't hear anything about joint training with Class Two."

"I'm envious that you have enough members."

"Huh? I don't see Class Two's ace around."

However, this time, the circumstances were different.

As Class Three entered the training facility, they found the girls from Class Two already warming up.

When the girls from Class Three appeared, the sharp gazes of the Class Two girls converged on them.

Especially, they were glaring at me.

The Class Two girls seemed to reject the presence of a man like me, who was not even a Valkyrie.

Standing beside me, in her tracksuit, was Lieutenant Hayase.

"Could you stop staring at my attendant like that? ...I might mistake it for a challenge."

With her hands shoved into the pockets of her jacket, Lieutenant Hayase showed no signs of fluster despite being in front of the Class Two girls.

One of the girls from Class Two, who had been warming up, stepped out of the group and stood in front of us.

The slender girl in a tracksuit had long, dark purple hair tied back in a ponytail with a white cloth, and her sharp green eyes were glaring at us.

She exuded a sharpness akin to a blade, and after glancing at Lieutenant Hayase, she looked at me, widening her eyes slightly before furrowing her brow.

"...You really chose a man as your attendant, huh?"

It was clear from her demeanor that she found my presence distasteful.

Lieutenant Hayase smirked provocatively at her.

"Do you have a problem with that? This is Seiryuu Kiyone, a candidate for ace in Class Two. She's quite the man-hater. Even her homeroom teacher, Yoshizawa-sensei, dislikes men, so it's a class full of like-minded individuals."

It seemed that even the instructor of Class Two had a dislike for men.

Indeed, when pointed out, the atmosphere between Class Three and Class Two felt different.

Though they were all wearing the same academy-issued tracksuits, their demeanor and behavior appeared distinct.

Kiyone—Lieutenant Seiryuu was glaring at Lieutenant Hayase with her sharp gaze.

"I've heard. That man is a pilot of a trash weapon that can barely handle a second-class opponent. It's quite eccentric to choose such an incompetent man as a subordinate, let alone an attendant."

Kiyone's words dripped with disdain for men.

Her gaze towards me was fierce, laced with anger.

However, at the same time, her words were not entirely unfounded.

The Thunderbolt I piloted had no achievements against first-class opponents.

From her perspective, it was understandable for her to view me as a non-combatant.

As I remained speechless, Lieutenant Hayase stepped forward to shield me from Kiyone's glare.

"The fact that I chose him holds value. You have no right to speak on that, especially since you're not even an ace. If you're frustrated, then go become an ace and come back to talk."

Kiyone's frown deepened at those words.

"Just because you became an ace in a class like Class Three, don't get too full of yourself. The value of an ace in Class Three and an ace in Class Two is different. —Does a leader of a monkey mountain not know who to challenge?"

Now it was Lieutenant Hayase who was beginning to show anger in response to the provocation.

"Then shall I beat you down so you can't complain again? A coward who still can't take the ace position shouldn't get too cocky."

"—I'll kill you."

The atmosphere was charged, on the brink of conflict, yet for some reason, the other girls did not intervene.

Normally, I would be the type to wait for someone else's instructions in such disputes.

Had I not been told to do something, I might have quietly observed as usual.

However, Lieutenant Hayase was my commanding officer now.

As her subordinate, I had the duty to support her.

"Lieutenant Hayase, we shouldn't fight before the joint training. I don't mind if they say anything about me."

When I said that, Lieutenant Hayase glared at me.

"Being belittled as an attendant is the same as being challenged to a fight. I can't just stay silent when someone says I have no discernment. Besides, they were the ones who threw the first punch."

In response, Kiyone seemed unwilling to back down either.

"The ace of the burdened Class Three is acting all high and mighty... I'll take care of you and your attendant, so hurry up and prepare your dress. Or are you just all talk?"

I couldn't stop the fight between the two.

Looking around for help, I noticed Major Sorakujima and Captain Sasaki seemed to be observing quietly.

The other girls appeared to be enjoying the spectacle of the two's confrontation.

"It's a match between the current ace and the next ace."

"Shall we place bets on who will win?"

"I personally hope Hayase loses at least once."

Among the girls of Class Three, some hoped for Lieutenant Hayase's defeat.

The gazes of the Class Two girls were cold.

"Here she goes again, acting out."

"What about Reona-onee-sama?"

"She said she'd be late due to a meeting with the teacher."

In this situation, I recalled my days as an infantryman.

Back then, fights were common, and many would start intense brawls during training.

I had been challenged to fights numerous times.

However... I never expected that fights would occur in the Valkyrie academy as well.

No, more than that, it seemed like they were quite accustomed to fighting; was that just my imagination?

As I pondered how to stop the fight, a loud voice rang through the training facility.

"That's enough from both sides!"

Turning my face towards the voice, I saw the girls from Class Two—Kiyone included—straightening their postures.

Upon seeing the person who had appeared in the training facility, Lieutenant Hayase also seemed to calm her anger.

I was intrigued by the person who had stopped their quarrel with a single word, but my eyes were wide as I stared at her.

"Lieutenant Hayase... who is she?"

Lieutenant Hayase seemed to notice my surprise and introduced the short-haired girl who had her hands on her hips and was smiling.

Her flaxen hair was short, shaved at the back, with a thin strand hanging down on the right side, creating an asymmetrical look.

With red eyes—slightly droopy—she stood at around 170 centimeters tall.

She gave off a cool rather than beautiful impression.

"That person is Usami Reona, the captain leading Class Two... and also their ace."

Not only was she the captain, but she was also the ace.

In Class Three, the captain was Major Sorakujima, while the ace was Lieutenant Hayase.

Usami Reona held both roles.

It was a testament to her high abilities, but more than that, I couldn't believe I was meeting her again.

Lieutenant Hayase, noticing my unusual demeanor, spoke up.

"Is there something wrong with Usami?"

"...I have met her before."

"Huh? No way."

I still remembered the day I met her.

While it was a shocking encounter, it was just that—nothing more.

For her, it might even be a blemish on her record.

"Though, she probably doesn't remember me."

As we spoke, Captain Usami approached Kiyone, smiling as she admonished her.

"Honestly... without me, you little kittens can't keep it together. And you're the worst offender, Kiyone."

Captain Usami referred to the girls from Class Two as "little kittens."

I looked at Lieutenant Hayase in surprise.

Lieutenant Hayase seemed to understand my astonishment.

"She's not a bad person, but she loves her theatrical lines. Well, she's a cool type of girl, so she's quite popular in the academy. Many look up to her as an older sister."

As she said this, Lieutenant Hayase looked at me with a puzzled expression, as if to say, "I can't understand this world."

To me, she seemed like a strange girl.

However, she appeared to manage Class Two well.

The fact that Kiyone, who had been so confrontational moments ago, was now subdued in front of Captain Usami was proof of that.

Even when scolded, she didn't sulk and seemed to accept the admonishment.

"I apologize. It's just that Lieutenant Hayase was being provocative."

Kiyone seemed to be trying to shift the blame onto Lieutenant Hayase.

For a brief moment, Lieutenant Hayase looked irritated, but Captain Usami let out a small sigh.

"If you can't even brush off provocations, you'll never take the ace position from me. Besides, didn't you provoke her too, Kiyone? If you lie, it makes me sad."

"...I'm sorry."

As Kiyone averted her gaze to apologize, Captain Usami turned to us.

"So, I'd like you to forgive her, Lieutenant Hayase."

In front of Captain Usami's smiling face, Lieutenant Hayase seemed to have her spirit deflated, as if it didn't matter anymore.

"That's fine. If it turns into a fight, you can just crush her."

In response to Lieutenant Hayase, Captain Usami became cheerful.

"Hahaha! Lieutenant Hayase really has the personality of an ace! But please don't underestimate the ace candidates from Class Two. In close combat, Kiyone surpasses me. If you're not careful, she'll overtake you."

While smiling, her eyes seemed to say, "I won't lose to you."

Lieutenant Hayase also wore a combative smile.

"I'm looking forward to it."

Captain Usami nodded a few times before turning her attention to me.

Memories of my past with her resurfaced, and I straightened my posture, feeling cautious.

Captain Usami approached me.

"It's been a while, Hifumi Ren. I never thought we'd reunite here at the academy."

"I'm very pleased to see you again, Captain Usami."

As I saluted to avoid any disrespect, Captain Usami appeared somewhat bored.

"Oh? Even though we've had a fateful reunion, you're being so cold~. I was hoping to thank you for that time."

"Thank you?"

At that moment, I didn't take her words literally.

I braced myself for what I thought might be a form of retribution.

As Captain Usami drew closer, she reached out her hands towards my head.

Could it be a knee to the head!? I was shocked, wondering if I had been hated that much, and I prepared to defend myself, thinking that a knee strike from a Valkyrie would leave me severely injured.

Noticing my tension, Lieutenant Hayase reached out to stop her.

"Wait, what are you doing—"

As I braced for impact, Captain Usami leaned in and kissed my cheek.

"...Huh?"

As I stood there dumbfounded, Captain Usami took a step back and placed a finger on her own luscious lips.

Then, she looked at me with the face of a child who had just succeeded in a prank.

Around us, the girls from Class Two were screaming.

"U-Onee-sama!?"

"Why would you do something like that to a man!?"

"No way... I haven't even had that done to me!"

The person in question seemed unconcerned by the surrounding chaos.

"It's a thank you for helping me. Oh? Was it my lips you liked? Sorry, but my lips aren't cheap. You'll have to settle for my cheek."

I pressed my hand against the cheek that had been kissed, still unable to comprehend what had just happened.

In a panic, I looked over at Lieutenant Hayase, who had become incredibly displeased.

"Lieutenant Hayase, what is the meaning of this?"

"...Why didn't you avoid it?"

I thought it would have been better to be hit just once, but I never expected it to end like this.

While I was still stunned, Captain Usami clapped her hands to gather attention.

I questioned whether it was necessary to draw attention, but Captain Usami gave her orders.

"Now then, we can't waste any more precious time for joint training. Let's wrap up the warm-up quickly."

And thus, the joint training with Class Two began.

With Lieutenant Hayase turning away from me, I pondered what the right response would have been.

However, no answers came to mind.

I never anticipated a kiss would come my way.

Then, I felt as if someone was watching me, and I turned my face in that direction.

Standing there was Lieutenant Seiryuu, glaring at me with a murderous intent.

She noticed my gaze and quickly left the scene, but it seemed she had come to resent me for respecting Captain Usami.

"...This joint training is going to be full of challenges."
