【Digital Exclusive Short Story】 HifumiRen Wants to Choose

HifumiRen had volunteered for the Prometheus Project, but for some reason, he was currently attending a school that trained Valkyries. It was unprecedented for a male like Ren to be enrolled in a school primarily for female students being trained as Valkyries.

In such a situation, Ren found himself in a less frequented area of the academy, sneaking around to check his surroundings. In a place where not only students but even faculty rarely visited, there stood a vending machine. It was functioning well, but the surface showed signs of slight rust.

Faced with the vending machine in this deserted spot, Ren swallowed hard.

"I never expected to find one in a place like this."

The vending machine exuded a dubious atmosphere, but it sold food items. However, the products were unpopular among the academy's staff and students, consisting of portable meals.

Several types were available, all wrapped in silver packaging. On top of that, labels with ingredient lists and flavors were haphazardly stuck on. Ren leaned forward, resting his chin on his hand, and examined them with a serious expression.

"I'll definitely go for the chocolate flavor, but I should also pick one or two more. Considering the rarity, I can't pass up the banana flavor either."

The portable meals sold in the vending machine were the same ones Ren had eaten during his time as an infantryman. They were designed to quickly replenish nutrients, rich in vitamins but often criticized for their taste by the academy's community. However, for Ren, who had little experience with sweets during his infantry days, the mere fact that these products were sweet made them valuable.

Since coming to the academy, his opportunities to eat such items had decreased, so when he found the vending machine in this location, he trembled with joy.

"This time, I'll buy one... no, two banana flavors."

As Ren reached for the vending machine, he heard a voice calling him from a short distance away.

"Ren-kun, where are you?"

Realizing it was Louise's voice, Ren withdrew his hand and walked over to her.

"I'm over here, Louise."

Upon spotting Ren, Louise smiled brightly and waved her hand.

"I found you, Ren-kun! Let's go eat together!"

"Together for lunch? I was planning to grab something from the vending machine, but what about you, Louise?"

"The cafeteria! Students can use it at a low cost, so it’s a lifesaver even before payday. You’ve never used it, have you?"

Ren felt hesitant about going to the cafeteria where the female students gathered. The reason was his awareness of being an outsider in the academy. For him, meals during his infantry days were a time of relaxation, and he didn't want to intrude on that time by visiting the cafeteria.

"Wouldn't it be a bother if I used it?"

Despite his reluctance, Louise grabbed his left arm and pulled him along forcefully.

"You're worrying too much. Come on, let's go! By the way, the most popular dish at the cafeteria is the stamina set. It’s loaded with meat and carbohydrates and super popular. ...Oh, but my personal favorite is the omelet rice set!"

"The stamina set, huh? That’s a bit unexpected."

Thinking it sounded like something men would enjoy, he reconsidered when he heard that the female students liked it, realizing it probably wasn’t what he had imagined. The stamina set must be something stylish, he thought.

Louise began to explain the contents of the stamina set to Ren.

"First, the rice and miso soup come in large bowls, and the main dish usually features generously grilled chicken or pork. It also comes with fried potatoes and yakisoba, which are delicious! Plus, everyone adds pudding or ice cream for dessert."

Listening to Louise’s description, Ren thought,

(Is the content really more fitting for the name 'stamina set' than I imagined?)

That day, when Ren actually used the cafeteria, he saw a stamina set that looked like it could easily fill a big man to the brim.
