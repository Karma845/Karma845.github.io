Chapter 1: Class Three

Multiple trucks filled with soldiers were driving along a road that had been shattered and roughened by the attacks of Gijuu.

The soldiers were crammed into the cargo beds, and I, Hifumi Ren, was among them.

My uniform was stained with mud, and I had only a single bag as my belongings.

Most of the soldiers aboard wore gloomy expressions.

Our base had been decimated due to the Gijuu invasion, and the survivors, like us, were being sent to a different assignment.

It seemed that the war itself had ended in victory, but the losses among us infantry were severe.

While the organization boasted of its victory, the soldiers fighting on the front lines felt little of that triumph.

"Another different unit, huh?"

We survivors were set to be assigned to a new unit.

This had become a recurring cycle every time a unit was decimated or disbanded and reformed.

However, the past of being a soldier enhanced with modifications haunted me wherever I went.

I had never been welcomed at any of my previous assignments, and there were times when I had even been shot at from behind on the battlefield.

I was not particularly good at building relationships, so I could only hope that the unit I was assigned to would allow me to operate without issues on the battlefield.

"I wonder what kind of unit I’ll be assigned to next…"

Though the heat from the many people around was unpleasant, the open cargo bed of the truck allowed me to feel the wind, which was refreshing.

As I looked up at the sky while the truck swayed violently, it seemed we had finally arrived at our destination.

Upon disembarking, I found the captain of my new assignment waiting for us.

The tall, sturdy soldier, who had been alternating his gaze between the screen of his terminal and my face, flashed a bright smile, revealing his white teeth.

"You must be Corporal Hifumi Ren, right? My name is… no, just call me Muscle from now on, that’s what the squad calls me."

Nicknames, call signs, or code names.

It was common to refer to each other with short and easy-to-call names within a squad.

In my case, most of the names I had been called were derogatory, like cyborg, killing machine, or doll.

"Understood, sir."

As I executed a perfect salute, a gesture I had been taught since childhood, the squad leader, who had just told me to call him Muscle, rested his chin on his hand and observed me with interest.

"You’re formal, but you seem serious, which is reassuring. We have a lot of quirky characters in our squad."

It wouldn’t be surprising if the squad leader had already reviewed my history.

Normally, I would be subjected to sarcasm or beaten down to establish hierarchy.

Yet, this squad leader was different, which surprised me.

"—You don’t know my background?"

"Huh? Your situation? Ah, you’re one of those enhanced soldiers, right? I thought you were a fierce warrior who has survived harsh battlefields. I expect great things from you."

The new squad leader, who was smiling while knowing my background, didn’t seem to care at all about me being an enhanced soldier.

Such a response was rare, and it made me feel uneasy, prompting me to ask.

"Is that alright? I’m one of those enhanced soldiers who went out of control."

One of the reasons enhanced soldiers were disliked was due to the rampages of some of them.

Among those of us often sent to harsh battlefields, there were those who, fed up with their treatment, staged a coup.

It was quickly suppressed due to its lack of planning, but it resulted in significant damage to our allies.

From that point on, the image of enhanced soldiers as traitors who would go rogue spread within the organization.

The new squad leader sighed softly.

"But you’ve fought without going rogue until now, right? If that’s the case, I’ll welcome you as an immediate asset. Now, let’s wrap this up. I’ll introduce you to our quirky members, so follow me."

"—Understood."

Though I was slightly bewildered by the unusual response, I followed the new squad leader's back.

◇

—And now, I was walking down the hallway, following the back of a girl who would become my new squad leader.

In the early morning, Valkyries were starting to arrive at the school, and I passed by several of them.

It was an unnatural sight to have a male like me in this place, so everyone looked at me with surprise.

Sometimes, I was met with open disdain, but Lieutenant Hayase Maya, who was walking ahead of me, didn’t seem to care.

Lieutenant Hayase, with her medium-short red hair, walked briskly in front of me.

Her posture was stable, conveying that she had been well-trained.

The Valkyries who fought against the Gijuu were the hope and protectors of humanity.

There was no way they could be weak, but the issue lay in their appearance.

Lieutenant Hayase was indeed fit, but to me, she looked her age.

Her limbs were slender, and her well-defined waist had feminine curves.

She didn’t look like a fighter at all.

By appearance, I was taller and had more muscle mass.

However, their strength was not something that could be predicted by looks.

With those slender arms, they possessed the strength to easily overpower even the most robust soldiers, and I had been astonished when I trained in hand-to-hand combat with Valkyrie candidates.

Though she appeared to be just a delicate girl, the existence of a Valkyrie was a mystery that deepened the more I learned about it.

And now, she was about to become my superior—my captain.

As Lieutenant Hayase turned her upper body to look at me while walking, it seemed she had a question about my story.

"I heard about your comrades, the ones you want to avenge."

"Yes. What I just mentioned was my encounter with the squad leader."

"That’s good. I’m glad you met a nice squad leader. But when I heard about you before, your squad leader’s call sign was Protein, right? Based on what you just said, it should be Muscle now, right?"

It was understandable to be curious about the squad leader’s call sign.

There were various circumstances behind it.

"Yes. It seems he wanted to be called Muscle, but our subordinates insisted on calling him Protein, and in the end, he gave in."

It was a rather disrespectful thing to say about a superior, but the previous gambler and loser insisted that "Protein is enough."

Lieutenant Hayase looked puzzled upon hearing the reason.

"Is it a custom of infantry? A style? Either way, I don’t think I could get used to it. If I’m called by a ridiculous call sign, I’d feel like I’ve been challenged to a fight."

Lieutenant Hayase’s words revealed an undeniable readiness for a fight.

"Speaking of which, in your case, it’s Cherry, right? …That’s quite a peculiar call sign."

When the conversation shifted to my call sign, Lieutenant Hayase’s voice dropped.

Suddenly, it became hard to hear, but I decided to continue the conversation without worrying about it.

"That story came about when I was bombarded with questions while deciding on my call sign, and from that, several candidates were chosen, but there were also—"

As I tried to explain in detail, Lieutenant Hayase’s face turned red, and she quickened her pace, walking ahead.

"You don’t need to go into detail!"

I had been careless. No matter how much of a superior she was, I should have considered that she was a woman.

"I-I apologize."

As I chased after Lieutenant Hayase at the same speed, she suddenly raised her voice.

"Anyway, today is the day you’re officially assigned to Class Three. Have you thought about your greeting? Since I personally picked you up for my squad, I won’t tolerate any disrespect."

Her voice seemed to rise an octave, but I pretended not to notice and replied.

"Understood! I will give my all for the greeting!"

I put enthusiasm into my response, but it seemed Lieutenant Hayase was not pleased.

She suddenly stopped and sighed.

"…You’re really stiff, aren’t you?"

"I’ve been told that often."

Remembering how my former squad leader had also chuckled at my stiffness, I felt a bit nostalgic—and a little sad.

◇

"I am Corporal Hifumi Ren, the test pilot for the Prometheus Project! I will be serving in the combat unit as well, but I hope you will consider the circumstances!"

I raised my voice and executed the salute that had been drilled into me in Class Three.

The classroom for Class Three was a tiered classroom, just like Class Five.

The difference was that, being a combat unit, it had a larger budget allocated to it.

I could immediately understand that it had more funding than Class Five’s facilities.

The desks and chairs used by the female students were also of higher quality than those used by the candidates in Class Five.

There were thirteen female students seated.

They were the Valkyries who had originally been enrolled in Class Three.

And on the podium, four of us, including myself, who had been assigned to Class Three, stood in a line.

The other three assigned to Class Three were members of the First Squad who had rescued me during the last battle when I was surrounded by Gijuu.

Next to us stood Colonel Dainichi Minami, the instructor for Class Three, and Major Ryudia Kagami, the assistant instructor.

Colonel Dainichi was a cheerful and bright instructor, resembling a child.

With her long brown hair tied up, she wore a suit with a deep slit in the skirt.

However, even in a suit, her demeanor conveyed a sense of youthfulness.

"Everyone, attention! Today, we have a new member joining Class Three! And, believe it or not, one of them is a boy! I never thought a boy would transfer into my class. Oh, and by the way, this is a historic achievement since the school was founded. Isn’t that amazing?"

With a grin, she placed her hand on the back of her head, looking far from what one would expect from an instructor.

Despite being a male assigned to the Valkyries—especially to a combat unit—she seemed to be taking it quite lightly.

Next to Colonel Dainichi, Major Kagami displayed a slightly exasperated expression.

Major Kagami was tall, making the 160 cm tall Colonel Dainichi appear small.

Though she had a slender build, she was quite well-trained, and her long silver hair reached down to her knees, fading to a pale blue at the tips.

With her white skin and red eyes… she had shown almost no expression, giving off a somewhat cold impression.

She corrected Colonel Dainichi’s statement.

"Not just in the academy, but in the entire history of Valkyries, this is a first."

When Colonel Dainichi turned her face toward Major Kagami, she excitedly shook her hands up and down.

"Indeed, as Kagami-chan said! You’re quite impressive!"

"I don’t feel happy being praised for something like this."

Though Colonel Dainichi was said to be nearing thirty, she seemed to lack composure.

In contrast, Major Kagami appeared so calm that she seemed almost reliable, to the point where one might think she lacked emotions.

In my case, I simply struggled with expressing emotions, but I felt a sense of familiarity with Major Kagami.

However, I couldn’t help but think about her attire.

She wore slim pants and a vertically striped sweater on top.

It seemed she had chosen a calm color to match her hair, but the sweater was too short, exposing her abdomen.

From the gap between the sweater and pants, her toned abdomen was visible.

Major Kagami didn’t seem to care about her appearance.

They were in charge of educating the Valkyries assigned to the combat unit and would command from the rear during actual combat.

In this classroom situation, it felt like my head was spinning, but to me, they were both figures far above me.

I had also been promoted to second lieutenant when my assignment to Class Three was officially decided, but there was still a significant gap between us.

Colonel Dainichi looked at us.

"Now that we’ve finished our introductions, it’s time for the Q&A session!! Let’s go, everyone! We’ll save the rare boy for last, so let’s start with the others—"

Just as the Q&A session was about to begin, a female student called for a halt.

With her tanned skin and blonde hair, she had modified her uniform to expose her cleavage.

"Colonel Dainichi, can I ask something?"

She had naturally slanted eyes, but her gaze toward us—no, toward me—was sharp.

Colonel Dainichi smiled at the girl.

"What is it, Suzuka-chan?"

The girl, referred to as Suzuka, stood up from her seat, glanced at us, and then directed her question at the instructors.

"I don’t mind that a new member is being assigned… I can even begrudgingly accept that there’s a boy. But can this guy even be useful?"

The question of whether I would be helpful in actual combat made the gazes of the female students in the class turn fierce as they looked at us.

I could hear whispers.

"Those are the kids who were rescued during the last battle when they were isolated, right?"

"I’d prefer at least someone who can be useful, even if they’re just for numbers."

"Where are we supposed to fit them in? I don’t want to disrupt our squad just to accommodate them."

As the voices grew louder, Major Kagami opened her mouth.

"Suzuki, are you dissatisfied?"

Under Major Kagami’s red-eyed gaze, Suzuka took a step back but still wore a stern expression.

"…I am dissatisfied. It’s a nuisance to have a rookie with questionable abilities. I don’t want them in my squad, at least."

I felt a mix of emotions toward the girl who was sticking to her opinion.

In the place I had come from, it was unacceptable to talk back to an instructor.

I had been taught that the academy was different from a regular military, but still, expressing one’s opinion felt like an act that wasn’t allowed.

That’s why I found her incredible for speaking her mind.

The three female students next to me displayed different expressions.

Suzuki Megumi, with her short orange hair and tanned skin, was known to be good at sports.

She seemed to be emotional, as she snapped back at the current words.

"We can fight too if we have Valkyrie Dresses! I’ll prove it in actual combat!"

Against Megumi’s bold statement, Nishitani Kanako, a petite girl with long, curly hair, tried to pull her back by grabbing her arm.

"Y-You can’t do that. Everyone here is top-tier. You shouldn’t pick a fight…"

She appeared to be cautious of her surroundings, showing a somewhat fearful demeanor.

"Stop it. It would just look bad for us to say anything right now."

The last of the three was Asai Mami, who had short navy blue hair.

She was tall and slender, with fair skin.

Though she had seemed slightly tense earlier, she was now looking down, biting her lip in frustration.

Megumi shook off Kanako’s hand and glared at Mami.

"Are you just going to stay quiet after being challenged?"

"…I don’t want to be dragged into this. If you want to fight, do it alone."

"What did you say?!"

As the two began to argue, the rank insignia of Lieutenant Suzuka was visible, and she laughed derisively.

It seemed she thought it wasn’t worth her time to respond, but her gaze shifted from me to Lieutenant Hayase.

"By the way, it seems there’s a fool who pulled in someone who can’t even beat a first-class soldier into their squad."

All eyes in the classroom turned to Lieutenant Hayase, who had been quietly sitting in the back corner.

"If you have something to say, say it clearly."

Under Lieutenant Hayase’s scrutinizing gaze, Lieutenant Suzuka visibly became displeased.

"Just because you’re an ace doesn’t mean you can do whatever you want! It’s one thing if they’re a candidate, but we have no idea if this experimental unit will even be a combat asset—especially since he’s a boy, right? If you want to indulge in romance, go ahead, but don’t drag everyone in the Boots Cat along with you."

I scanned the classroom, observing the hostile gazes directed at Lieutenant Hayase.

They were all filled with animosity.

Colonel Dainichi appeared to be watching the situation with a hint of enjoyment and curiosity.

While I was exasperated by Colonel Dainichi, Major Kagami was also observing the situation.

It seemed neither of them intended to intervene.

…Lieutenant Hayase was isolated.

As I felt that the situation was precarious, Lieutenant Hayase slowly stood up from her seat.

Then, she fixed her gaze on Lieutenant Suzuka and spoke.

"You have quite the naive mind if the first thing that comes to your head is romance. I pulled him in because I thought he was more suitable than anyone else."

With confidence—and a hint of provocation—her words visibly affected Lieutenant Suzuka.

"Are you saying this boy is more suitable than anyone else? What can a nobody who can’t even defeat a first-class soldier do?"

"I acknowledge him. That alone holds value. Besides, he’s at least not a nobody compared to you or the others."

"…What do you mean by that?"

The atmosphere in the classroom turned tense.

To be honest, I was grateful for being held in high regard.

Thanks to Lieutenant Hayase’s advice, I had been able to operate the magic power converter installed in the prototype experimental machine, Thunderbolt.

I personally felt indebted to her, and I knew the strength she had shown on the battlefield.

The sight of her effortlessly taking down a first-class soldier had been burned into my memory.

I personally held Lieutenant Hayase in high regard.

However, the behavior in the classroom was problematic.

After saying her piece, Lieutenant Hayase returned to her seat, seemingly uninterested in the gazes directed at her.

The classroom was filled with a tense atmosphere, and as I sensed a crisis, a bespectacled female student stood up.

"Everyone, that’s enough."

Lieutenant Suzuka turned her body toward the girl.

"But, Mai!"

"Stand down. After all, it was Suzuka who ruined the welcoming mood for the newcomers, right? You shouldn’t bully the newcomers who are happy about their official assignment."

The one admonishing Lieutenant Suzuka was Major Sorakushima Mai.

She was the captain of Class Three—Boots Cat.

In a classroom filled with colorful hair, her ordinary black bob cut stood out.

Major Sorakushima smiled.

"I welcome all four of you. I know this is sudden and confusing, but let’s get along from now on."

The three newcomers, addressed by the captain, nodded, albeit with troubled expressions.

"And you are Hifumi… Ren, right?"

"Yes! Major Sorakushima, since my rank is lower, you can call me by my name."

As I requested to be called by my name, I noticed Lieutenant Hayase frowning at the corner of my vision.

Had I said something to offend her?

Major Sorakushima wore a slightly troubled expression.

"I don’t want to call an older man by his name, but if you insist, I’ll accept it."

"Thank you."

As I saluted, Major Sorakushima turned to the instructors.

"Colonel Dainichi, I believe this is a situation where you should have intervened?"

When she voiced her opinion to the instructors, Colonel Dainichi wore a meaningful smile.

"Hehe… This is also an opportunity for the students to grow. By the way, can I leave the new students’ assignments to you, Mai-chan?"

I wasn’t sure what she meant by "assignments," but I felt it would be inappropriate for me to interrupt their conversation, so I decided to remain silent.

Then, Major Sorakushima glanced at me before looking at Lieutenant Hayase.

"If that’s the case, let’s give our ace a chance to grow as well. Even though she finally has a servant, it’s still a bit lonely with just one."

"Ooooh… Does that mean?"

Colonel Dainichi seemed to have guessed the answer but wanted Major Sorakushima to say it.

Lieutenant Hayase appeared to have realized it as well, her body lifting from her seat.

"Wait, hold on!"

Major Sorakushima wore a mischievous smile, like a child who had successfully pulled off a prank.

"I’ll leave the education of the newcomers to Lieutenant Hayase. For a while, you’ll be paired with the newcomers in your squad. Now, let’s all go on break. We’ll dismiss for today."

As the dismissal was announced, the Valkyries belonging to Class Three stood up and left the classroom in unison.

"Ah, it’s finally over."

"Now we can relax."

"Should we go out and play? It’s too much trouble to go back and change."

Left behind were the instructors, the newcomers, and Lieutenant Hayase.

Lieutenant Hayase trembled with rage before raising her voice.

"You all just dumped everything on me and ran away!"

Colonel Dainichi, after observing Lieutenant Hayase’s state, nodded several times before leaving the classroom.

"You’ll have to practice having subordinates, Maya-chan. Think of this as a good opportunity and do your best!"

Major Kagami let out a small sigh.

"That Dainichi is as carefree as ever."

However, it seemed she agreed with leaving the newcomers in Lieutenant Hayase’s care.

Facing the stunned Lieutenant Hayase, Major Kagami calmly stated.

"Maya, think of this as a good experience and accept it."

With those words, Major Kagami left the classroom.

As soon as the instructors were gone, Lieutenant Hayase seemed to snap back to reality.

She began to scrutinize the three Valkyries standing next to me.

"…I only needed one servant, but now I have to deal with extra baggage."

In front of Lieutenant Hayase, who was complaining, the three girls, now referred to as baggage, wore complex expressions.

While it seemed I was being evaluated positively, I couldn’t afford to feel elated.

I was beginning to notice the significant issue Lieutenant Hayase had regarding the command of her unit.
