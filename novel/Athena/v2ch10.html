Chapter 10: The Green Watchdog

The second group of female students, in standby mode, took turns entering the waiting room.

They had changed into their pilot suits, and the waiting room was furnished with sofas and monitors, making it a comfortable space.

Among them was Reona.

Her pilot suit for the Special Equipment stood out with a different design compared to the other girls.

However, for someone like Reona, who loved to stand out, it was a welcome change.

The only thing she disliked was the design itself, but that was a necessary compromise for the sake of the pilot's safety.

At the moment, she was sitting on a beach chair-like sofa, reading a magazine.

If she turned her gaze slightly to the side, she could see Kiyone sitting in the corner of the room.

Although her disciplinary action had ended, she was to be monitored by Reona, the class representative, for a while.

The reason both Special Equipment holders were in the waiting room together was precisely that.

Under normal circumstances, the two of them would not enter the waiting room together.

If two Special Equipment holders acted together, their rest timings would synchronize as well.

If an emergency deployment was ordered while they were resting, it would delay the deployment of the Special Equipment pilots.

This time was an exception.

"It’s tough to maintain focus like this. Kiyone-chan, is there anything you’re personally careful about?"

Since the disciplinary action had ended, Reona spoke to her as usual.

However, Kiyone had been somewhat absent-minded lately.

Even now, her response to Reona's question was delayed.

"...Huh? Oh, I do try to move around a bit before entering the waiting room."

"It doesn’t look like it’s helping. By the way, what’s been going on with you lately? You seem a bit off since your disciplinary period ended."

Reona thought it was also her job to care for her important junior’s mental well-being and showed a sense of maturity, encouraging Kiyone to share if she had any worries.

Kiyone looked down for a moment before raising her face, slightly blushing.

"I was told I’m beautiful."

Reona hesitated for just a moment at Kiyone's words.

It was a rather common topic in this academy.

"Did a junior start to admire you? If that’s the case, I think you should tighten up a bit so you don’t disappoint them."

Reona assumed that Kiyone had received a confession or admiration from one of the girls in the academy, which was quite likely.

Many girls looked up to Reona as an older sister.

So it wouldn’t be surprising if there were girls who admired Kiyone as well.

However, it seemed the person in question was the problem.

"I haven’t been told something like that since I was a child, and the only ones who said it were family."

"There are people who notice you."

"But I hardly have any connection with that person."

"I don’t think time matters when it comes to love or admiration, though?"

"...I still think it’s strange."

"It’s not strange. You should think about it more lightly. Kiyone-chan, can’t you respond to that person’s feelings? If not, it’s important to properly decline."

Having seen seniors who took an ambiguous stance and ended up in a troublesome situation, Reona made it a point to decline if someone confessed.

Indecisiveness was the worst.

This was something she was determined to convey to Kiyone.

"It takes courage to decline, but if you don’t handle that well, the aftermath can be scary. I’ve seen it happen firsthand, so I know it’s true."

Reona offered her advice out of kindness, but Kiyone didn’t look entirely displeased.

Her cheeks were flushed, and the fact that she had been thinking about the other person recently indicated there was some interest.

Kiyone nodded slightly.

"Um... I think I’ll try to respond to their feelings. It’s a precious offer, and more than anything, it feels a bit rude to decline after being invited so passionately."

As Kiyone twisted her body, making up her mind, Reona felt a sense of excitement.

If Kiyone was going to decline, she wouldn’t press further, but if she intended to date, that was a different matter.

"In that case, as your superior, I should keep track of your relationships. It’s fine if you don’t want to talk about it, but who is it? From another class?"

While she was genuinely curious about Kiyone’s romantic situation, the Valkyries often had to deal with the shadow of death.

She wanted to keep track of things to ensure that if Kiyone lost her life, her belongings could be passed on to the other person—this was also a concern.

Kiyone hesitated, unsure if she should answer, but perhaps sensing Reona’s thoughts, she revealed the identity of the person.

"It’s Hifumi Ren, the one we fought against the other day."

"Oh~ Ichinose-kun, huh? That’s... huh?"

The other girls in the waiting room pretended not to be interested but were listening intently to the conversation.

However, when Ren's name came up, everyone was shocked and stared at Kiyone.

Reona's composure crumbled, and she couldn't hide her agitation.

"Wait a moment! Hifumi Ren, the one we fought in the tournament? Not a different person with the same name, right? It’s the same person, right?!"

As she nearly fell from her chair in disbelief, Kiyone tilted her head.

"I don’t think there’s anyone else with such a distinctive name. It’s definitely Hifumi Ren. On the day I went to apologize, he said my—was beautiful..."

Kiyone's shy words were difficult to catch at times, but it seemed that Ren had indeed called Kiyone beautiful.

Reona began to tremble.

"I-I can’t believe Kiyone-chan had a crush on him... Huh? Should I have accompanied you when you went to apologize?"

Reona was so downcast that the surrounding girls began to worry, but when the familiar siren of the waiting room rang out, her expression changed, and she quickly stood up.

"The Gringarm Team is on emergency deployment. Please board the transport aircraft immediately."

Everyone dashed out of the waiting room, heading for the transport aircraft loaded with Valkyrie Dresses.

The resting female students would soon rush over as well, following as the second wave.

Reona switched her mindset and shouted while running.

"Seriously, what a nuisance at such an important moment. Sorry, but I’ll take it out on the Gijuu!"

◇

From the school building's window, they could see the transport aircraft taking off.

During class, Lieutenant Hayase stood up and approached the window to check the outside.

"The deployment is as swift as ever. It’s a stark contrast to us."

Her unnecessary comments made the gazes of the Third Academy students toward Lieutenant Hayase sharp.

Kagami-sensei, who was in charge of the class, let out a small sigh.

"Everyone, that’s it for today’s lesson. We will prepare for deployment and wait."

The female students of the Third Academy grumbled as they stood up and left the classroom.

"It’s rare for the Second Academy to request reinforcements."

"Is it really necessary to wait for them?"

"We’re already short on combat power in the Third Academy."

Kagami-sensei clapped her hands to hurry the complaining students along.

"Just hurry up."

Since the students weren’t in a rush, it didn’t seem urgent, but that was not the case.

I decided to confirm with Kagami-sensei just in case.

"Kagami-sensei, should I be deploying as well?"

Kagami-sensei looked at me with her arms crossed, her brow furrowed.

"You are already a member of the Third Academy, and I’ve informed the development team. Then fulfill your duty."

"Yes! My apologies!"

As I hurried to leave the classroom, Kagami-sensei called out to stop me.

"And Hifumi Ren."

"Yes?"

"Call me Major now. And don’t forget your superior."

When I turned back, Lieutenant Hayase was still gazing out the window.

After saluting Major Kagami, I approached Lieutenant Hayase to seek instructions.

"Lieutenant Hayase, I’m heading to prepare for Thunderbolt’s deployment. As I mentioned before, I’ll be waiting inside the transport aircraft, so if you need to join, it will be after we deploy."

When Lieutenant Hayase turned around, her demeanor was slightly different from usual.

The smile had vanished, and she exuded an aura of fighting spirit that even I found intimidating.

"Get going and finish your preparations quickly. You might actually deploy this time."

"Is that a decision?"

I had only been ordered to wait, but it seemed Lieutenant Hayase intended to deploy.

The reason was typical of her.

"It’s a hunch. A hunch. I won’t accept complaints if I’m wrong."

With that, Lieutenant Hayase left the classroom first, and I followed suit.

◇

The Second Academy was flying a flag depicting the face of a watchdog drawn in golden thread against a green background.

The battalion name was Gringarm.

Currently, they were inside the transport aircraft, where the first wave was receiving a situation report from Colonel Ichika.

"We have confirmed the emergence of a medium-sized gate. Our mission is to destroy the gate and eliminate Gijuu of rank two or higher."

Ichika, whose atmosphere remained unchanged, walked among the Valkyries lined up in two rows inside the transport aircraft, looking at their faces.

"As of now, we have not observed any natural dissolution of the gate. It continues to spew out Gijuu. The number of rank one Gijuu is slightly higher than expected for a medium-sized gate, but we have received reports that it is within acceptable limits."

There are rules when Gijuu emerge.

A wormhole called a gate connects to another world, from which a large number of Gijuu are released.

The main Gijuu are rank three, but those are left to the ground forces.

The Valkyries deal with Gijuu of rank two or higher, which the ground forces cannot handle.

"The command on site will be entrusted to Reona... which means it’s the usual routine."

Stopping in her tracks, Ichika issued orders to everyone.

"Eliminate the Gijuu as usual, and return without losing a single one of you. That’s all from me."

Seeing her students salute in unison, Ichika felt a sense of relief in her heart.

(Reona is here, Kiyone is here, and the others are all top-notch... The Second Academy is at its strongest point in history right now.)

She felt reassured, knowing that their combat power was more robust than when she was a student.

However, she wouldn’t show such a face to her students.

Reona commanded everyone.

"Everyone, hurry and put on your dresses."

The Valkyrie Dresses lined up against the side of the transport aircraft were donned one after another by the Valkyries.

Kiyone was among them.

"I will continue to win and prove my worth... For now, that’s all I need."

Having made a blunder previously, Kiyone was particularly motivated for this deployment.

She likely intended to redeem herself.

Once Reona donned her dress, she called out to those around her.

"You’re really fired up, Kiyone-chan. But if you rush your feelings too much, it won’t lead to good results. You need to relax a bit."

Once everyone had donned their Valkyrie Dresses, the second wave of transport aircraft approached.

Ichika signaled for deployment.

"Go, my Valkyries."

The hatches on the sides of the transport aircraft opened one after another, and a gust of wind blew inside.

Simultaneously, the Valkyries were launched into the air.

The launched Valkyries quickly regained their posture in midair and swiftly distanced themselves from the transport aircraft, heading toward the battlefield.

◇

A massive black sphere floated at the location where the gate had appeared.

With a diameter of over a hundred meters, Gijuu were overflowing from within, spilling out from its surface.

The rank three Gijuu, which couldn’t fly, fell into the sea one after another, then swam toward the land.

Some of the rank three Gijuu seemed unable to swim, and they perished in the water.

Even so, the number of Gijuu continuously spewing from the black sphere was considerable.

If the black sphere were left unchecked, the enemy forces reaching the land would likely number in the tens of thousands.

As Reona spotted the black sphere, she confirmed the disturbance in the communication lines.

When a gate appears, radar and other systems tend to malfunction.

The organization had concluded that this was due to a force field, essentially the influence of magic.

Countermeasures had been taken, but communication improvements had yet to be realized.

However, today, the range of communication disruption caused by the gate was broader than usual.

The Valkyrie Dress Reona wore was a custom Special Equipment designed for commanders.

It was enhanced for communication compared to other models, but it was still affected by the disturbance.

Reona felt her brow furrow at the faster-than-expected communication degradation, but as a commander, she held back.

"Are mechanical communications failing at this distance? This is a bit more troublesome than before. All units, lift the restrictions on weapon usage."

The Valkyrie Dresses used by the Valkyries utilized magical power, allowing for basic conversation even in areas with communication disturbances.

However, that conversation was mixed with noise, and if they got too far apart, communication would be lost.

The closer they got to the black sphere—the gate—the stronger the communication disturbance became.

Ultimately, the Valkyries had to rely on their own senses and magical power in battle.

As the Valkyries approached, the rank two and higher Gijuu waiting near the gate began to stir.

"Haha! It seems we have plenty of rank one Gijuu this time. Everyone, be careful not to isolate yourselves while fighting. Spread out!"

Issuing the command to scatter, the units spread out in squads.

Just as Reona gave the order, the Gijuu surrounding the gate launched their attacks.

Reona’s movements, evading attacks that shot out magical power like lasers and beams, were refined and appeared almost like a dance.

"Too slow, too slow! You can’t catch my beloved machine with that kind of attack!"

While Reona’s Valkyrie Dress enhanced communication capabilities, it was also a high-mobility type that could be called a high-mobility model.

It allowed her to quickly secure advantageous positions over her opponents on the battlefield, making it popular among the Valkyries.

However, to enhance mobility, it couldn’t carry many large weapons, which was a drawback.

In fact, aside from the large rifle in her right arm, the sub-arms of Reona’s Valkyrie Dress only held a small optical weapon in her left arm.

In her hand, she wielded just a trident.

It was rare for a high-mobility type to be equipped with heavy armaments.

Being a Special Equipment, it had a higher payload capacity than mass-produced models, but there were still limitations, so she always struggled with equipment selection.

As Reona signaled to her following subordinates, they readied their large rifles.

"Bang!"

At Reona’s cheerful shout, they pulled the trigger, and real bullets and beams surged toward the Gijuu that had been targeting them, piercing through.

Basically, Reona acted alone on the battlefield to draw the enemy’s attention, while directing her squad’s positioning to lure the enemy in.

With this combat style, Reona significantly reduced the mortality rate of the Second Academy, training them into a highly stable unit.

The Gijuu exposed to the long-range attacks of the Gringarm Battalion were primarily rank one Gijuu—tough Gijuu with thick armor, like the dinosaur-like Gijuu Baooga.

The thick outer shell of Baooga made it difficult to penetrate, and its magical force field was also robust, making it a simply strong and troublesome opponent.

They attempted to close the distance to engage in close combat with the Valkyries attacking from a distance.

Reona smiled as she observed the movements of the Gijuu.

"That’s a bad move. Because we—"

As the swarm of Gijuu advanced toward the multiple squads conducting long-range attacks, other squads launched their assaults from the sides.

Among them was Kiyone, who swung her sword down at Baooga.

A rapid descent from directly above.

The head of Baooga, protected by its force field and thick shell, was severed as if it had been struck by a guillotine.

Other Baooga were simultaneously attacked by multiple Valkyries from the Second Academy, falling into the sea.

As she brushed her bangs back with her right hand, Reona looked down at the falling Gijuu with cold eyes.

"—I’m better at close combat."

With many rank one Gijuu defeated, the Gijuu’s main fighting force had been diminished.

Reona issued an attack order to her subordinates against the now weakened Gijuu.

"Let’s keep firing. We’ll wipe out the rank one Gijuu and then move on to destroy the gate."

"Yes!"

Hearing her subordinates' responses, Reona watched as Gijuu were shot down one after another right in front of her.

The Second Academy had a high survival rate, which meant that seasoned Valkyries were more likely to remain.

They were well-trained, coordinated, and a unit without gaps.

Reona felt relieved seeing her subordinates’ performance.

(This time, it seems we’ll manage to get through safely again.)

◇

Inside the transport aircraft that had launched the Valkyries, Ichika was checking on the Gringarm Battalion’s status.

"The noise is still terrible as ever. I can hardly tell what’s happening."

As she grumbled, the communications officer took it seriously and apologized.

"I’m sorry. We’ve made significant improvements compared to before, but..."

"I know. Don’t worry about it."

Surrounding the transport aircraft that remained in the battlefield for command were the Valkyries providing escort.

More than two hours had passed since the combat began, yet the noise had not dissipated.

This was evidence that the gate had not been destroyed.

"They’re having a hard time."

As Ichika muttered, the female operator looked a bit perplexed.

"It’s only been two hours, though?"

Realizing that the joke didn’t land, Ichika noticed that this communications officer had no experience accompanying the Gringarm Battalion on deployment.

"With Reona and Kiyone both present, it’s concerning that they can’t destroy a medium-sized gate."

If it were another class—like the Boots Cat—it would take much longer.

However, the current Gringarm Battalion was the best Ichika had ever seen.

At this level, it wouldn’t have been surprising if they had already destroyed the gate and moved on to the extermination phase.

"Could there be a troublesome enemy? I don’t think a special class would appear at this scale..."

As Ichika pondered with her hand on her mouth, communication came in from the Valkyries providing escort.

"Colonel! Miho is!"

Hearing the name Miho, Ichika immediately envisioned the face of her student, Saikawa Miho.

She was a newcomer who had just been brought into the Second Academy, but she was a promising child.

Ichika had been keeping an eye on her, and she was not the type to flee from the battlefield.

It was concerning that she was approaching alone.

"What’s the situation? Were you hit?"

One of the escorts headed toward the ally in need of rescue, while another provided the situation report.

"It seems she’s taken quite a beating. She may have been ordered to retreat."

"Don’t speak in vague terms, you fool! Quickly put me through to Miho!"

"Y-Yes!"

The monitor in the transport aircraft displayed the face of the Valkyrie called Miho.

She had a wound on her forehead, blood streaming down.

Ichika’s eyes widened at the sight, but she remained calm, not wanting to reveal her agitation.

"What happened?"

"We’ve confirmed the emergence of a special class from the gate. It’s definitely Toto Dino. The older sisters tried to take it down, but a large number of enemy reinforcements were sent through the gate... I was told to report to you immediately, Colonel."

"A wide-area destruction type... Any other information?"

"N-No, none."

Seeing Miho on the verge of tears, Ichika gritted her teeth and spoke.

"Valkyries don’t cry on the battlefield! Reona and the others are fighting, right?"

"Y-Yes!"

"Request reinforcements immediately. You will fall back to the second transport for resupply and maintenance."

The second and third transports had already fallen back.

This meant to retreat to a safe zone and rest, but Miho was aware of this and tried to resist.

"I can still fight!"

"—You’re a hindrance. Fall back."

"!? ...Understood."

"Take her back."

Issuing the order to one of the escorts to take Miho back, Ichika instructed the communications officer to connect her to the academy head.

As she did so, Ichika felt a sense of urgency in her heart.

(A special class appearing from a medium-sized gate is rare. And a large number of reinforcements? What on earth is happening?)

Ichika realized from her experience as a Valkyrie that this situation was unusual.

For a medium-sized gate, the number of enemies being produced was far too high.

(With the Third Academy as a combat force, I have significant concerns. If only the First or Fourth Academy could come out, I would feel more at ease.)

Having observed the joint training, Ichika felt that the Third Academy was indeed a weak combat force.

While reinforcements from other classes would be reassuring, the Third Academy felt unreliable.

(If my class were to be wiped out, I would hold you, Hinata, responsible...)

Worrying about her students, Ichika concealed her anguish behind a calm facade.
