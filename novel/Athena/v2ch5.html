Chapter 5: The Rescue of the Valkyrie

A squad clad in special reinforced armor suits dashed through a forest filled with unfamiliar plants. The voice of our squad leader, Protein, came through the communication device in my helmet.

"Just a little further to the destination. Keep stealthy as much as possible and head there at full speed!"

What a reckless order.

The veteran members of the squad, Gambler and Loser, began to complain as we ran.

"Same old nonsense from him."

"If we get spotted by the Gijuu, we'll be overwhelmed!"

The area we were operating in was where the Gijuu had surged through the gate. Despite the fact that the Gijuu had yet to be eradicated, our squad had been dispatched. As Loser pointed out, if we were discovered by the Gijuu, we would be surrounded and crushed by sheer numbers.

The reason the higher-ups had sent us into such a situation was simple.

A special rescuer had fallen into enemy territory.

Even the squad leader couldn't hide his dissatisfaction.

"Since the goddess of the skies fell into enemy territory, it can't be helped. According to the intel, we're almost at the drop point, but there's a chance she's being chased by Gijuu. Don't let her slip away!"

The one we were heading to rescue was a Valkyrie, the star of the battlefield. She had been shot down during combat and had fallen right in the middle of where the Gijuu were swarming, which is why an infantry unit was deployed for the rescue.

Loser sarcastically remarked about being thrown into a mission with a high chance of death.

"How many infantry will die just to save a single Valkyrie?"

Gambler agreed with that sentiment but was more pragmatic.

"One Valkyrie is a more valuable asset than our lives."

As the noise level rose, the squad leader reprimanded us.

"Focus on the mission a bit! ...By the way, there are four of us, right? I've only heard one person's voice since earlier. Where's the new guy?"

Gambler and Loser called out to me as I kept my eyes peeled for threats from behind.

"Newbie Cherry is keeping up just fine."

"Cherry, you should at least complain a bit. At this rate, we could die in battle at any moment!"

Having just been assigned, I was struggling to adapt to the squad's unique atmosphere.

"...I apologize. I was concentrating on the mission."

The squad leader looked a bit conflicted at my response.

"That's reassuring as a subordinate. I wish the other two would take a page from your book. But you should try a bit harder to fit in with the squad. Creating a comfortable working environment is also an important skill for a soldier."

"...I haven't trained in communication skills."

My answer seemed to amuse Gambler and Loser, who both burst out laughing.

The squad leader chuckled as well.

"That might be your personality. Though, you're a bit too passive—"

"Squad Leader!"

As I interrupted and took off running, the three of them followed.

The sounds of gunfire and the footsteps of Gijuu reached our ears.

A girl in a tight-fitting special suit was running away from the Gijuu, a handgun in hand.

Fortunately, there weren't many Gijuu pursuing her.

"So you've made it this far. Everyone, let's help the Valkyrie and earn that special bonus!"

At the squad leader's command, we opened fire on the Gijuu, successfully ambushing and taking them down.

Gambler and Loser kept watch around us while the squad leader contacted headquarters.

The squad leader then gave me an order.

"Cherry, you're the escort for the goddess. Be polite."

"...Understood."

I approached the Valkyrie, who had managed to escape from the Gijuu but was now sitting on the ground, gasping for breath. I lifted my helmet's face shield, revealed my face, saluted, and extended my hand.

"I'm glad you're safe. We are—"

The Valkyrie swatted my hand away as I tried to help her up.

For a brief moment, uncertainty flickered across her face, but she quickly hardened her expression and stood up on her own.

"I can't remember every infantryman. Now hurry up and take me to safety."

Without even looking at us, that Valkyrie made Loser complain through the communication device.

"Just as the rumors say, she's got quite the ego. We're not even on her radar."

I said nothing and lowered my helmet's face shield.

We escorted her safely back to the secure zone.

In the secure zone, an aircraft was already waiting, and upon confirming the Valkyrie's safety, someone who appeared to be her comrade descended and embraced her.

"Usami! I'm so glad you're safe!"

"I'm sorry for the trouble, Yoshizawa-sensei."

"Idiot! It's Colonel now. Wait a moment, I need to contact the academy. —This is Yoshizawa. We've successfully recovered Usami Reona."

After the rescue, we learned her name was Usami Reona.

As we handed over the Valkyrie, Colonel Yoshizawa shot us a stern look and said,

"...My subordinate has caused you a lot of trouble. But forget about today. It's for your own good that you don't speak of it."

Though she expressed her gratitude, her expression warned us.

Watching the two enter the aircraft, Gambler remarked,

"It seems being saved by us is seen as a disgrace."

Loser let out a deep sigh.

"Even after risking our lives to save her, not a word of thanks from her. She had an attitude that wouldn't be surprising if she held a grudge against us."

The squad leader scolded the two of them.

"That's enough. If you keep quiet, you'll receive a special bonus. Asking for more than that will bring you misfortune."

As I watched the aircraft take off vertically, I muttered,

"Is that what a Valkyrie looks like...?"

"...And that was my encounter with Major Usami."

Currently, I was participating in joint training with Class Two at the indoor training facility.

We were doing jumping squats, and next to me was Lieutenant Hayase, who, despite her slender frame, jumped high and landed deeply before jumping up again.

Each movement was precise, putting a solid load on her muscles.

Lieutenant Hayase was performing movements that were exemplary, but during training, she had asked me about my past with Major Usami.

She was looking at me with a suspicious gaze.

"Would she really go that far over something so trivial? Was there something else?"

"Honestly, there was nothing. If anything, I wonder why she remembered me. I was called by my callsign back then."

"I think your callsign is questionable. Well, I can somewhat guess what happened afterward."

"Isn't it supposed to be that Valkyries avoid contact with infantry? I thought it would be difficult for her to look into me."

"There's a rumor that your information is circulating within the academy due to your involvement in the Prometheus Project. If she looks into your background, she would recognize you as the infantryman from that time."

The thought of my information being out there was concerning, considering the project's confidentiality.

"...I will consult Dr. Allison immediately."

Talking to Dr. Smith about this would just end with him saying, "That's unfortunate," and laughing it off.

I had no choice but to leave it to Dr. Allison.

However, it seemed Lieutenant Hayase found something intriguing in my story.

"More importantly, why did you share unnecessary details? The part about the squad leader and you was irrelevant, right?"

"...I judged that it was necessary to explain the circumstances."

"Is that so? It was clear you wanted to impress me with the wise words of your former squad leader to help you manage the squad."

"I had no such intention... No, you're right, Lieutenant Hayase. I overstepped."

I had been seen through by Lieutenant Hayase.

Rather than making excuses, I decided it was better to acknowledge it and apologize.

Just then, Instructor Kagami, who had been watching the training, approached us.

"Mayaya, Hifumi Ren, you both seem quite relaxed. As a special punishment, an additional three hundred reps for you. If you can chat, you should be fine, right?"

I was indeed reprimanded for talking.

I was aware that it was shameful behavior on my part, but I couldn't defy Lieutenant Hayase when she directed her attention to me.

However, Lieutenant Hayase didn't seem too bothered.

"Talking actually increases the load, you know. Besides, this is just a warm-up."

"...An additional two hundred for you. I’m curious to see if you and Hifumi Ren's subordinates can handle it."

With that, Instructor Kagami walked away, and the three new recruits behind us looked despondent.

Collective responsibility... Because we were in the same squad, I had dragged them into our punishment.

It was already a tough training session, and now they had to do five hundred more reps than the others, which was only natural.

Moreover, the training wouldn't end there.

I was filled with guilt and remorse, but Lieutenant Hayase was different.

She glanced back at the recruits and let out a small sigh.

"We're lacking in stamina."

Even though we were both Valkyries, I was astonished at the stark difference between an ace and a regular soldier.

While we were being given extra reps, Lieutenant Seiryuu was shooting us a cold glare from nearby.

◇

Once the stamina training finally ended, Megumi was fuming in the locker room.

After that, Maya had spoken to Ren, resulting in us being made to do additional reps for every exercise.

Megumi was barely managing to stand with her trembling body.

"That girl is really pushing it... Just the stamina training has left my body in tatters."

During the training period, Valkyries were subjected to rigorous training.

Moreover, there were various other training sessions lined up afterward.

They spent almost the entire day training to maintain a body capable of withstanding real combat.

Sitting on a bench in the locker room, Kanako hung her head, looking pale enough to vomit at any moment.

"It's strange that Hifumi, who isn't even a Valkyrie, is handling this with a straight face... ugh."

"Don't throw up here."

Sitting on the same bench, Mami also seemed to be reaching her limit just from the stamina training.

Megumi pounded her trembling hand against the locker, but it made little noise.

"That girl is mostly Gijuu, so she's fine! Besides, Hayase is the real monster. She handled the extra reps with a cool face."

While they were suffering because of Maya, Mami was thinking more rationally.

"That's what top-tier stamina looks like. It's clear that the Fifth Academy is on a different level."

"That doesn't matter! I will never forgive her!"

"What do you plan to do about that?"

Mami knew Megumi was rebellious toward Maya, but she looked puzzled about what could actually be done.

They were the newcomers, while the other was an ace.

It seemed she wanted to avoid any foolish acts of revenge against a superior opponent.

However, Megumi had a surefire plan.

"I'll use her weakness against her."

Kanako lifted her head.

"Weakness? Does she even have one?"

Megumi pulled out her device from the locker.

"Just watch. I'll make it so she can't defy me."

As Megumi said this, the battalion commander, Kuushima Mai, entered the locker room.

Mai, who oversaw Class Three, seemed to have come to check on the newcomers.

"So you're still here. Hurry up and change, or you'll be late for the next training session."

With a kind demeanor, she spoke to the inexperienced newcomers, but Megumi showed her the screen of her device.

"Chairwoman Kuushima, there's something I want you to see."

"What is it? ...Oh."

Mai appeared interested in the image Megumi showed her.

Feeling a sense of accomplishment, Megumi requested a transfer to a different squad.

"I'm going on a date with a guy on my day off. This isn't allowed, right? I would prefer to be assigned to a proper squad rather than being with a delinquent ace like her. Is there anything you can do about it?"

Megumi's actions stemmed from Maya's isolation in Class Three.

Even as an ace, it was clear she had no allies within Class Three.

Mai was aware that Maya had been stripped of her ace position and, believing she held a personal grudge, Megumi provided the information.

The deal was set... or so Megumi thought.

Mai turned her gaze to the two sitting on the bench behind Megumi.

"Are you two involved in this as well?"

Kanako and Mami seemed to have grasped the content of the image from Megumi's words.

However, perhaps not wanting to get involved, they desperately shook their heads.

Mai smiled upon seeing this.

"Good. I'm glad it's not just a bunch of foolish newcomers doing something stupid."

"...Huh?"

Megumi was taken aback by Mai's unexpected reaction.

Mai leaned in and spoke to Megumi in a low voice.

"I'm not interested in someone who sells out their comrades. Neither I nor the others want a traitor."

"But, Chairwoman, Maya was stripped of her ace position—"

"—That is an undeniable fact, but personal matters should not interfere with unit operations. This is not the Fifth Academy. This is Boots Cat—Class Three."

As Megumi's gaze wandered, Mai shot her a cold glare.

"Do your best to earn Hayase's recognition. If not, you'll revert to being a candidate."

With that, Mai left the locker room.
