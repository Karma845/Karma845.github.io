Chapter 2: Formation of the Hayase Team?

After leaving the Third Academy classroom, we moved to the back of the school building.

When we were told to come to the back, the three others besides me wore uncertain expressions, but upon arrival, we found a beautiful area with flower beds and benches prepared.

Lieutenant Hayase, sitting on a bench with her legs crossed, shot us a sharp look.

"Let's start with self-introductions."

We were puzzled at the mention of self-introductions.

After all, we had just done that in the classroom.

Noticing the three girls exchanging confused glances, I decided to ask on their behalf.

"Lieutenant Hayase, may I ask a question?"

"Go ahead."

She replied immediately, but for some reason, the three girls looked at me with cold eyes.

Ignoring their stares, I continued with my question.

"Since we already introduced ourselves in the classroom, should we assume that the self-introduction here has a different meaning?"

Lieutenant Hayase seemed to understand my intent, but it appeared she was dissatisfied with the introductions from the classroom.

She was concerned about the lack of information.

"What can you learn just from hearing names? I wasn't even interested enough to listen to the introductions."

"Well, that's..."

"The only one I wanted as my direct subordinate was you. I had some information I could gather beforehand, but I didn't have time to look into any unnecessary details. So, if you have preferences regarding combat style or positioning within the team, just say it."

Lieutenant Hayase didn't bother hiding her true feelings, and the three girls reacted differently to being told they were of no interest to her.

Asai-san seemed slightly annoyed but calmly introduced herself.

"I'm Asai Mami. I've been serving as a mid-range fighter in my previous squad. I hope to continue in that role, but I'm open to changes if the battalion commander or squad leader has other intentions."

Lieutenant Hayase directed a cold gaze at Asai-san's introduction.

"What’s your weapon?"

"…I use two Gatling guns as sub-arms. I also wield a spear."

"Sounds like a combat style that someone who wants to maneuver skillfully in mid-range would like. Do you have any other preferred weapons?"

"N-no, not really."

"Oh, I see."

After that, Lieutenant Hayase lost interest and shifted her gaze to the next girl.

"Next, you over there, looking suspicious."

Nishitani-san, who had been flustered after hearing Asai-san's introduction, was called out.

She reacted with a start, her body jerking in surprise, but she managed to introduce herself in a trembling voice.

"I-I'm Nishitani Kanako. I used to be a front-line fighter, and I-I-I like large rifles and great swords... um, I like other weapons too..."

Seeing Nishitani-san's wandering gaze, Lieutenant Hayase quickly cut her off.

"You're lacking confidence. Next."

The third was Suzuki-san.

Having had time to think after hearing the other two's introductions, Suzuki-san appeared calm.

"I'm Suzuki Megumi. I used various weapons during my time as a candidate, but my favorite is the large lance I use as a sub-arm. I also hope to be on the front line, but I can convert to any role."

"Hmm."

Lieutenant Hayase observed Suzuki-san carefully.

Unlike the other two, Suzuki-san seemed to take this attention positively.

"By the way, I'm thrilled to be part of Lieutenant Hayase's team! I'll do my utmost, so please guide me!"

Suzuki-san didn't stop at just introducing herself; she expressed her admiration for Lieutenant Hayase.

There was a hint of her desire to be liked by Lieutenant Hayase in her demeanor.

While Suzuki-san seemed satisfied with her cheerful greeting, Lieutenant Hayase's response was cold.

"Do your best? What are you misunderstanding?"

"…Huh?"

As Suzuki-san froze, Lieutenant Hayase bluntly stated her opinion.

"Doing your best is a given here; it doesn't even warrant evaluation. What I'm asking is what results you can provide."

"Uh, well… I mean… support…"

"Is support your job? Must be nice and easy."

As Suzuki-san's gaze wandered, Lieutenant Hayase smirked slightly.

"There are those who are all talk. Besides, why should I have to teach you? What were you doing in the middle school or in the Fifth Academy?"

Ignoring Suzuki-san, who looked pale and silent after being belittled, Lieutenant Hayase turned her attention to me.

"Next… Hifumi."

Unable to voice my opinion against Lieutenant Hayase's approach, I began my self-introduction as requested.

"I am Corporal Hifumi Ren! I am a test pilot for the Prometheus Project, and unlike everyone else, I will be piloting a humanoid weapon. The data on operation and coordination is still limited, and we are in the trial-and-error stage, so I am not at a hopeful stage yet. Also, since humanoid weapons involve many confidential matters, there are restrictions on the information I can disclose, but I have been informed by the development team that the Thunderbolt will continue to use large-caliber armaments."

As for the weaponry of the humanoid weapon, I wasn't particularly skilled, having only used what was sent to me last time.

I had some confidence in dual-wielding daggers, but considering the burden it placed on the machine during the last battle, I might be prohibited from using them.

There was little I could say at this point.

I thought I would be reprimanded by Lieutenant Hayase for this, but it turned out differently than I expected.

Lieutenant Hayase nodded a few times, wearing a satisfied expression.

"I personally love humanoid robots. I’d like to observe, so if possible, could you get permission from the development team?"

"…Yes! Understood!"

Though I felt a bit blindsided, if Lieutenant Hayase was satisfied, I had no intention of pursuing it further.

However, the remaining three seemed dissatisfied with this treatment.

Suzuki-san glared at me and raised her voice.

"Why is this guy getting special treatment?! He should be scolded like the rest of us!"

As Suzuki-san spoke up, Lieutenant Hayase crossed her arms and looked displeased.

While I contemplated whether to intervene, Lieutenant Hayase struck at Suzuki-san's sore spot.

"Who was it that charged too far into the front lines during the last battle? Because of that, you got jealous of the one who risked their life to save you… You really are small-minded, in every sense."

"Wha—?!"

Ignoring Suzuki-san, who was left dumbfounded by being called small, Lieutenant Hayase stood up.

"Alright then, let's disband for now. We'll meet in the classroom a week from now."

As Lieutenant Hayase walked away, Asai-san hurriedly called out a question.

"Are we really disbanding like this?! You were supposed to provide us with training for the newcomers!"

She tried to stop Lieutenant Hayase, who seemed ready to abandon the training she was assigned.

However, Lieutenant Hayase had her reasons.

"That training will be decided after the break. We just finished actual combat, and the Third Academy is on break too. You all are on break, so make sure to rest your bodies while you can."

With that, she turned her back and started walking away.

We were left standing there for a while, but suddenly, Suzuki-san burst out laughing.

"Haha… Hahaha! What the heck was that woman?! She’s so obviously favoring this guy!"

Perhaps sensing the danger of Suzuki-san's outburst, Nishitani-san stepped in to stop her.

"You shouldn't say that about an ace. If she dislikes you, you'll be sent back to the Fifth Academy."

"If I have to fight under someone like her, I'd rather be sent back and assigned to another battalion! And you, you’re lucky, huh? You must be thrilled to be favored."

I understood that Suzuki-san's anger was directed at me, but it wasn't something I could be happy about just because I was being favored.

"No, I'm not happy about it."

"Really? You’re just trying to curry favor with her."

As Suzuki-san said that and left the scene, the other two glanced at me a few times before walking away as well.

Though they didn't voice it, they seemed to share the same sentiment.

Left alone, I couldn't shake the feeling that the squad wouldn't function properly in its current state.

"…I’ve been assigned to the Third Academy, but I’m starting off with a break. It’s a shame I couldn’t at least receive a training menu for future reference."

I had thought the newcomer training would start, but being told we were on break caught me off guard.

◇

Having been assigned to the Third Academy, I came to the hangar where the Prometheus Project was being conducted, realizing there would be no training for newcomers.

Here, a plan was underway to turn humanoid weapons that had previously been unable to handle Gijuu into weapons against them, with male pilots.

It seemed to be a highly significant project for humanity, but the development site was temporarily set up in a corner of the academy grounds, which was also referred to as an aerial fortress.

When I returned to the development site, Dr. Allison Green, the deputy chief, noticed me and turned her face toward me.

Dr. Allison was a woman with long blonde hair tied up messily, who wore a lab coat while working.

She was diligent, but occasionally showed concern for me, who was treated like a guinea pig.

"You’re back quite early. Did you get kicked out of the Third Academy?"

She seemed worried that I had been expelled due to resistance from the Valkyries assigned to the combat unit.

From behind Dr. Allison, a short man with a distinctive mushroom-shaped hairstyle appeared.

Dr. John Smith, the chief developer and the oldest here, had a demeanor that made him seem much younger than his actual age.

He was also the one who had implanted limbs and organs made from Gijuu cells into my body.

He was a key figure in the Prometheus Project.

"That would be a problem. The upper management thought you would be assigned to the Valkyrie unit. They even promoted you accordingly, so if you got expelled, it would complicate things."

It was rare for Dr. Smith to express concern for me, so I decided to clarify the misunderstanding.

"No, that's not it. The Third Academy is going on break, so the newcomer training will take place after the break. Today was just a meet-and-greet."

Upon hearing my explanation, Dr. Smith sighed in relief.

"That's a relief. I was worried that if you got expelled after the Thunderbolt was deployed to the combat unit, the additional budget would vanish."

It seemed that his concern was less about me personally and more about the budget that had been added in relation to me.

Dr. Allison sighed deeply upon hearing Dr. Smith's words.

Though she was involved in the project as the deputy chief, most of her work revolved around looking after Dr. Smith.

She was an important figure herself, but if Dr. Smith was left unattended, it would lead to various complications, making her a hardworking individual.

"Regardless of the budget issue, I'm just glad you weren't expelled on your first day."

I sensed something odd in Dr. Allison's reaction.

"…That's strange."

"What is?"

"I thought you would say that the Thunderbolt's deployment for actual combat was premature."

When I voiced my doubt, Dr. Smith joined the conversation, having had the same thought.

"That's certainly true. I thought Dr. Allison would resist, but she accepted your case without hesitation. Is it really because of the additional budget?"

Was she only accepting my assignment because she wanted the extra budget? Dr. Allison's cold reaction suggested she didn't appreciate that implication.

"…Don't compare me to Dr. Smith. I simply judged that the Thunderbolt was ready for actual combat, and that's why I approved its assignment to the combat unit."

It seemed Dr. Allison had her own investment in the Prometheus Project, as she gazed at the Thunderbolt, surrounded by maintenance personnel in the center of the hangar.

The Thunderbolt, secured by arms, had its non-functional parts being removed, leaving only the head and torso.

Maintenance personnel were working around the clock to repair the armor plates on the head and torso.

As I looked at the Thunderbolt, I apologized to the two of them.

"I'm sorry for damaging such an important machine."

Dr. Smith was the first to speak up.

He had both hands stuffed in the pockets of his lab coat, looking pleased.

"It's a shame it got damaged, but I think it's a plus because we gained valuable combat data. Besides, at this point, the pilot is more valuable than the machine, and I’d like to express my gratitude that you returned safely."

Dr. Smith's joy at my return stemmed from the fact that they couldn't prepare another test pilot.

This was a major issue for the Prometheus Project, as the militarization of males—allowing them to wield magical power—was incredibly difficult.

To handle magical power, limbs and organs made from Gijuu had to be implanted, but the success rate for such surgeries was only about ten percent.

Even if successful, few could endure the intense pain of rehabilitation, and without both magical power and pilot suitability, they were useless.

There were many barriers to the militarization of males.

Dr. Allison began to explain the upper management's intentions to me.

"You don't need to worry about the machine. Most people are happy that humanoid weapons have proven effective against Gijuu. While you might be an unwelcome guest here, some in upper management have high expectations for you."

"I will do my best to meet those expectations, even if my power is limited—"

I was about to respond with that, but suddenly, Lieutenant Hayase's words echoed in my mind.

"Doing your best is a given and doesn't even warrant evaluation."

Noticing my hesitation, Dr. Allison seemed curious but didn't pursue the matter.

"Anyway, from now on, you'll be deploying with the Boots Cat squad. Soon, parts and armaments for the Thunderbolt will be delivered one after another."

I had something I wanted to confirm with them regarding that matter, so I turned my body and straightened my posture.

"I have a question regarding the Thunderbolt's armaments."

"What is it? If you have a weapon you want to try, just make a list later, and we can consider it."

"No, what I want to confirm is about armaments capable of defeating a Special Class."

Currently, it would be difficult for the Thunderbolt to face a Special Class… rather, it would be challenging.

In perfect condition, it might be able to evade, but it didn't possess any armaments capable of delivering effective blows.

This was partly due to the magical output, for which I bore some responsibility, but I didn't expect a rapid increase in magical power at this point.

—If that were the case, I would have to prepare a different solution.

It seemed Dr. Smith had already been considering this.

"What you’re asking is whether there are weapons that you and the Thunderbolt can operate to defeat a Special Class, right?"

"Yes."

Dr. Smith's expression turned serious.

"Compared to the Valkyries, your magical output isn't high. The Thunderbolt's performance is the same. Compared to the Valkyrie Dress, it’s a bigger target for the Gijuu."

After Dr. Allison glanced at Dr. Smith, she turned her gaze to me.

"Upper management has presented the next objective. As you seem to be concerned, defeating a Special Class with the Thunderbolt will be a future challenge."

"I thought so."

Gijuu were classified by strength and threat level, with lower numbers being simply stronger.

The Valkyries, recognized as combatants, usually faced Special Class Gijuu, while even candidate Valkyries could handle Second Class ones.

In other words, for the Thunderbolt to be recognized as a combat asset, it needed to defeat at least a Special Class.

If it was deemed only capable of handling Second Class Gijuu, the Prometheus Project would likely be halted due to cost-effectiveness concerns.

It seemed Dr. Allison was troubled by this.

"Humanoid weapons are expensive. If we invest this much budget and can only handle Second Class Gijuu, the opposition will seize the opportunity to stop the project. We need to be able to face at least a Special Class, or we’ll need a significant improvement in budget."

When discussing budget matters, Dr. Allison shot a resentful glance at Dr. Smith.

She likely wanted him to consider cost-effectiveness instead of spending the budget like water.

Dr. Smith, however, was smiling cheerfully.

"Once the production system is established, costs will naturally go down. More importantly, regarding weapons capable of facing a Special Class… I’ve been thinking about it, but it’s a bit challenging."

"Even for you, Dr. Smith?"

"It’s possible to defeat them, but considering operational aspects, it’s difficult. In any case, this is a challenge we need to solve, so you should take a break. You’re now a member of the Third Academy. If the others are resting, you can rest too, within limits that won’t disrupt the plan."

As Dr. Smith returned to work, Dr. Allison began explaining future matters to me.

"Since the Thunderbolt is in that state, there won’t be any test pilot work for a while. As long as you complete the training menu, you can do as you please afterward."

"Yes! Understood!"

I saluted, but Dr. Allison didn't even look back as she walked away.

◇

As a test pilot for the Prometheus Project and a member of the Boots Cat battalion, what do I need to become suitable?

The answer is simple.

No matter what unit you go to, you need physical strength as a foundation, and it’s also important to train the reflexes and judgment required for a pilot.

In other words… training.

"Alright!"

The development team had accommodations near the hangar.

It was just a place with container houses lined up, but I had a private room as a test pilot.

Many among the development team expressed dissatisfaction, but I was grateful for having a private room, even if it was small.

Until now, I had shared large rooms with my comrades as an infantryman.

Thinking back to my former squadmates made me feel a bit lonely, but being alone was comfortable and allowed for no reservations, as MC and Hair Set often said.

They often lamented wanting to use a private room like the captain.

They said they wanted a private room to focus on drawing comics.

If those three knew about my situation, they would surely be envious.

…Recalling the past, I lightly shook my head to switch my mindset.

On the wall of my minimally furnished room, I had posted a paper listing the training menu I needed to complete during this break.

The schedule was filled with various training plans from morning to night, and many had diagonal lines drawn through them.

Each line marked completion, leaving only the final day's menu remaining.

"This break has been quite meaningful."

With no test pilot duties or training with the Valkyries, I felt satisfied having successfully completed the training menu I prepared.

"Only today’s final day remains."

Just as I was about to start training, I noticed some commotion outside.

It seemed someone from the development team was talking near my container.

"This will be the lieutenant's room."

"Oh, I see. Thank you."

I heard male and female voices.

The female voice was particularly familiar, and I was startled, hurriedly trying to open the door.

However, before I could, the door was kicked open.

"Hifumi, what the hell are you doing?!"

The person who burst into my room was Lieutenant Hayase, dressed in casual clothes.

I was momentarily confused since she wasn't in her usual uniform, but I quickly straightened my posture.

Lieutenant Hayase seemed displeased and wore a stern expression.

It appeared I was the cause of her frustration, but I had no idea why.

"Lieutenant Hayase!? I thought today was a day off!"

The only possibility I could think of was that I had mistakenly thought the break period was over.

However, I had confirmed it multiple times beforehand, so I shouldn’t have been wrong.

Lieutenant Hayase approached me with large strides, looking up at my face.

We were only a few centimeters apart.

"What are you doing during your time off?"

"…Training, ma'am."

"Why? It’s your day off, right?"

"Yes, I was training to make the most of my time off."

"Huh?"

For some reason, Lieutenant Hayase and I were not on the same wavelength in our conversation.

It seemed she shared my confusion, as she stepped back and began to grumble.

During this, she glanced at the training menu I had posted on the wall.

"I thought you were hiding away, but you’re just holed up training? You can’t rest your body if you train every day."

It seemed my judgment had been mistaken.

I asked Lieutenant Hayase what I had done wrong.

"What did I do wrong? If you could, I would appreciate your guidance."

After pondering my words for a moment, Lieutenant Hayase's face lit up with a thought.

It was a rather delighted expression.

"In that case, let me teach you how to spend your day off. You are my subordinate, so it shouldn't be a problem."

"Huh? But I was about to train…"

"…That’s an order from your superior."

As Lieutenant Hayase issued her command, I reflexively saluted.

It didn’t matter that it was a day off.

"…Yes! Understood!"

"See? You should have said that from the start. Honestly, what a troublesome subordinate."

…She was angry just a moment ago, but why was she suddenly so cheerful?

I couldn’t figure out the reason behind Lieutenant Hayase's change in attitude.

MC and Hair Set had said that women’s feelings were complex and mysterious, and I was currently experiencing that firsthand.
