Chapter 11: Valkyries of the Boots Cat

As I waited inside the transport aircraft, dressed in my pilot suit, Dr. Allison hurriedly boarded with the development staff.

"Dr. Allison?"

"We're deploying. A reinforcement request has come from the Second Academy."

"...Understood."

I never thought Lieutenant Hayase's intuition would actually be right.

Dr. Allison's expression was more serious than usual.

"Also, a Special Class has been confirmed. It seems the Second Academy is engaging it, but there's a possibility of combat if things go poorly."

"Special Class!?"

I instinctively lifted myself from my chair.

Special Classes are stronger than First Classes and cause significant damage.

There have been numerous instances where a Special Class appeared in a city and was blown away without a trace.

I had seen the materials about them many times during training.

Dr. Allison tossed me a tablet and instructed me to review the information.

"It's a massive Gijuu known as a Wide Area Destruction Type. There's a chance we might have to deal with it, so make sure to memorize the details. Also, keep an eye on the battlefield situation."

I had memorized the information about the Toto Dino during my training.

It's a giant, disc-shaped Gijuu, protected by a hard outer shell on top.

It attacks by firing lasers and beams from the bottom, and it's a Special Class that is simply large and extremely difficult to destroy.

However, as I checked, I found that there was no detailed information about the battlefield.

It seemed true that the Second Academy was in dire straits due to enemy reinforcements, but I couldn't glean anything more.

After finishing my review, I returned the tablet to Dr. Allison.

"I'll wait in the cockpit."

"Please do. And I'm sorry, but this time it will be a trial by fire again. Be careful with the new equipment, especially since we’ve only done test firing so far, and we don't know how it will perform in actual combat."

"Understood."

As I responded, Dr. Allison turned her back to me.

"...Come back safely. Without you, the Prometheus Project can't progress."

I felt that Dr. Allison was genuinely concerned for me in her own way.

To respond to that feeling, I saluted.

"If it comes down to it, I'll return with just my two daggers."

I expressed my determination to survive, but this statement backfired.

Dr. Allison turned to me, her brow furrowed.

"I can accept that if the situation is unavoidable, but I won't allow you to abandon the new equipment just because you're more comfortable with familiar weapons. That weapon is extremely delicate, and if it's discarded in the sea, it won't be usable even if recovered. Understand? I won't accept anything other than unavoidable circumstances."

It was a weapon developed with a significant budget aimed at defeating First Classes.

It wouldn't sit well with Dr. Allison if it were easily discarded.

"...I'll do my best."

However, in actual combat, anything could happen.

Not wanting to make a rash promise, I gave a vague answer, which only made Dr. Allison's expression more severe.

"I'll ultimately leave it to the pilot's judgment, but if you abandon it, you'll be writing a report as thick as a dictionary, so be prepared."

"...Yes, ma'am."

For a moment, I was overwhelmed by Dr. Allison's intensity and unintentionally responded in a foreign manner.

I recalled that the MC had said the same thing when he angered a female officer.

Among them, Megumi was also changing into her pilot suit.

Without speaking to anyone, she was battling the fear of standing on the front lines as a true Valkyrie.

(If I had known it would come to this, I should have retired sooner.)

In a situation where she felt isolated within the unit, her survival rate would drastically decrease.

At worst, there was even a possibility of being shot from behind by other Valkyries who disliked her.

Though she had sown the seeds of this situation herself, Megumi couldn't help but feel terrified about deploying.

(I thought I could become an elite by becoming a true Valkyrie.)

As her hands trembled and she struggled to change into her pilot suit, Maya passed by her without even glancing at her.

"Doesn't she even notice me? If only that person..."

Despairing at the indifference of her training officer, Megumi began to tremble with anger when Suzuka came over and wrapped her arm around her shoulder.

Suzuka pulled Megumi closer to her.

"Hey, traitor."

"W-What is it?"

Suzuka shot a cold glare at Megumi, who looked frightened.

"Don't do anything stupid. If you shoot Hayase from behind, I'll shoot you down immediately."

"W-Wait, aren't you supposed to hate each other?"

"Yeah, I do. I absolutely hate Hayase. I can't stand that she took the ace position and the Special Equipment from Mai. But... she's our ace."

There was no lie in Suzuka's words.

As Megumi nodded small in response, Suzuka released her.

"Don't worry about what's behind you. We won't do anything stupid like shooting a foolish newcomer from behind. Just focus on fighting ahead."

With that, Suzuka left the locker room with her subordinates.

Before she knew it, Megumi's trembling hands had stopped.

Looking at her hands, Megumi said,

"What’s this? There's a sense of camaraderie after all."

On the first day of their meeting, they had shown their animosity, so she thought Maya would be isolated in actual combat as well.

But in reality, the Boots Cat trusted Maya when it came to fighting.

After finishing changing, Megumi left the locker room.

"You heard that, right? We're heading to rescue the Second Academy. And unfortunately, it seems we don't have the luxury of letting the newcomers play around. Asai, Nishitani, and Suzuki will be left to Hayase."

Maya, who was entrusted with them, looked slightly dissatisfied.

"I think it would be better to guard the transport aircraft. I have my robot, so I don't need to worry about support."

She protested, but Kagami wouldn't budge.

"The development team for that robot has contacted us, expressing concerns about its armament. It's an order. Think of taking care of the newcomers as part of their training."

"Okay..."

Maya responded with a lack of enthusiasm, turning her gaze to the three nervous newcomers.

"Just don't get in my way."

That was hardly the words of someone entrusted with training newcomers.

Seeing this, Kagami let out a small sigh and turned her gaze to Mio.

Mio shook her head in concern and tightened her expression.

"Alright, everyone onboard! We're going in to rescue the Gringarm Team!"

Maya complied with Mio's command, but let out an exaggerated sigh.

"...Mio's encouragement is so deflating."

Maya inadvertently revealed her true feelings, and the other Valkyries subtly agreed.

As everyone donned their Valkyrie Dresses, Kagami approached Maya and spoke.

"Maya, your attendant has already entered deployment preparation. Once you go outside, gather the three of them and join up."

"Understood. It's my first time fighting alongside them, but I expect great things."

As Maya, now equipped with the Special Equipment Valkyrie Dress, said this, Kagami looked at the three newcomers who were nervously struggling to put on their equipment.

"Take care of the other three as well. If you favor them too much, he might attract jealousy from others and become uncomfortable."

Receiving Kagami's advice, Maya smiled faintly.

"Those three are different from him, Kyou-chan."

"...I see. Do as you like."

Kagami realized that anything she said would be in vain and moved to help the bewildered newcomers.
