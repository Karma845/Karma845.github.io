Chapter 9: Standby

The training period for Class Three had come to an end.

Outside, it was already dark, and the Valkyries, freed from their training, were changing into their uniforms in the locker room.

The scent of deodorant and other products lingered in the air, and groups had formed among the same squad.

Sasaki Suzuka, the vice president, was surrounded by her subordinates from the same squad.

"Suzuka-san, here’s a sports drink!"

"Thanks! ...Ahh! That really hits the spot!"

Having undergone quite intense training, the atmosphere in the locker room was bright now that they were released from it.

"I feel so much better knowing that we’ll be back to normal starting tomorrow."

"Huh? Where’s Hayase?"

"She probably went to her favorite attendant, right? She changed quickly and left."

"That was fast!?"

The girls, who had been quiet during the training period, were now naturally chatty, albeit still exhausted.

Except for one.

Suzuki Megumi, the newcomer assigned to Class Three.

Even though she had been freed from the rigorous training compared to Class Five, her expression was far from good.

Concerned for her was Nishitani Kanako, another newcomer.

While changing, Kanako peeked at Megumi's condition.

"Um, Megumi-chan? Starting tomorrow, we’ll be in standby, so training and classes will be more relaxed. I heard we’ll have some free time too. Want to go out somewhere?"

Standby meant being on standby for deployment as a reserve for other classes.

In case a Gijuu of a scale that a single class couldn’t handle appeared, they would be on standby to deploy as reinforcements.

Starting tomorrow, Class Two would enter their standby period, making Class Three the reserve.

Class Four would be on vacation, while Class One would be in training.

This cycle was established.

During standby, training would still occur alongside regular classes, but there would be more leeway compared to the training period.

After school, they could act freely, and a day off was also prepared.

However, for emergencies, leaving the academy was not permitted.

Thus, Kanako had invited Megumi to hang out within the academy.

Megumi glanced at Kanako but didn’t respond, ignoring her.

"Megumi-chan?"

"Just drop it, Nishitani-san."

It was Asai Mami, another newcomer, who stopped Kanako from inviting her.

Having finished changing, she wore a somewhat relieved yet unsociable expression, as the training period had ended safely.

Though she possessed a body stronger than an average person as a Valkyrie, her limbs were slightly trembling now.

It seemed the training for the combat unit had been tough for Mami as well, and she looked like she wanted to return to her room and lie down immediately.

Mami had a reason for stopping Kanako.

"You know Megumi’s evaluation, right? If Hayase-san decides she’s unnecessary, she’ll be sent back to Class Five. After that, she’ll be branded as someone who was kicked out of Class Three, and the chances of her being pulled into another class are zero... She won’t be able to rise again."

"B-but..."

"She’s already finished as a Valkyrie. Worrying about her is pointless."

Within Class Three, Megumi’s evaluation had plummeted.

As proof, when Suzuka and the others finished changing and passed by the three, they called out to them.

"You two seem to have survived those hellish days, which is a relief. I thought at least one of you would collapse and end up in the hospital, but you’ve got some guts. I like you, so I’ll treat you to dinner tonight. I’ll be waiting outside, so hurry up!"

Both Kanako and Mami were called out, but Megumi was ignored.

Indeed, while Maya was disliked in Class Three, Megumi’s betrayal by trying to sell out her superior was viewed as problematic.

Megumi was in a situation where it wouldn’t be surprising if she were sent back to Class Five at any moment.

Hastily, Mami urged the flustered Kanako to change, and the two left the locker room.

Before long, Megumi was left alone.

Once alone, Megumi pressed her forehead against the locker and let her tears flow.

"...I finally got assigned, and look at what I’m doing."

She regretted her reckless actions.

◇

Life in Class Three after the training period had ended was not much different from when she was in Class Five.

The girls continued to come to school every morning, attending classes and training.

Just because they were on standby, it felt like their daily lives had returned.

Thanks to that, the newcomers under Hayase Lieutenant’s care became more pronounced.

Two of them seemed fine, but the remaining one was under considerable pressure.

After school, I was called to the fenced basketball court outside, where I was accompanying Hayase Lieutenant.

She was practicing her shooting, and I was picking up the balls and throwing them back.

The balls arced beautifully into the net, producing a satisfying sound repeatedly.

Watching Hayase Lieutenant sink shot after shot without hitting the rim, I decided to bring up Suzuki-san.

"Suzuki-san seems to be isolated."

As I tossed the ball back, Hayase Lieutenant responded curtly.

"Yeah... Shh! Twenty in a row! I’m feeling good today."

Uninterested, Hayase Lieutenant didn’t seem to care much about my observation, so I shared the rumors regarding her treatment.

"People in class seem to think she’ll be sent back to Class Five."

As I said that, Hayase Lieutenant made her twenty-first shot.

She turned to me with a skeptical look as I picked up the ball and tossed it back.

"I’m surprised you could even talk to the class."

"I overheard it during break."

"I don’t see the need to eavesdrop on trivial conversations. Besides, it’s up to me to decide Suzuki’s future since I’m in charge of training the newcomers... Ugh, this is frustrating."

Her next shot hit the rim but still went in.

However, Hayase Lieutenant deemed it a failure.

Receiving the ball from me, she began spinning it on her fingertips.

"What do you plan to do with Suzuki-san?"

With no other way to phrase it, I asked directly, and Hayase Lieutenant gave a wry smile.

"You really need to work on your phrasing. Well, it’s fine. Let’s see... before I can make a decision, Suzuki will probably break down and run away."

She implied that it didn’t even require her judgment.

Given Suzuki-san’s current state, it wouldn’t be surprising if she left Class Three on her own.

"I don’t intend to help... No, never mind."

I had asked about the reason for Suzuki-san’s isolation, but it seemed her betrayal against Hayase Lieutenant was the cause.

In such a situation, I couldn’t imagine Hayase Lieutenant would help her, but she still answered my question dutifully.

"Helping or not, my duty is to train the newcomers. I have no obligation to take care of their personal lives."

She had helped me when I was in trouble, didn’t she? I thought, but I didn’t argue back.

It seemed my expression had given me away.

Hayase Lieutenant shot me a disgruntled look.

"Just so you know, I only helped you because I dislike Louise. Don’t get the wrong idea."

"I didn’t say anything."

"Your face said otherwise."

"I wasn’t aware of it. I’ll be more mindful from now on."

"Talking to you throws me off my game."

As Hayase Lieutenant began practicing dribbling, I informed her about our upcoming plans.

"Hayase Lieutenant, actually, adjustments for the new equipment will start tomorrow. After school, I—"

I was about to say I wouldn’t be able to join her, but Hayase Lieutenant casually interrupted.

"You don’t have to worry about that. You can prioritize that. Personally, I’m curious about what new equipment the robot will have. Additional equipment means a power-up flag."

She referred to the humanoid weapon as a robot and called new equipment a power-up flag... Come to think of it, before entering the training facility, I used to watch a lot of superhero shows.

I liked the flow where heroes would prepare new techniques or weapons to defeat strong enemies.

"I can’t provide details, but it’s true that the additional equipment will serve as a power-up flag."

"Wait, really?"

Hayase Lieutenant, who had stopped dribbling, held the ball with sparkling eyes.

"I’ve prepared the most suitable weapons for defeating a first-class target."

Currently, the Prometheus Project had been deployed in actual combat and had achieved the feat of defeating a second-class target.

In the future, improvements would be made from both hardware and software perspectives regarding the efficient use of magical power in humanoid weapons.

From a useless toy to a toy with potential utility—this was, according to Dr. Allison, the current evaluation of Thunderbolt.

The next goal for the development team was to aim for defeating a first-class Gijuu, moving beyond being just a toy.

Hayase Lieutenant looked a bit disappointed.

"Isn’t that just a realistic enhancement plan? Isn’t there a new robot coming or a fusion to make it stronger?"

"I think that’s a bit too much to hope for. Adding optional parts seems more realistic than fusion."

While the Prometheus Project was reportedly more anticipated than before, it seemed there was no budget allocated for preparing a new model.

It looked like they would continue to improve and use the existing Thunderbolt.

"By the way, Hayase Lieutenant, you really like heroes and robots, don’t you?"

"I played with boys when I was a kid. It’s a shame I haven’t been able to find any like-minded individuals since coming here."

"I haven’t watched superhero shows in a while, so I can’t keep up with your core discussions."

I used to enjoy them as a child, but I lost the opportunity to watch them.

Having been away for too long, I couldn’t relate to Hayase Lieutenant.

She looked a bit disappointed and lonely, but she masked it by shooting again.

"Well, it’s not something I’m forcing on you. If you feel like it, give it a watch."

The ball swished through the net beautifully.

As I watched Hayase Lieutenant walk over to retrieve the ball, I couldn’t help but think.

If she showed more of her true self, she might not have been isolated in class.

If she could support the newcomers like she did when she helped me, the training would go smoothly.

It was frustrating that she had the potential but lacked the motivation.

◇

In the hangar used for the Prometheus Project, Allison was checking the new equipment for Thunderbolt.

The mechanics were assembling the new gear, and she was overseeing them as the site supervisor.

"Be careful with the rails. How’s the power supply coming along?"

The mechanic, who was preparing to connect to Thunderbolt, shook his head and crossed his arms.

"As for energy, we can only prepare cables for the connection with the magic power converter."

The size of the new equipment was comparable to the entire length of Thunderbolt, at least in terms of length.

If it were to be operated, it would likely be difficult to handle.

Additionally, the element of magical power was a problem.

Allison placed her hand on her forehead, expressing her dissatisfaction to the higher-ups.

"It would have been easier if we had ordered from the developers of the Valkyrie Dress, but they’re being stubborn and trying to prepare it themselves, causing issues on-site."

The new equipment was provided by a company separate from the one developing the Valkyries’ gear.

Since it was a weapon that utilized magical power, it would have been better to rely on a company with proven results... It was only natural for the site to be dissatisfied.

At that moment, Dr. Smith appeared with Ren and countered her opinion.

"That’s a valid point, but conversely, the companies developing the Valkyries’ weapons aren’t familiar with handling humanoid weapons. There are pros and cons to both sides."

When Allison turned around, Dr. Smith was resting his chin on his hand, looking at the new equipment that was being assembled.

From a distance, he looked like a gigantic mushroom, hunched over and peering in.

While Allison was lost in such trivial thoughts, Dr. Smith straightened up and scratched his head.

"It looks too big to handle."

Agreeing with Dr. Smith’s sentiment, Allison began discussing operational concerns.

"It’s too large even for a humanoid weapon to handle. When in use, it will need to connect directly to the capacitor mounted on the back for energy supply."

Dr. Smith nodded and turned to Ren.

"Can I get your opinion on the new equipment from a pilot’s perspective?"

Ren’s expression sharpened as he faced the new equipment.

"It’s difficult to evaluate the new equipment since I haven’t used it yet, but I feel that the equipment operated simultaneously with it will be crucial."

Dr. Smith agreed with Ren’s opinion.

"We can’t prepare weapons for one arm and shoulder, so the usable weapons will inevitably decrease."

"Yes. In that case, I’d like a weapon like a Gatling gun for the other side."

As the two began discussing various ideas, Allison interrupted.

She didn’t want to be burdened with their expectations.

"I’m sorry to say this, but there’s no Gatling gun among the equipment that arrived this time. I’ve made a request, but I received a response that it will take some time to prepare."

Upon hearing that, Ren pondered for a moment before responding.

"...May I check the currently available weapons?"

"The main new equipment that arrived is a light machine gun for humanoid weapons."

"Is that all? What about the small missile container?"

"That will also take time."

Due to the lavish spending during the first deployment, Thunderbolt had limited weaponry.

It was a necessary measure to increase the chances of Thunderbolt returning safely.

However, Allison couldn’t help but think that they should have kept at least some weapons in reserve.

Dr. Smith was smiling cheerfully.

"Most of the weapons for humanoid weapons are discontinued. Honestly, just having the new equipment prepared is a blessing."

Ren turned his gaze back to the new equipment.

"Anything beyond this would be greedy."

Dr. Smith adjusted his round glasses with his index finger.

"It seems they hurriedly prepared the optimal weapons based on Ichinose-kun’s fighting style, so there are bound to be rough edges. However, with this, defeating a first-class target is possible."

Agreeing with Dr. Smith’s optimistic outlook, Allison added,

"That’s what we need. The real issue is whether the adjustments can be completed in time. Class Three will soon enter the standby period. If that happens, the possibility of deployment will arise."

Dr. Smith tilted his head.

It seemed he had found a mistake in Allison’s understanding.

"There’s still a possibility during standby, right? Let’s hurry up and finish the adjustments. If we do, we might get a chance to fight a first-class target soon. We don’t want to miss an important opportunity."

Seeing Dr. Smith cheerfully participating in the adjustments for the new equipment, Allison cursed inwardly.

(For you, it might be an opportunity, but for the pilot, being forced to face a first-class opponent with weapons that might not even be usable is a different story.)

It wasn’t just a matter of power.

Whether it was effective against Gijuu or not, they couldn’t determine usability until it was proven.

The test pilot, Hifumi Ren, would have to verify that in actual combat.

Feeling sympathy for him in her heart, Allison shook her head to dispel the feeling.

"...Pilots, get ready. Let’s push forward with the adjustments while Dr. Smith is enthusiastic."

"Understood."

After watching Ren salute and hurry towards Thunderbolt, Allison turned her gaze back to the new equipment.

(Now, it looks like it’ll be a challenge to make this usable.)
