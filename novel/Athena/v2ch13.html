Chapter 13: Boots Cat and Gringarm

"I'm not going to cry just because I was forgotten!"

The transport aircraft carrying the Valkyries from the Second and Third Academies returned to the academy in the middle of the night.

Everyone looked utterly exhausted, but fortunately, there had been no casualties.

With dawn approaching, it didn't feel like the right time to celebrate our survival with sweets.

Maya wanted to quickly take a shower and change, but as soon as Kiyone burst out of the transport from the Second Academy, she snapped at her.

"You're so noisy. We're tired over here, so let's do this tomorrow."

It seemed that Kiyone didn't like being treated like a crybaby by Maya.

Despite being extremely tired, she still went out of her way to protest, which was quite something.

(This might be something she'll hold against me. Ugh, what a hassle.)

Thinking that she had made a mistake, Maya started walking away from the transport's landing area.

Kiyone followed closely behind her.

"Don't run away. At this point, let's have a competition. I'll decide who's the best."

"How rude! I helped you out, and even destroyed the gate. I would have liked a word of thanks."

"I had to face a Special Class opponent! What’s a gate compared to that?"

"A Special Class, huh... Were we able to recover the corpse?"

It was clear that Maya was trying to change the subject, but Kiyone seemed interested in it as well.

"I think we could because the recovery team came. I thought I had damaged it quite a bit, but from what I heard, its condition was better than expected."

Since the entire Second Academy had attacked to defeat it, even if the surface wounds were few, the insides were likely a mess.

Maya said, "It's been a while since we faced a Special Class."

Kiyone's mood seemed to brighten at those words.

"Yes, thanks to that, I received praise from the academy head over the communication."

Seeing Kiyone's happy expression, Maya sensed that it wasn't just a simple compliment.

"Were there any other comments?"

Kiyone seemed to have been waiting for that question.

"I was told it was a good decision not to take away my Special Equipment. They said they would continue to entrust me with it, so I should do my best as an ace candidate."

Kiyone had nearly been removed from the ace candidate list at one point, but it seemed her achievements this time had been significant.

Maya looked enviously at her.

"That's nice. I want to take down a Special Class and earn some points too."

Kiyone grimaced at Maya's desire for more Special Class kills.

"Are you out of your mind? Even aces usually don't want to encounter Special Classes if they can avoid it."

Maya said triumphantly to Kiyone, who was reluctant.

"That's where you and I differ. I won't run away just because it's a Special Class. Understand that difference, ace 'candidate'?"

"You looked down on me again! And the talk about crying isn't over yet!"

"...You're so persistent."

In the hangar where the Prometheus Project was being advanced, development staff were swarming around the returned Thunderbolt, conducting detailed checks.

"...The inside of the railgun is melting?"

"This is already unusable."

"Moreover, an abnormal surge of magical power was confirmed during combat. What could this be?"

The development staff were investigating various things—this was just the beginning for them.

They had been woken up in the middle of the night, and it seemed they would be busy from here on out.

On my end, I was undergoing a simple check from Dr. Allison.

"Hallucinations and auditory hallucinations during combat... It's not surprising given the circumstances, but this is quite sudden."

Dr. Allison's gaze shifted to Dr. Smith, who was excitedly examining my condition.

Dr. Smith was carelessly touching my upper body, which was bare.

Especially the places where I had exchanged limbs with the Gijuu.

"The rapid increase in magical power should be investigated regarding its effect on the Valkyries' limiter release state. Still, it's impressive that the Valkyries' power could cause the instruments to malfunction temporarily. I definitely want to study this further, but..."

Dr. Allison continued.

"That's impossible. That academy head won't lend us the female students. Also, please don't investigate without permission. You'll be kicked out of here."

I had to warn Dr. Smith, as he might attempt something reckless.

Dr. Smith looked disappointed at what I said.

"I understand. Right now, the Prometheus Project is the top priority. By the way, I was amazed by the railgun's power. It easily took down a First Class."

Dr. Allison, who was conducting my examination, nodded in agreement while working.

"It was an outstanding achievement. If the railgun itself hadn't become unusable, we could have celebrated without reservation."

"We accomplished the task of defeating a First Class, so let's just be happy about that."

"While we succeeded in resolving it through weaponry, the Thunderbolt still has its flaws. The path to formal adoption remains distant."

While the two of them were discussing various things, my simple examination came to an end.

Dr. Allison spoke about future plans.

"We'll conduct a detailed examination in a few days. I want to borrow the academy's facilities, but I've been told that injured Valkyries take priority."

As I put on my jacket, I replied.

"That's not a problem. I'll explain the situation to the teachers of the Third Academy."

"That would be helpful. The teachers of the Third Academy tend to ask a lot of questions, and I'm not good at dealing with that. I can't shake the feeling that they're probing for something."

I imagined the face of Teacher Dainichi Minami.

If that childish person were involved, she would likely show interest in the Thunderbolt without the secret project in mind.

"I hope it's just curiosity."

"I hope so too, but this place feels like enemy territory. I think it's better to be overly cautious."

To Dr. Allison, the Valkyrie academy seemed like enemy territory for the Prometheus Project.

I had heard that there were people opposed to the project, and as someone who had been attacked, I couldn't deny that feeling.

As I was about to leave, Dr. Smith called out to me.

"Oh, and one more thing, Ichinose-kun."

"Yes?"

"I want you to stay on good terms with the Valkyries—especially the ones with Special Equipment. That way, we'll have a higher chance of collecting data like this again."

It seemed Dr. Smith wanted to increase cases like this to gather data.

However, I had one problem.

"...I'll do my best as much as I can."

"Oh? Your answer is vague today, unlike usual."

"I'm aware that I have issues with my communication skills. Even if I try, there are times when the results don't follow, so I thought it best not to raise expectations too high."

Dr. Smith nodded deeply at my opinion.

"I understand. I'm not good at socializing either."

"Yes. I also struggle with it."

As we nodded at each other, Dr. Allison raised her voice a notch.

"That much is clear."

The next day—or rather, I was unsure if I should call it that.

I had gone to sleep before dawn and woke up around noon.

I was compiling the report related to this battle and was visiting Teacher Dainichi Minami, the homeroom teacher of the Third Academy.

Normally, I wrote reports as part of the Prometheus Project, but now that I was also enrolled in the Third Academy, I needed to write reports for both.

"Is Teacher Dainichi Minami here?"

The staff room used by the teachers of the Third Academy was different from what I had imagined.

In the staff room of the elementary school I attended, there were many teachers, but at the academy, there were staff rooms prepared for each class.

Three large desks were set up, and the furnishings were all high-end.

Waiting for me upon entering was Teacher Kagami.

"I'm surprised. Have you already finished the report?"

"Yes."

I brought the documents in a physical format because it was deemed more appropriate.

The higher-ups wanted to avoid leaving digital records of information related to the Prometheus Project to prevent leaks.

According to Dr. Allison, it was a fleeting resistance.

Teacher Kagami checked the contents and smiled.

"It seems fine. By the way, I'm curious about who Maya chose as her attendant. This is making me a bit hopeful."

"Is there something going on?"

"Maya is not good at this kind of work. Please support her moving forward."

Having been asked by Teacher Kagami, I saluted and replied.

"I will assist her within the bounds of confidentiality."

"Good, that's fine. By the way, how was the first battle with the Boots Cat?"

Teacher Kagami asked for my impressions, but I didn't sense any ulterior motives behind the question.

It seemed like mere curiosity.

"...I was painfully aware of my own inadequacy."

I stated the facts.

However, Teacher Kagami did not seem convinced.

"You defeated multiple First Classes, right? The Prometheus Project should be highly regarded."

"Indeed, I achieved the defeat of a First Class, but I haven't reached a fundamental solution. I believe this was only possible thanks to the cooperation of Lieutenant Hayase and the Boots Cat team."

I couldn't say that the same result could have been achieved with just the Thunderbolt alone.

After hearing my impressions, Teacher Kagami pondered for a moment before saying.

"...You should be a bit more conceited; it would make you more endearing."

"I'll do my best."

After my response, Teacher Kagami widened her eyes and then smiled.

"You really are interesting. You're excellent as a soldier, but you seem to lack in the human aspect... We might be similar in that regard."

"Similar, in what way?"

That Teacher Kagami and I were similar? I tried to find commonalities but couldn't think of any, so I asked, but she seemed unwilling to answer.

Teacher Kagami shook her head with a wry smile.

"Forget what I just said. More importantly, take care of your body today. There's been some conflict regarding the upcoming shifts, so we don't know how things will go from tomorrow."

"Conflict?"

"Between the First and Fourth Academies. The Third Academy has been supported until now, but that means we were placing a burden on others."

Since the Second and Third Academies had been deployed and were exhausted, the next standby group would be the Fourth Academy.

However, since this was sudden, it seemed there were complaints from both the Fourth Academy and the First Academy, which would take over afterward.

Would I have to wait several days to be deployed again?

It was indeed wise to rest my body.

"I understand."

As I was about to leave, Teacher Kagami called out to me.

"HifumiRen... Please take care of Maya. She's easily misunderstood."

Lieutenant Hayase is easily misunderstood? If I had heard that a little while ago, I might have doubted it, but for some reason, I could believe it now.

"I understand."

On my way back from the staff room.

"Well then, see you tomorrow, Teacher."

Just at that moment, Major Usami came out of the Second Academy's staff room.

I saluted.

"Major Usami, thank you for your hard work."

"Oh? It's Ichinose-kun. I heard you had a big success, huh? I'm glad to see you doing well."

Major Usami gestured for me to follow her as we walked down the hallway together.

"You were amazing during your infantry days, but you're also doing great here."

The topic turned to my infantry days—specifically, the time I rescued Major Usami.

"I thought you might have held a grudge against me back then."

"Why's that?"

"Well... back when I was an infantryman, there was a rumor that Valkyries felt insulted when they were rescued because they had high pride."

In reality, Valkyries had treated infantrymen coldly.

I learned the reason when I came to the academy, but the possibility that some Valkyries might feel humiliated by being rescued by infantrymen couldn't be dismissed.

In fact, Major Usami's attitude back then seemed to indicate that she disliked us.

Major Usami scratched her cheek, looking troubled.

"I was just acting, you know. I guess that means my acting was that good?"

"Wasn't it different?"

Major Usami shared her feelings from back then with me.

She looked nostalgic, but her expression turned slightly grim as she recalled that time.

"I was fine until I was shot down and landed safely, but the Valkyrie Dress broke and wouldn't move. I felt really lonely waiting for help to arrive."

Being left alone in a place swarming with Gijuu, it must have been terrifying even for a Valkyrie.

They are strong in their physical forms, but even facing Third Class Gijuu would be difficult.

While one or two might be manageable, the real problem is their numbers.

If spotted, enemies would gather one after another.

Major Usami looked a bit embarrassed.

"Back then, I thought I wasn't afraid of dying in battle. But when I was actually thrown into the battlefield in my flesh, I imagined myself being killed without being able to resist at all, and I trembled. I realized how foolish I had been."

Major Usami, smiling awkwardly, stopped and turned her face toward me.

"I was prepared for the worst when I was found by the Gijuu and thought it was the end. It was then that you all came to my rescue."

"—I see."

Major Usami looked embarrassed.

Her cheeks were flushed.

"Back then, I used to mock the stories of Valkyries falling in love with infantrymen who rescued them. But when I actually found myself in such a situation, my heart was moved. I thought it might be fate. But... I was told to never get too close. I knew that if I got involved carelessly, I would be a burden to the person who helped me."

"I learned about that in the Fifth Academy's class."

"Oh, really? But do you know this? When the academy finds out that a Valkyrie might have developed feelings for someone, they go all out to crush that person. They say that in the next battlefield, they get sent to a war zone."

"...No."

As an ominous atmosphere began to drift, Major Usami laughed, saying it was a joke.

"Sorry, that's just a rumor. But even if there's a slight possibility, it could be a hassle, so I decided to act coldly. Maybe my actions themselves were part of the academy's intentions."

Regardless of the truth, there seemed to be a concerted effort to keep Valkyries away from romantic relationships with the opposite sex.

In such a situation, it was surprising that exceptions like me were allowed.

The head of the Third Academy had said it was part of social learning, but perhaps there was some deeper intention behind it.

Major Usami's expression turned slightly troubled.

"...I didn't really dislike you. I just didn't want to be a burden. I'm sorry if I made you misunderstand."

"No, I apologize for misunderstanding. I was making assumptions without knowing the circumstances."

"That's fine... But I never thought Ichinose-kun would like someone like Kiyone-chan."

Major Usami's words, spoken with a sad smile, left me puzzled.

I didn't dislike Kiyone and personally found her admirable.

However, something felt off.

"Kiyone-chan is indeed a wonderful person. I'm impressed by her superb swordsmanship. I asked her for guidance, but unfortunately, she declined."

"...Huh? Wait a minute."

"Yes?"

As I waited with my hands behind my back, Major Usami looked deep in thought.

She was holding her head.

"Did you say Kiyone-chan is beautiful?"

"Yes. I said Kiyone's swordsmanship is beautiful."

"Weren't you talking about being invited or something?"

"I also use dual daggers on my own, so I asked for her advice on that."

Major Usami's mouth dropped open in shock... and then she sat down on the spot, still holding her head.

"You're not being clear enough, Kiyone-chan. That's why I keep saying we should communicate properly, but she really..."

It seemed there had been some misunderstanding, but at least it appeared the confusion had been cleared up.

Major Usami stood up, embarrassed, and started to play with her hair with her fingertips.

"I'm sorry. I had a lot of misunderstandings."

"It seems the issue has been resolved, which is good. By the way, Major Usami, there's one more thing I want to apologize for."

"What is it?"

Major Usami tilted her head, looking a bit younger than before.

As a fellow squad member, I apologized for Lieutenant Hayase's rudeness.

"I want to apologize for Lieutenant Hayase. While I think her words can be harsh, she is kind at heart. I believe this incident may have upset you, so I wanted to express my apologies—"

"Oh, that matter is fine."

"—Is that so?"

Major Usami smiled sadly, her eyes reflecting a hint of sorrow.

"That was Lieutenant Hayase's way of showing concern, right? It's true that I'm considering retirement, and she probably used strong words to prevent me from overdoing it. I know she's kind, despite her harsh words."

It seemed Lieutenant Hayase's true intentions had reached Major Usami.

I felt relieved.

"Thank you."

"You're welcome. By the way, are you free today? Everyone was tired yesterday, so we skipped it, but how about gathering those who are awake for lunch? I'll treat you to celebrate your achievements, Ichinose-kun."

In terms of rank, Major Usami was far above me, and it was appropriate to respect her.

It was no problem for me to be treated by someone of a higher rank.

However, I couldn't help but wonder if it was right for me to be treated by someone younger than me. That thought crossed my mind, but if I refused, it might embarrass Major Usami.

"...I'll accompany you."

"That's great! Then I'll go call some acquaintances. But, since everyone was so exhausted from yesterday's battle, there might not be anyone awake. If that's the case, it will just be the two of us for lunch!"

Indeed, everyone had looked exhausted from yesterday's battle.

It wouldn't be surprising if they were resting in bed right now.

Major Usami was in a good mood as she sent messages from her device.

The restaurant Major Usami designated was near the academy.

It was the same place where I had been treated to parfaits before, making it a place with sentimental value for me.

Since we entered the restaurant around lunchtime, there weren't many customers.

There were no Valkyries—female students in sight, just a few academy staff members here and there.

We were shown to a table for six, where we began to place our orders.

A tablet device was placed in the center, and each of us entered our orders when we were ready.

As I was about to place my order last, I noticed Major Usami across from me looking slightly displeased.

"Kiyone-chan and Hayase-chan really have a lot of stamina. I never thought they would be awake. They could have rested a bit longer, you know?"

Lieutenant Kiyone, sitting next to Major Usami, seemed pleased to have been praised.

"I have confidence in my stamina."

"Yeah, that's right... Sigh."

Next to Lieutenant Kiyone, Major Usami wore a somewhat complicated expression, which caught my attention.

However, I focused my mind on the tablet device.

Ordering was the priority right now.

Then, as I was about to choose a dessert, Lieutenant Hayase, sitting next to me, grabbed my right wrist.

"I told you it's lunch."

"...I already had a portable meal for lunch, so I thought dessert would be fine."

"I told you that's not allowed, right? If you want something, it has to be after lunch."

"...Understood."

Having experienced a similar exchange before, I tried to say I had already eaten lunch, but it didn't work.

Major Usami, who had been watching our exchange, smiled as she spoke.

"That's surprising. Ichinose-kun likes sweets. In that case, I'll order a mint parfait, and we can share it."

I was momentarily impressed by the idea of sharing, but then I realized the trap of sharing a mint-flavored dessert.

As I hesitated, Lieutenant Hayase, who was sitting next to me, spoke up.

"You hated mint flavor, didn't you?"

Major Usami reacted strongly to Lieutenant Hayase's statement.

"Really?!"

When I silently nodded, Major Usami's expression turned sad.

"But mint flavor is delicious..."

"I appreciate the sentiment, but I really can't handle it..."

I recalled the shy boy who considered mint flavor the pinnacle of desserts.

Whenever he expressed gratitude, it was always with mint-flavored sweets, and I felt it would be rude to dismiss his feelings, so I endured eating them.

Even now, if Lieutenant Hayase hadn't mentioned it, I would have likely endured it again.

At times like this, I envied Lieutenant Hayase's straightforwardness.

Seeing Major Usami's sad face, Lieutenant Kiyone glared at me.

"You're really a guy who can't read the room. Rei, I'll order chocolate flavor, so let's share that."

Lieutenant Kiyone tried to comfort Major Usami, but it seemed to have the opposite effect.

"...Read the room, Kiyone-chan."

"Why not?!"

Lieutenant Kiyone looked utterly surprised, as if she hadn't expected to be blamed.

After finishing our orders on the tablet, it seemed we would chat until the food arrived.

Lieutenant Hayase initiated the conversation.

"By the way, Usami, do you have anyone you're targeting in the Second Academy?"

"Are you talking about scouts? Sorry, but I can't tell you that. If I get pulled into the Third Academy, Yoshizawa-sensei will visibly get upset. It was especially bad when it was Hayase-chan's turn."

Had Lieutenant Hayase also been scouted by other classes?

Indeed, she seemed exceptional even to me.

However, I was unsure how she ranked among the Valkyries.

From Major Usami's words, it seemed she was a talent sought after by multiple classes.

While Major Usami laughed, Lieutenant Kiyone looked displeased.

"I think it was a good decision not to pull her into the Second Academy."

Lieutenant Hayase retorted to Lieutenant Kiyone's statement.

"I don't want to be in the stiff Second Academy either. Besides, isn't the Second Academy too biased? Their combat style leans heavily toward close combat, and there are many who dislike men, starting with Yoshizawa-sensei."

It seemed Lieutenant Hayase had a particular image of the Second Academy, which appeared to be biased.

Major Usami wore a complicated expression.

"This is troublesome. There are parts I can't argue against, so I can't refute it."

When Major Usami gave up on arguing, Lieutenant Kiyone stepped in to support her.

"That's just Hayase's arbitrary image, so don't worry about it."

This prompted Lieutenant Hayase to respond.

"Huh? Kiyone, you're proof of that. You're the daughter of a traditional martial arts dojo and absolutely hate men, right?"

Lieutenant Kiyone, accused of hating men, glanced at me before adopting a demure attitude.

"...I didn't hate them from the start. I worked hard to take over my family's dojo, but my weaker brothers were chosen as successors just because they were boys. In the end, my father didn't recognize my efforts... It's only natural to hate them."

It seemed Lieutenant Kiyone had her reasons for disliking men.

Lieutenant Hayase took an interest in that story.

"Were you planning to take over the dojo?"

"Yes. While it may be unknown nationally, it was well-known locally, and I had a decent number of students."

From Lieutenant Kiyone's proud words, it was clear she had a strong attachment to her family's dojo.

For some reason, Lieutenant Hayase was looking at Major Usami.

Although Major Usami should have noticed her gaze, she ignored it, drinking water.

It seemed she knew something, so I decided to ask.

"Major Usami, do you know something about this?"

"Huh?! Oh, um..."

Seeing Major Usami flustered, Lieutenant Hayase seemed to have gained some certainty.

"This is something you know, isn't it? Usami, since we're at it, spill everything."

Lieutenant Kiyone's eyes widened in surprise.

"Rei? Do you know something?"

Major Usami looked visibly panicked, beginning to sweat.

"Well, when Kiyone-chan became an ace candidate, there was a home visit with Yoshizawa-sensei. It was a remote interview, but, um..."

Major Usami seemed reluctant to answer.

As she hesitated, Lieutenant Hayase grabbed Major Usami's shoulders and shook her.

"Why didn't you tell me?!"

"Well, because..."

Major Usami's gaze was darting around.

As Major Usami hesitated to speak, Lieutenant Hayase, exasperated, began to speculate about what had happened.

"I think this is a misunderstanding on Kiyone's part."

"I have the same feeling."

"Could it be that the whole successor thing was a misunderstanding on Kiyone's part?"

"Perhaps they determined her suitability as a Valkyrie and decided to abandon the idea of her being a successor?"

As we speculated various possibilities, Lieutenant Kiyone, who had been listening, began to argue back.

"My father told me he would leave the dojo to me! Besides, I was told I couldn't take over when I was in elementary school, before my suitability was determined!"

Lieutenant Hayase looked at the silent Major Usami and then questioned Lieutenant Kiyone.

"Did your brothers happen to be strong?"

"That's impossible. My brothers were too busy teaching the children and didn't have enough time to practice themselves. I dedicated all my time outside of school to training at the dojo. I was working hard alone almost every day."

Brothers who were busy teaching children... Lieutenant Kiyone training diligently alone...

As I felt like something was connecting, Lieutenant Hayase reached the answer first.

"Have you ever taught others at the dojo?"

Lieutenant Kiyone looked puzzled by the question.

Why would she ask that? She seemed to not understand the intent behind the question.

"Do you think someone as inexperienced as me could teach others? Besides, if I had time to teach someone, I should be training myself, right?"

Lieutenant Hayase sighed deeply and then looked at me.

"What do you think?"

"I'm not well-versed in dojo management, but considering the position of an instructor, it seems more appropriate for your brothers than for you, Lieutenant Kiyone."

Upon hearing my thoughts, Lieutenant Kiyone scoffed.

"Who would want to learn from someone weak? You really don't understand anything about dojo management."

I had hit a sore spot.

Indeed, I was a novice when it came to dojo management.

However, I could derive answers from my experiences thus far.

Moreover, a former squad gambler had once taught me something.

"That may indeed be a novice's opinion. However, just because someone is a top-tier practitioner doesn't mean they are also a top-tier instructor. Perhaps your father evaluated your brothers' teaching abilities higher than yours, Lieutenant Kiyone?"

"No... but..."

Lieutenant Kiyone seemed to be pondering, her heart wavering.

Lieutenant Hayase glared at her.

"You must have done something else wrong too. You were overly competitive during the inter-class matches, and I bet you were beating up your opponents since you were a child, right? You probably thought it was the weak ones' fault for losing and beat them up."

Lieutenant Kiyone looked down.

It seemed she had some awareness of her past actions.

Perhaps because we had reached an answer, Major Usami finally spoke up.

"Kiyone-chan... According to what I've heard from your parents, the students at the dojo were worried about you taking over. They said they were anxious because you would mercilessly beat up even those older than you. In this day and age, traditional martial arts dojos are struggling to gather students."

Lieutenant Kiyone began to tremble, tears welling up in her eyes.

Finally, the reason Lieutenant Kiyone couldn't take over the dojo became clear.

"In other words, while your individual skills reached a certain level, you were still deemed unsuitable overall. It seems they were concerned about your abilities as an instructor and your character, which is why you couldn't take over. If that's the case, it would be best for you to work on your character while honing your teaching skills moving forward."

I thought this would allow Lieutenant Kiyone to overcome her past and move forward.

It was a piece of advice given with good intentions.

However, Lieutenant Kiyone began to cry.

"...I really hate men. Especially you, I hate you the most."

"What?!"

I was taken aback by her reaction, while Lieutenant Hayase and Major Usami looked at me with exasperated expressions.

"You're really a demon for delivering the final blow."

"Ichinose-kun, you should be a bit gentler. I understand you mean well, but I'm genuinely worried about your future."

It seemed my advice had been misguided.

I thought it was the most accurate advice possible, but it seemed that dealing with people was indeed difficult.
